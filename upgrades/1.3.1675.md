## What Changed

All four evolution stores now handle zero terminal allowance correctly. New soft total
retentionTargets settings preserve legacy max settings and active work. Finished records
are durably preserved in the existing archive before hot-list eviction; archive copies
remain locally readable across peer outages after synchronization. Retention counts report
the actual retained population, including overflow and unknown measurements.

Each machine now keeps a bounded, signed observation of peers' scheduled-job catalogs. The lease holder compares those observations with its own jobs, repairs missing shipped defaults without changing existing jobs, and alerts when coverage is missing or uncertain. Promotion catch-up decisions are durable and default to asking before an overdue job runs.

Machines can exchange explicitly marked user job definitions and declared project files over a signed, encrypted peer connection. The feature begins off by default and its first enabled stage only previews received files. An operator must approve the exact file root on the source before export and again on the recipient before installation. The scheduler checks imported files before each run and alerts on transfer or integrity gaps.

## What to Tell Your User

Finished work and applied lessons remain available in the archive when they leave a work
list. Open work stays visible even when it exceeds the target. I can inspect the archive
and report pressure without cancelling commitments to make a number look smaller.

After a lease move, Instar checks whether the new holder has the jobs it needs and alerts you about gaps. Overdue jobs on a promotion require your review unless a safe explicit policy applies. Git-sync stays disabled by default; job definitions and dependencies that exist only on another machine need manual restoration.

A lease handoff can now expose jobs that exist only on another machine. Private transfer can copy an approved job and its declared files to the new holder once both machines have been explicitly enabled and the source and recipient approvals are given. This does not turn on git-sync or copy Instar configuration, tokens, or secrets.

## Summary of New Capabilities

Authenticated archive pagination and retention reports expose full preserved records,
per-machine pressure and explicit unavailable peers. Old record numbers remain reserved.

- Signed, metadata-only peer catalog observations remain available when a peer goes offline.
- Pool job listings show coverage status, source, age, and reason.
- Missing built-in jobs are restored without enabling git-sync or replacing custom settings.
- Coverage and catch-up alerts retry after delivery failure and remind while unresolved.
- Promotion catch-up decisions survive restart and use live lease authority before launch.

- Signed, recipient-encrypted transfer of individual approved jobs and declared files.
- Quarantine, preview, PIN-gated approval, revocation, and rollback with scheduler admission fencing.
- Durable transfer gaps and run-time file integrity checks.
- Crash-safe pending state that withholds incomplete imports.
