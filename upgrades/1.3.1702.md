## What Changed

A fix attempt's setup takes minutes. A busy machine can miss renewing its own lease and take it back, which raises the ownership number without any change of owner. The attempt's next save was then refused as stale and it stopped, after setup had finished and its safety check had passed. An attempt that is still on its owning machine now moves to the new number and carries on. A machine that is no longer the owner is now stopped by a direct check just before a session would start. The last-tick record now also survives this case, so status stops showing an old result.

## What to Tell Your User

Feedback fix attempts no longer get thrown away when a busy machine briefly renews its own lease during setup.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Fix attempts continue through a same-machine lease re-acquisition during setup.
