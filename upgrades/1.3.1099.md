## What Changed

The Standards Enforcement Coverage audit now reports and gates every Standards Registry family independently, in addition to its existing whole-registry ratio. Each family has a byte-bound review record and its own exact, non-lowering floor. A family edit without matching immutable evidence, a missing or reset family identity, or a family-specific coverage regression now fails CI even when larger families keep the aggregate green.

The foundational **Structure beats Willpower** standard now names the real coverage ratchet that enforces it, moving The Root from 0/1 to 1/1 and raising the measured whole-registry ratio from 0.7073 to 0.7195.

## What to Tell Your User

Standards coverage can no longer hide a weak area inside a healthy global average. Every constitutional family has a visible last-audited fact and its own ratcheting baseline. A weekly review-due signal keeps old audits visible without turning age into an arbitrary expiry wall.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Reports matching per-family measurements in source CI and the packaged runtime auditor.
- Fails on missing/empty/Root-less full checkouts, stale or mutable evidence, identity resets, malformed records, and exact floor regressions.
- Records one family or all families explicitly; new admission is deliberate and old floors cannot be lowered through record mode.
- Re-surfaces 90-day semantic reviews through a nonblocking scheduled issue.
- Keeps the existing aggregate, dangling-reference, false-claim, and parser-scope gates intact.
