## What Changed

- **The "I have your message; my reply is delayed" notice is only sent for a held session reply.** On a machine that does not hold the serving lease, every held outbound Telegram message used to post that notice into its topic, including background output (job results, status lines, restart notices). On the Mac Mini all 231 lease-held messages were background output, so topics nobody had written in got bursts of 5 to 7 notices at a time. A `HeldNoticeGate` now sits in front of both held paths (forward-to-holder and the direct lease hold): the notice is sent only when the held message was written by a session (`producerKind: session`, read from the sealed origin envelope; `listHeldOperations` now returns `producerKind`), and at most once per topic per 6 h. A suppressed notice is audited as `notice-suppressed` in `logs/telegram-origin-held.jsonl` with its reason (`automation` or `already-noticed`). The hold itself, its status row, its retries and its expiry report are unchanged. Direct-hold audit rows now carry `ts`.
- **System topics are one record for the whole agent.** A standby machine's state is read-only, so a standby that had never saved an Updates topic id created a new "📢 Updates" topic on every boot, failed to save the id, and created another on the next boot (one machine had 41). Now only the machine holding the serving lease ever creates 📢 Updates, 🔔 Attention or 🩺 Agent Health. A standby reads the holder's ids from the new `GET /telegram/system-topics` (20 s after boot, then every 30 min) and records them through `StateManager.adoptSystemTopicId`, the one standby write allowed, for those three keys only. The 🩺 Agent Health topic id is now saved (`agent-health-topic`) instead of living only in memory, and the Updates topic is reused by name before one is created. A hub the holder creates lazily is saved too, and a machine promoted to holder after booting as a standby ensures its own topics on the next pass. Adoption carries an anti-thrash brake (`withAdoptionBrake`): a holder record that keeps changing is followed at most 3 times per kind per boot, then held and logged; the convergence model is registered as `system-topic-adoption` in the self-action registry.

- **Housekeeping:** `pnpm-lock.yaml` refreshed for `@sagemindai/threadline-core@0.1.0` (added to `package.json` on 2026-10-20 without the lockfile), which had every PR's `UX assertions (messaging E2E)` job failing at `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile`.
- **Housekeeping:** `DELETE /threadline/pairing/restart-notices/:id` (from the previous release) is now declared in the write-domain registry (cluster-shared, like its sibling invite routes), so the write-domain conformance ratchet is green again.

## What to Tell Your User

You should stop seeing "I have your message; my reply is delayed" in topics you haven't written in. That line now only appears when I was actually replying to you and the reply got held up, and only once per topic. You should also stop getting new empty Updates, Attention and Agent Health topics: only the machine in charge makes them now, and my other machines use the same ones. The duplicates that already exist stay until they are deleted; I can delete the empty ones for you.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- `GET /telegram/system-topics` → `{ topics: { updates, attention, agentHealth }, holdsLease }`.
- `HeldNoticeGate` (session-reply-only, once per topic per 6 h) on both held-reply paths; `notice-suppressed` audit phase.
- `StateManager.adoptSystemTopicId` and `SYSTEM_TOPIC_STATE_KEYS`; standby adoption loop (`src/messaging/SystemTopicAdoption.ts`).

## Evidence

- `tests/unit/held-notice-gate.test.ts`: background, unreadable and legacy authors get no notice; a topic is told once per cooldown; an undelivered notice leaves the topic eligible; the forward path reports `noticeSuppressed`; the real origin store returns `producerKind` from the envelope; the CLAUDE.md row refresh is idempotent.
- `tests/unit/system-topic-single-creator.test.ts`: a standby (or an unreadable lease) creates no hub or lane topic; a standby posts into the adopted lane; the holder creates the lane once and records it, and reuses a recorded id; adoption replaces a stale id, never clears one, survives a failed or malformed fetch, and is a no-op on the holder.
- `tests/integration/telegram-system-topics-route.test.ts`: the route is authenticated and returns the record; a read-only standby adopts it over HTTP while its other state stays read-only.
- `tests/e2e/telegram-notice-and-system-topic-wiring.test.ts`: server.ts passes the gate to both held paths, the hooks to both TelegramAdapter constructions, starts adoption on both boot paths, and never mints a topic in boot ensure* on a standby.
- Live diagnosis, 2026-10-20: Mac Mini `pending-relay.echo.sqlite` — 231 lease-held operations, all `server-automation`; Mama PC `topic-session-registry.json` — 41 "📢 Updates" topics, no saved `agent-updates-topic`.
