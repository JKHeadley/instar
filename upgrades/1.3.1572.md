## What Changed

The Desktop page lists every machine that cannot be opened, with its full reason and the one step that fixes it, below the machine picker. Reasons now name the fix: unlock at the Mac then tap Retry, sign in at the Mac, switch back to the agent's user, or the exact System Settings path for the Screen Recording or Accessibility permission.

## What to Tell Your User

If a machine can't be opened, the Desktop page now says why in plain words and what to do about it, instead of a cut-off line inside the picker.

## Summary of New Capabilities

Readable, actionable not-ready reasons on the phone.
