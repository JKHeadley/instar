## What Changed

Historical undelivered-reply scans now reconcile durable Attention records without posting per-reply notices to old topics or the Attention hub. Original local conversational session send failures retain their existing notice path. Background recovery retries and credential-holder reporting are also record-only. Accepted originals close their record locally at any delay without a legacy status post or a claim that a separate explanation was delivered. Old queued interruption notices are withheld from both local and forwarded replay, with uncertain receipts preserved. Jobs and callbacks without explicit live authority remain record-only. Feedback fb-f348f8e4-15f.

Quiet by Default can now let one scheduled job through while the others stay quiet. A job report delivers when `notifications.selectivity.push.jobs.<slug>` is true and the report's job identity is verified. That identity comes from a session the scheduler started, or from the scheduler itself, and is sealed into the signed message record so recovery and other machines reach the same decision. A slug supplied in message metadata confers nothing. The new route `POST /notifications/jobs/:slug/push` takes the same operator proof as the category route, the choice replicates to the agent's other machines, and `GET /notifications/selectivity` lists each job's state. A report the job addresses to the topic where the operator said yes stays there; everything else from an opted-in job, and everything from a dashboard opt-in, uses the Attention topic. The agent's instructions, the scheduler skill and job creation output now offer the choice. The default stays quiet.

## What to Tell Your User

Old reply-delivery records no longer fill quiet topics with interruption notices. Their delivery status remains available in Attention.

When I create a job that reports to you, I now ask whether you want to see its reports. Say yes and that job's reports reach you, in the conversation where you said it or in the Attention topic, while your other job reports stay on the Notifications page. You can also tick individual jobs on that page.

## Summary of New Capabilities

None. This repairs background notice behavior without changing notification preferences or reply recovery.

Per-job report choice for Quiet by Default: `POST /notifications/jobs/:slug/push` with a dashboard PIN or a recent operator message, per-job state in `GET /notifications/selectivity`, job rows on the dashboard Notifications page, and advice at job creation.
