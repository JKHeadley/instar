## What Changed

- **Pairing buttons on a new agent.** Telegram Approve / Finish / Not them / "It's one of my agents" were offered only when the lifeline ingress guard was in **enforce**. A fleet agent stays in **observe** for its first 24 h to 7 days (the exit rule), which is exactly when its owner pairs it, so every first pairing on a new agent said "Open the Threadline tab on the dashboard" (Justin's PP test, 2026-10-21). `LifelineIngressGuard.tapsProvable()` is new: true in enforce, and in default observe once the lifeline has armed the guard with its secret (armed observe already refuses any tap without the secret). False when bypassed, when observe is held by config (`enforce: false`), when no secret is held, or with no lifeline ingress. `routes.ts` wires pairing's `enforcing` to it. Every tap still needs the lifeline secret, the owner's id, the chat and the one-time token (spec §6.1, r5).

## What to Tell Your User

When you pair a brand-new agent, it now shows Approve and Finish buttons right in Telegram instead of sending you to the dashboard. Your taps are still checked the same way, so only you can approve.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- `LifelineIngressGuard.tapsProvable()`.

## Evidence

- `tests/unit/lifeline-ingress-guard.test.ts`: a new fleet agent is false before the lifeline's proof and true after (and an unproven tap is refused in that state); enforce true; held observe, bypass and a wrong secret false; true across a restart. Mutation-checked.
- `tests/integration/pairing-invite-telegram-routes.test.ts` (real routes): observe with the lifeline proven gives Approve/Decline, a secretless tap gets 401, the owner's proven tap approves; observe never proven keeps the dashboard pointer. Mutation-checked against the old `mode() === 'enforce'` wiring.
- `tests/e2e/threadline/pairing-handshake-production-path.test.ts`: two real agents on a real relay, the inviter with a real guard in armed observe: a Finish button, no "dashboard", an unproven tap refused, then "Connected ✓" on both sides. Mutation-checked.
