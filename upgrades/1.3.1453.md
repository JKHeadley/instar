## What Changed

- `src/core/SubscriptionReloginRuntime.ts`: new read-only `blockers()` — per needs-reauth account on this machine with no repair: `no-open-incident` or the repair's own admission refusal (same `admissionFor` the scan uses).
- `src/server/routes.ts`: `GET /subscription-relogin` returns `blockers`; `?scope=pool` merges every machine's rows (machine id + nickname).
- `dashboard/subscriptions.js`: the Subscriptions grid shows the reason in one plain sentence under a needs-sign-in cell's Sign in button, and says when a machine is not running the repair at all.
- CLAUDE.md template + `migrateClaudeMd()`: awareness bullet ("Why a sign-in repair did not start").

Why: on 2026-10-26 many cells on the Mac Mini and the Windows PC read "Needs sign-in" with no repair and no reason; on the Mini four had no confirmed failure on that machine and one had no browser profile there.

## What to Tell Your User

When an account says "Needs sign-in" and nothing is fixing it, the dashboard now tells you why in one sentence, for example that this machine has no browser signed in to that account. You can still tap Sign in.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- `blockers` on `GET /subscription-relogin` (and the dashboard line) answer "why didn't the repair start?".
