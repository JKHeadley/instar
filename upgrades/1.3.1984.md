## What Changed

Interactive cloud Codex conversations now use the existing Codex account resolver, pass its actual home to launch and resume placement, and record the matching subscription account. Codex placement and reactive selection prefer the earliest fresh valid weekly reset among eligible accounts, then use their existing tie-breaks. Five-hour resets cannot override a different weekly spending deadline. Explicit account homes and local models remain separate from automatic selection.

If an automatically moved saved conversation cannot be placed or fails during startup, the session is reported failed, admission is released, and its saved rollout and pending message are retained instead of silently starting fresh. Account-selection refusals before tmux creation also release admission.

## What to Tell Your User

New Codex conversations now use the account whose available weekly allowance expires first. Previously, ongoing chats could skip the account picker and stay on the computer's default login. Existing busy conversations are not restarted just to change accounts.

## Summary of New Capabilities

None — this repairs existing Codex account placement and conversation continuity.

## Evidence

Unit checks cover the displayed five-account case, weekly versus short-window deadlines, unknown or expired dates, explicit pins, local models, refusal cleanup and failed-resume preservation. HTTP integration and lifecycle checks use real pool/resolver/manager dependencies and prove the selected account and launch home agree. Normal complete Local Verify remains required; no published or live-install result is claimed by these targeted checks.
