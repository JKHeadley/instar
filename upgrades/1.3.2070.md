## What Changed

Local Verify preserves bounded transcripts when unit processes fail without naming a failed test. It keeps those transcripts independently from checkout cleanup and records when output was omitted.

## What to Tell Your User

An unexplained unit test failure leaves more diagnostic detail available after the temporary checkout is removed.

## Summary of New Capabilities

Local Verify keeps up to eight private transcripts per machine for this failure class.

## Evidence

Focused checks cover early output, truncation, private retention, and the existing failure record.
