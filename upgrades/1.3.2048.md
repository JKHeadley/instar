## What Changed

Plan approval requests now name the work in the visible list and approval panel. Previously the list hid that explanation and showed a round number. The button uses an explicit approval label. Existing eligibility, permission recording and start behavior are unchanged.

## What to Tell Your User

Plan approval requests now tell you what work you are approving before you open the details. Approving still gives permission without starting work by itself.

## Summary of New Capabilities

No new capability; clearer presentation of existing plan approval requests.

## Evidence

223 focused checks, one existing production Needs lifecycle test, the build and four browser width/theme previews passed. Model and rendered-page tests cover named and unnamed steps, detail failures, tracking-only projects and the unchanged acknowledgement payload. Full shared verification and release remain pending.
