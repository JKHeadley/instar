## What Changed

On the Conversations page, Accept all waited only 60 seconds for the other machine to confirm each change, and stopped the run after two projects in a row were still waiting. With a busy machine, confirmations can take one to two minutes, so a run that was working stopped and needed Continue again and again. The page now waits up to two and a half minutes for each change and stops only after three still-waiting projects in a row.

## What to Tell Your User

- **Accept all on a busy machine**: "Accept all now gives your other machine more time to confirm each change, so it no longer stops halfway when that machine is slow."

## Summary of New Capabilities

| Capability | How to Use |
|-----------|-----------|
| Accept all tolerates slow confirmations | Automatic |
