## What Changed

Added a default-off, evidence-only security-review preflight. After a normal session starts, it can privately capture complete versioned chunks of the findings ledger, selected threat map, rotation state and dispatched prompt. Capture never changes review admission, prompt, model, required reads or completion. Missing/changing inputs and storage errors leave ordinary review intact.

## What to Tell Your User

This update adds a disabled diagnostic for future work on repeated security-review context. It does not skip reviews, suppress reads or turn on any token-saving change.

## Summary of New Capabilities

Optional private full-input packets for security-review, configured with `jobs.securityReviewPreflight.enabled` and an explicit absolute `sourceRoot`. Both delivery and review remain unproven by these packets.

## Evidence

Focused unit, HTTP and production-shaped restart tests cover complete byte coverage, changed/missing inputs, privacy, bounds and normal model-session behavior. Complete verification is assigned to throughput. No live configuration change or realized savings is claimed. Design and ELI16: docs/specs/security-review-preflight.md and its .eli16.md companion.
