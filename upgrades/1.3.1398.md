## What Changed

- **A machine that owns a conversation but not the serving lease now forwards its replies to the lease holder even while the session pool is dark.** The adapter route pre-empted the forward decision with "pool dark → send direct", and a direct send from a non-holder is always held (`authorize` requires the lease), so every reply from such a machine sat in `lease-not-held` until the lease moved (the Mama PC's ~12 h of silence on 2026-10-21). The decision is now `decideHolderForward()`: a confirmed healthy peer holder → forward at every pool stage; only an unsettled lease keeps the dark-stage direct send (no settle wait on a pool that is not rolled out).
- **The topic-name probe is not sent from a forwarding machine.** `resolveTopicName()` posts a real `"."` to read the topic name; on a non-holder that send was held and later replayed into the topic. The stored or default name is used instead.
- **A held "Session respawned." notice no longer reports the respawn as failed.** `POST /sessions/refresh` answered `respawn_failed` for a respawn that had succeeded, so callers respawned again (duplicate sessions). The notice is best-effort and logged.

## What to Tell Your User

If I run on more than one machine, a machine that is working on your conversation but is not the one allowed to send right now will hand its reply to the one that is, instead of going quiet until the sending role moves. You should also stop seeing a lone "." appear in a topic, and a restart that worked is no longer reported as failed.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- None (a fix to the existing forward-to-holder path).
