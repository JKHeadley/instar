# The phone message box no longer hides under the bottom bar, and leftover tiles are gone

When you open one of your agent's sessions on the dashboard from a phone, there is a box at the bottom where you type a message to that session. In the Instar iPhone app that box was invisible. The app has its own tab bar at the very bottom of the screen, and it tells the page how much room it takes. The dashboard's own bottom bar (Home, Work, Capacity, People, Settings) grew to make room for it, but the message box did not take that extra room into account, so it ended up underneath the bar. There was also an older problem: the box was meant to stay pinned just above the bar as you scroll, but a separate rule that stops the page scrolling sideways quietly switched that pinning off. So the box only appeared where it happened to fall in the page.

This change fixes both. The sideways-scroll rule now uses a gentler setting that still stops sideways scrolling but no longer disables pinning. Everything that has to sit above the bottom bar now uses a single measurement that includes the app's tab bar. It was tested at two phone screen heights, with and without the app's tab bar, scrolled to the top, middle and bottom: the box was visible every time.

The second part is tidying. Several pages were merged into other pages over the last weeks (for example Initiatives and Blockers into Plans, and Tokens into "What thinking costs"). Their old tiles stayed behind with a note saying "Now inside …", which made the menus look cluttered and out of date. Those tiles are removed. Any old link to one of them still opens the page it was merged into, so nothing breaks. The "Send content" tile is also removed, because every session's message box now has its own button for attaching files; its old link opens Sessions. Finally, the "Messages to you" tile no longer talks about held replies, since held replies are being fixed rather than shown.
