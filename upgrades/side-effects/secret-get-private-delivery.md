# Private secret retrieval

A selected leaf can be delivered to a command's stdin with both child output streams suppressed. Built-ins can receive it through an explicitly configured separate inherited pipe on descriptor 3. Terminal, regular-file and merged stdout/stderr requests are refused. The temporary scalar pipe mode emits a fixed deprecation notice. A distinct pipe alone cannot distinguish shell capture from a transcript collector. Aggregate arrays require a named leaf.

The update refreshes the bundled helper and load assessment script, and patches known guidance. Custom callers retain scalar pipe capture during the compatibility window. The fleet inventory and migration precede the later strict cutoff. Synthetic fixtures verify compatibility and containment.
