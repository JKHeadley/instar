# Linking a computer to your phone, explained step by step (ELI16)

## What was wrong

To read and comment on your agent's documents from a computer, you link that computer to your phone once. The computer shows a square code; your phone scans it. The page that shows the code said, in one long sentence, "Open the Instar app on your phone, go to People → Link a computer, scan this code, and check the app shows the same code". It never said what linking was for, the button it named did not exist in the app until build 112, and the steps after scanning (the "Link for 30 days" button and Face ID) were not mentioned at all. Justin found it out of date and hard to follow.

## What changed

The page now starts by saying what linking does: this computer can open your documents, read them and comment, for 30 days, without signing in again. It only sees documents you can see, and you can remove it from your phone at any time under People → Linked computers.

Then it lists five numbered steps, with each button's name shown exactly as the app shows it:

1. Open Instar on your iPhone.
2. Tap "People".
3. Tap "Link a computer".
4. Scan the code on this screen.
5. Check that the code on your phone matches the one here, tap "Link for 30 days", then confirm with Face ID.

If your app does not have "Link a computer" yet, the page says to update the app, or to open any document in the app and tap "Open on a computer" instead.

The scan code is large and centred in its own box, with the matching check code in big letters under it and a gently pulsing "Waiting for your phone…". On a wide screen the steps and the code sit side by side; on a narrow window they stack.

## What did not change

How linking works: the key, the code, the 30-day limit, the Face ID check, and what a linked computer may do are exactly as before. Only the words and the layout changed. Undoing it is a revert.
