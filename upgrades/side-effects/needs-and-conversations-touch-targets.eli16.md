# Easier Buttons In Needs You And Conversations

The dashboard has a few controls that look clickable but are too small to tap reliably on a phone. The Hide button at the top of the Needs you list has a target about 29 pixels wide and 20 pixels tall. The Show more and Show fewer buttons are 36 pixels tall. On the Conversations overview, the Overview and All tabs are 30 pixels tall. These measurements come from the actual served dashboard files rendered with invented content at 390 and 1280 pixels, in both themes. Click testing found no larger invisible area around them.

This repair gives those specific controls a minimum target 44 pixels wide and 44 pixels tall. Their existing actions and wording stay the same. It does not change which requests appear, grant access to private conversations, send messages, or change cached history. The close button also falls below the desktop web minimum height of 24 pixels today; the tabs and Show more already meet that desktop minimum but miss the phone requirement.

The larger controls make the Needs you header a little taller. Its existing scrolling list must still fit and remain usable, and the Conversations tabs must still wrap when space is short. Browser checks compare actual target boundaries, open and close the list, change conversation views and enlarge the relevant text by 25 percent. Existing Needs you and Conversations checks guard the surrounding behavior. Complete repository verification is handled by the normal throughput queue after Chief publishes the exact commit. These local results do not claim the repair is released or verified on a physical phone. A revert removes the stylesheet changes without changing any saved data.
