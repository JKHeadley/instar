# The Work page cards line up on a wide screen

The Work page shows a card for each part of the work: Sessions, Schedule, Promises, Plans, and a few that only appear when they apply, such as Development, Feature maturation and Ideas from other projects. On a phone they stack in one column, which is fine. On a computer screen the first three sat side by side, but every card after that stood alone on its own row, leaving a ragged column down the left with empty space beside it.

The reason was that each of the cards that only sometimes appear was built in its own separate little grid, so it could never share a row with the others. Now those cards are placed inside the same grid as the rest, so on a wide screen they fill rows of three like everything else, and on a phone they still stack in one column. Cards that should be hidden stay hidden exactly as before.
