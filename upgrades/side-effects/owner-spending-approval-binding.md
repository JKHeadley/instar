# Combined spending disclosure binding

This dark codec hashes the ordered, server-rendered money-layer enable disclosure and synthetic grant terms under a distinct spending purpose. The fixture checks that changes to the plan, displayed scope, revisions and owner or device generations change the digest.

- Authority. The helper takes typed material only. It verifies no proof, creates no grant, mounts no route and writes no state. Future callers must validate canonical fields and render them from authoritative sources.
- Risk. A digest is a binding, not authority. Before any live action, the installed app and server must agree on the exact bytes and validate the enrolled owner's signature and current authority.
- Rollback. No persistent configuration, external call or migration.

Independent review concurred for the synthetic checkpoint. Activation requires validated monetary units, canonical list order and a fixed app/server vector.
