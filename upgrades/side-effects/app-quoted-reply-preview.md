# Signed App Reply previews and capability

## Authority and privacy

A signed reader receives a source preview only if the exact held reply base and original are both currently readable and the machine proof binds the relation. A positively failed signed origin cannot supply a preview. Older unsigned originals keep their ordinary held-store authority. Missing or denied original results share the unavailable state without source identities or text. Absent or denied reply bases produce no reply field. Bearer callers, peers and disabled holders receive no preview projection. The signed refresh rechecks only its submitted base event IDs and bounds each request to 50.

Reply availability comes from the live send and feature switches, verified signed actor, current local custody, and send authority. Pooled listings recompute it after the peer response and cannot inherit it from a peer. Nothing in the projection grants access or adds effects to send or Stop.

## Recovery and verification

Disabling quoted Replies removes capability and preview fields and refuses refresh. Tests cover enabled and disabled responses, exact history, batched refresh, source revocation, failed source signatures, peer capability stripping and production routing. The native fixture passed standalone Swift. Native composer integration and the delayed-history ordering test remain pending.

## 6b. Operator-surface quality

The server exposes one optional action and one plain unavailable state. A client can keep the current draft and attachments while selecting or clearing a reply. It receives no proof internals or raw authorization details to show the user. Native rendering and phone usability remain client verification steps.

## Framework generality

The signed and storage paths are shared across clients and independent of agent frameworks or model providers.
