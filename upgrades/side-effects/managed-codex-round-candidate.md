## Decision-point inventory

Reuse round preflight. Require one approved active item, explicit owner, acknowledgment for the precise round and no project or child blockers. Return versioned eligibility facts. The existing dispatcher checks those facts before and after the fixture spawn.

## Under-block

## Level of abstraction and signal versus authority
