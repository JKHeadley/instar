# Plain overview: approve or decline from the dashboard

Some of my promises wait only for your yes or no, for example "I'll run the real test on a
Mac once you say so". Until now the dashboard could only send you to the chat to answer, or
let you mark the reminder as handled, which closed it without telling me anything.

Now such a promise shows two buttons: Approve and Decline. Approve tells the session that
made the promise to go ahead, and the promise becomes mine to finish. Decline closes the
promise and tells the session not to do it. Either way, a short line saying what you decided
is posted in that conversation, so there's a record where the work happens. If you'd rather
talk it through before deciding, "Reply in the conversation" is still there.

The buttons only appear on promises that are waiting for your go-ahead; everything else looks the same as before.
