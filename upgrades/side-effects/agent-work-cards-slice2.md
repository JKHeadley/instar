## Summary of the change

Slice 2 of agent-work-cards-default builds §8 (card owners across the team) and §9 (blocking links between cards). **Owners:** any person in the replicated team record may carry a card, checked at write by the service through a `teamMember` seam (owner / `team` standing / `not-a-team-member` / `team-unavailable`), never `GET /team/people`; the owner or the current carrier passes a card (an `edited.carrier`), the agent passes only a card it carries, only to a person, only as an ask (`recommendation` + `whyYou`), and never sets `carrier: agent` (`agent-cannot-take-card`); a pass to a person who cannot see the card is refused `new-carrier-cannot-see-card` unless the owner makes it; a member's carrier write folds as agent-written for approval and a request approval never covers a card a member passed; the carrier's lane is "Waiting on you" (the owner's "Waiting on others"), `overSoftBudget` is per carrier lane; a member's taps are `done` as the current carrier bound to the material hash and an answer to a question addressed to them (the fold ignores any other member tap, so another machine's record cannot smuggle one); ## 1. Over-block

- **A guest-tier team person cannot carry a card** (`not-a-team-member`): the spec names "owner or `team` standing"; a guest reads but does not act. Intended; the refusal names the person. Reversible by the owner raising their standing.
- **A pass from the agent to a member who cannot yet see the card is refused** even when the owner would plainly want it: the spec's floor ("a pass never widens access except the owner's"). The refusal says to ask the owner to pass it, and the owner's pass is one PIN or phone action.
- **`team-unavailable` (503)** refuses every person-carrier write while the team log exists but cannot be read (an absent log is a team of one, answered `not-a-team-member`, never a standing 503): no card changes hands on a guess (spec §8). It is retryable and says so.
- **A member's pass with any other field on the record is `owner-required`** rather than partially applied: a member edits nothing but `carrier`; the fold drops other fields on a member record from another machine for the same reason.
- **A member's `done` needs the material hash**: a stale tap after a reassignment is `stale-card`. The console and the phone send it already; a hand-written call must include it.
- **A `blocked-by` on a blocker the writer may not read is `not-found`**, as the spec requires (no existence oracle), even though the write is otherwise legitimate: the writer asks the owner to link it.
- **`createAsk` refuses a differing body under the same `askKey`** (`ask-exists`): a session that edits its ask after the fact must use the records route on the ask, not a second `/asks` call.
- **A scoped session reaches `/asks` only on its own card (or the card its recording conversation produced)**: a session that wants to raise an ask on another card has no path; that is the allowlist's rule for every card route.
- **A blocked card is in nobody's "Waiting on you"** even when the viewer is the carrier of a blocked card: the spec says a block is never a waiting status; the card shows "Blocked by: …" in "Waiting on others". No issue identified beyond that choice.

## 2. Under-block

- **The member phone write path is not built** (slice 3, tracked <!-- tracked: wc_65c9f55755696498 -->): a member cannot pass, close or answer from their phone until slice 3 relaxes the owner-only phone gate and the app has the screens. The server rules are in place and tested with a member principal, so nothing a member COULD do today is wider than the spec; the gap is narrower, not wider.
- **`personMayRead` answers `null` on a machine that cannot read the team log**, which the pass floor treats as "cannot see": a legitimate pass from the agent is refused there until the owner makes it or the log is readable. Narrower, named.
- **A `carrier-departed` record is written only on the lease holder**, from `afterTeamRows` and the tick: a member's departure recorded on a standby while the holder is dark is reconciled at the next holder tick (60 s), never lost.
- **The cycle refusal walks the writer's own index** (the spec's rule); two machines can still close a cycle concurrently, which the holder ends at the later link with `reason: cycle` (tested). Between the two writes and the holder's reconciliation both cards read blocked (bounded by the tick).
- **`blocker-reopened` is written once per reopen tap**; an unarchive or reopen the holder never sees (a card read `unknown` here) writes nothing, and the dependent stays workable (nothing re-blocks). Correct by the spec; a blocked card whose blocker is `unknown` stays blocked (tested indirectly by the "absent blocker keeps the card blocked" rule in `reconcileBlockers`).
- **`roleVisible` requires current standing** (owner or member); it does not re-check that a requester still holds a conversation grant, because the requester arm is a card-level rule by spec.
- **Names in lines and briefs** come from the route layer's `displayName` (the team log, else "someone" for a non-owner): a member added to the team but not yet named reads "someone" in "passed to someone by Justin". Presentation only; the record carries the key.

## 3. Level-of-abstraction fit

Each rule sits where its inputs are: team standing in the service (it needs the replicated record and the card), visibility in one read-side function every surface uses (the route adds the device grant; the service adds the role rule), the reverse index and its reconciliation on the lease holder's runtime (the one writer of system records), the fold's member-tap rules in the fold (the authority over records from any machine), and the phone's `pass` on the existing signed action line (`to` enters the digest only when present, so every line an existing app signs is unchanged). The asks operation lives in the service as one method so the route and a future in-process caller share the idempotency. No smarter gate exists for any of these; none should feed one.

## 5. Interactions

- **Visibility before authority (reviewer concern):** a member's `edit`, `addBlockedBy`, `clearBlockedBy` and `tap` answer `404 not-found` on a card they cannot see BEFORE any authority refusal, so a refusal code is never an existence oracle (the route already did this for reads). A question addressed to a member makes the card visible to them (`roleVisible` gains the addressed-open-question arm), so an answer is never asked of someone who cannot see the card; `setWaitingOn` still refuses a principal outside the team.
- **The fold and a member's pass:** a member's `edited.carrier` from any machine counts only when they were the carrier at that position (the same authority the fold holds over a member's done tap), so a replicated record cannot pass a card its writer did not hold.
- **Approval (slice 1 / work-board-asks §4):** a member's `carrier` write folds `lastWriter.carrier = 'agent'`, so a card a member hands to the agent is `needs-approval` under the strict policy (tested); `requestApproved` adds `!memberPassed`. The slice-1 rule that an outward flip and back restores the request approval is kept unchanged (its test still passes); only the member pass is sticky, as the spec words it.
- **Pickups (work-board-asks §6):** a `blocked-by` on a workable card is a `stop` cause (the stop brief names the blocker and, when its carrier left the team, says so); the system `unblocked` is the `work` cause back, `agentCaused` only when the blocker was agent-closed, so blocking and self-closing cannot buy pickups outside the churn budget. `blocker-reopened` and `carrier-departed` never change workability and are skipped by the cause walk.
- **The holder pass:** steps 4 and 5 (blockers, departures) run after the sweep, drain, lift and re-pickup, each to the remaining journal budget; the route also reconciles at a local link, evidence, claim, tap or pass (`afterBlockerWrite`) and `onCommitted` reconciles ONCE per peer batch that carries a link, tap, claim or edit, so a one-machine flow does not wait a tick and a two-machine flow converges on the holder without a scan per record. A system `unblocked` never triggers another reconciliation (its type is not in the trigger set), so no loop.
- **Quiet (slice 1):** `blocked` precedes `quiet` in `deriveStatus`; a blocked card is not workable, so `repickupOneQuiet` never picks it. A card unblocked after a long block has its quiet clock reset by the hand-over (`dispatched` injected/spawned counts as agent activity, slice 1).
- **Not-started (slice 1):** outranks `blocked` (approval first), which is why the tests approve agent cards before linking; an ask's parent that is `not-started` reads `not-started` with its link recorded — correct, the owner's row comes first.
- **Needs-you:** the member's lane and the owner's "Waiting on others" come from `laneFor`; `myBoardReason` is unchanged (`own` for the carrier). No new row kind; `blocked` never enters a waiting lane.
- **Shadowing:** `cardVisible` replaces two ad-hoc checks (`boardJson`'s grant filter and the route's `visible`) with one; no check is bypassed. `performOwnerTap` is kept as an alias of `performCardTap` (the phone wiring and older tests name it).
- **Races:** two machines writing the same `blocked-by` key agree (deterministic per blocker + episode); two holders racing a system `unblocked` share the key; the fold binds an `unblocked` that arrives before its link (pending list resolved after the walk).

## 7. Multi-machine posture (Cross-Machine Coherence)

**replicated**, through the existing `workBoard` replicated store (`work-card-record` journal kind, whole-record HLC merge, fold per card on every machine): `blocked-by`, `unblocked`, `blocker-reopened`, `carrier-departed`, every pass (`edited.carrier`), every member tap. Pass and departure lines are replicated conversation events keyed by the record id. The team record itself is the owner's replicated signed record (`TeamAccessLog`), read on each machine through `teamStanding`.

- **Only the lease holder writes system records** (`unblocked` with a system reason, `blocker-reopened`, `carrier-departed`), each with an idempotent key (`wc:<card>:u:<link>`, `wc:<card>:br:<reopen tap>`, `wc:<card>:cd:<carrier edit>`), so a takeover cannot double-write; a writer's `cleared` shares the link's key, so a link ends once whoever ends it. The reverse index is rebuilt from the replicated records at boot on every machine (`refold` maintains it); reconciliation runs at boot (the first tick), on every tick, on a local write (`afterBlockerWrite`) and on a peer's record (`afterWrite`), so "arrival, transition and boot" are all covered in either arrival order (an `unblocked` folds before or after its link).
- **The fold is per card** (the blocked card's status depends only on its own records); the cross-card facts (a blocker finished, a cycle, a reopen) are turned into the dependent's own records by the holder, never evaluated at fold time — so every machine folds the same status from the same records.
- **Machine-local by design (physical-credential locality):** the owner's phone `pass` is proven on the machine holding the device record and presence key (the existing phone-tap posture); `personMayRead` is answered from the team log on the machine asked and reads `null` where it cannot be read (the pass floor then refuses, never grants).
- **Departed carriers:** `afterTeamRows` fires on every machine that pulls or pushes team rows, but only the holder writes; a standby's observation is reconciled at the holder's next tick.
- **User-facing notices:** none added (no push, Telegram or Slack notice; one-voice gating is not engaged because nothing is sent).
- **Durable state on topic transfer:** all in the replicated store; the card's conversation key is derived from the card id on every machine.
- **URLs:** none generated.
- **Status surfaces:** none of `/status`, `/health`, `/channels` changed. `/capabilities` (the work-board entry's `endpoints` list) names the asks route and the record kinds; its first verdict is the agent's board (the pooled read), as before. `GET /work-board` and the card read gain `blocked`, `blockedBy`, `blockedWords`, `blocks`, `blocksLine`, `blockerHistory`, `blockerReopened`, `carrierDeparted`, `carrierName`, `memberPassed` (pooled board read; `behind` as today).
- **Mixed versions (honest):** the four new kinds make a card read `unknown` on an OLDER build and stop that build's peer-stream applier at the first such record until it upgrades (spec §Multi-machine). The version floor (`AGENT_WORK_CARDS_MIN_VERSION`, 503 `mixed-versions`) is slice 3 (tracked above); during a mixed-version rollout window an older machine shows such cards as unreadable, which is the spec's named consequence and the reason the floor exists.

## 8. Rollback cost

- **Hot-fix release:** revert the release on every machine together (spec §Rollback). Cards carrying one of the four new kinds read `unknown` on the old build until re-upgraded; cards that only changed carrier (an `edited.carrier` to a member) fold on the old build as a person-carried card whose carrier is not the owner — the old route would have refused the write, but the old fold accepts the record (its `vCarrier` is shape-only) and shows the card as `with-person` in the owner's lane. A member's `done` tap folds on the old build as an archive by a non-owner (the old fold did not know members; it counts it), which is the same outcome.
- **Data migration:** none (no new config keys; records are immutable data).
- **Agent state repair:** none.
- **User visibility:** a blocked card reads `unknown` on the old build until re-upgraded; an ask already raised stays an ordinary person-carried card there.

It surfaced one honest scope limit (the member phone write path, slice-3 work with the iPhone screens, tracked above) and two under-blocks that are narrower than the spec, not wider. All three test tiers are green: the new unit file (24), the new integration file on the real AgentServer (5) plus the phone-rig scenario, the new e2e (two boots), and the pinning e2e files for the server wiring. Clear to ship stacked on slice 1 once the second-pass reviewer concurs.
