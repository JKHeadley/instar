# App message removal history projection

The store now offers explicit removal-aware browse and exact candidate methods. A page succeeds only on the immutable creation custodian with a stable journal and index snapshot. The removal must match the custodian, conversation, event and message IDs. Browse excludes matching rows before selecting the page, and a legacy exact identifier cannot retarget after one of its candidates is removed.

An incomplete proof gives the internal caller an unavailable result. It cannot authorize an unfiltered fallback. The helper checks index bindings against the journal on a cold snapshot and checks file and database stability before returning a result. The raw journal, ordinary browse, and existing route, replica and client surfaces keep their current behavior. An internal store reader supports the next visibility increment.

Cold verification is synchronous. Request-path activation requires a bounded or background proof strategy and an observed workload check across large histories and multiple conversations. Rollback removes the unused projection entry points and keeps existing journal data.

## 6b. Operator-surface quality

The future history reader will leave a page slot available for a visible message and refuse a link to a removed occurrence. This checkpoint exposes no control or change in mobile history.

## Framework generality

The projection is an App journal and SQLite read. It uses no model, agent dispatch or third-party provider.
