# Your imported chats in the Instar app (ELI16)

## What was wrong

The first part let your agent bring in your old Claude and ChatGPT chats. But only the agent itself and the dashboard could look at them, not the Instar app on your Mac.

## What changed

The Instar app can now list your imported chats, open one, search them, and run an import (preview first, then apply), using the same signed connection it already uses for everything else. Only your own app can do this: a team member's phone can't, and neither can a browser. The chats still stay on this computer.
