# Work Board approval provenance

The card view derives a history entry from the existing verified creation record and the same eligibility conditions used at creation. A withdrawal leaves that entry visible and removes the current approval. The console’s approval text distinguishes original requests, phone proof and console proof. Unknown proof is displayed without inventing a method.

- Over-block and under-block. No action is blocked or authorized by this display. Older card records without verified request fields gain no historical claim.
- Authority and interactions. The Work Board fold remains authoritative. The view does not add writes, synthesize a tap, or expose source message IDs through the new field.
- External surfaces. Existing visible cards gain a historical approval field. Redacted cards keep their existing redaction projection.
- Multi-machine posture. The field is equally derived from replicated creation records, with no machine-local cache or migration.
- Rollback. Reverting removes presentation only. Stored journal records stay unchanged.

Independent review concurred. The historical field preserves the owner’s request after a withdrawal, with the current approval cleared.
