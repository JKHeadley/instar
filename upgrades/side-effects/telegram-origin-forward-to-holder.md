## Summary of the change

Today a Telegram reply authored on a machine that holds the bot token but NOT the serving lease is refused by `authorize` and parked in an in-memory `destination-not-authorized` hold: silent to the user, lost on restart, and replayed every 15 min only to be refused again while the machine stays standby (the 2026-10-08 incident). §4 makes that machine FORWARD the sealed operation to the lease holder over the existing signed origin mesh (`capabilities` + `submit`), with `executionOwnerMachineId = holder`; the holder now runs the SAME lease check the direct path runs and answers a typed `not-lease-holder`. The reply request runs a bounded lease-settling window (≤15 s) plus ONE forward attempt (≤30 s); any failure becomes a DURABLE hold through the existing `recordOperationState('held')` with a new `hold_reason = lease-not-held` (+ a `holdDetail` carrying what recovery needs), the fixed outage notice goes THROUGH the holder, and ONE `high` attention item `telegram-origin-held:<topic>` is raised (three topics on one machine within an hour collapse to one). Recovery re-forwards the SAME operation (quick ladder at +10 s/+20 s from the 5 s boot tick, then the existing 15-min schedule); an attempt the holder never confirmed is resolved at that holder's `receipt`, never re-sent blind; after a lease move the old record is durably SUPERSEDED and a new one bound to the new holder lands once. Expiry goes through the existing `expireOperation` and is reported once with honest wording. `/health → telegramOrigin.heldForward`, `/telegram/origins/status.held[]` (now a union of the in-memory cache and the durable rows, each with its reason) are the read surfaces. The forward is switchable (`messaging[].config.messageOrigin.forwardToHolder.enabled`, default true, live); the durable held state, its reason and the notice are NOT behind the flag.

A latent bug found by the integration tier and fixed here: the store's recovery reconstruction added `allowedDerivations: []` to an ordinary child, which the original sealed admission omits, so every store-reconstructed replay failed the holder's seal check (`origin-plan-invalid`). The reconstruction now reproduces the sealed shape exactly.

## Decision-point inventory

- `TelegramAdapter.sendToTopic` / `willRelay()` — **modify** — the send decision becomes "no usable token OR (pool enabled AND this machine does not hold the lease)". `willRelay()` is true only for a DEFINITE forward (a healthy peer holds the lease); a still-settling lease keeps the local tone gate.
- `OriginMesh.handleOriginMesh` (holder-side `submit`) — **modify** — adds the lease check (`not-lease-holder`, typed, retryable) AFTER `execution-owner-mismatch` and BEFORE any store write. It executed unchecked before.
- `TelegramOriginService.#holdUnauthorized` — **add** — names WHY `authorize` refused: `lease-not-held` (durable, recoverable, notified) vs `destination-not-authorized` (foreign chat — unchanged, never forwarded).
- `OriginForwardToHolder.forwardReplyToHolder` — **add** — settle → one attempt → durable hold. `recoverForwardedHold` — **add** — receipt-first on outcome-unknown; same-operation re-forward; supersede on holder change. Pure orchestration; every side effect is injected.
- `OriginStoreBackend.supersedeOperation` / `recordForwardedAcceptance` / `listHeldOperations` / `heldForwardAdmissions` — **add** — durable state transitions; `supersede` refuses a row with any child not `queued` or any claimed entry.
- `TelegramOriginRuntime.runForwardLadder` — **add** — the 5 s tick driver, single-flight with `recoverHeld`.
- Attention item raise (`telegram-origin-held:<topic>` / aggregate) — **add** — signal only; hub-only, collapsed.

## 1. Over-block

- **A standby whose lease view is briefly stale** (holder expired but not yet re-elected) is `settling`: the request waits up to 15 s before anything is written. A legitimate local send on the machine that is ABOUT to win the lease is delayed by at most that window, never refused: a window that settles to self sends locally (`OriginForwardSettledLocallyError` → the adapter continues the local path).
- **A standby that cannot name any holder for 15 s** holds durably (`lastAttempt: unsettled`) rather than sending locally. This is the spec's deliberate choice: a dead holder whose lease has not expired must not be bypassed by a bootleg local send. The hold is reported and the ladder/schedule carry it; the first real holder is bound by the supersede path.
- **`willRelay()` returns true for a DEFINITE forward**, which skips the LOCAL tone gate (the holder's full outbound gate still runs on every forwarded operation — `authorizeSendPolicyDispatch` + `runtime.service.admit` + the holder's own policy). If the lease then settles on THIS machine (or the decision flips between the route's read and the send), the adapter refuses to send locally with a typed `OriginForwardSettledLocallyError` when the caller passed `gateSkippedForRelay`; the route catches it, runs the gate, and re-sends. No message is gated zero times (second-pass finding 3).
- **Media, edits and pins from a standby** are not forwarded in v1 (spec §4.1): they take the durable, reported hold. Over-block by design; the spec names them.

## 2. Under-block

- **A holder that accepts a forward but whose Telegram call fails after admit** is the holder's own existing outbox problem (its recovery), not this layer's; the standby's `receipt` RPC will read the holder's state honestly.
- **A transport timeout on `submit` is `outcome-unknown`**: never retried, resolved at the SAME holder's `receipt` on the next step. If the holder stays unreachable for the whole 6 h deadline, the row expires `effect-unknown` and is reported with the "may or may not have been delivered" wording. Exactly one copy may have reached the user; a second is structurally impossible on this path.
- **The in-memory `heldStatus` cache can list a row the store has since resolved** for one refresh interval (≤30 s). The union dedupes on operation id and the durable row wins the `hold_reason`; the dashboard read is observability only.
- **A standby with the forward switched OFF, a pool-dark two-machine agent, or a lease lost between the adapter's decision and `authorize`** takes the DIRECT-path hold: `#holdUnauthorized` records the row `held` with `lease-not-held`. Its sealed owner is this machine, so it cannot be forwarded; `recoverHeld` RE-ADMITS it the moment `holdsLease()` is true again and the ordinary replay delivers it (the claim fence admits `admitted|partial` only — without the re-admit this was a guaranteed 6 h loss, second-pass finding 1). While not the holder it waits (no reserve spent), and it is reported ONCE via `onLeaseHoldObserved` (notice through the holder if one is reachable, else the collapsed item). The kill switch therefore restores today's local wait minus the silence (the spec's point).
- **A refusal that names our operation id** (the holder's `admit` ran, or its outbox/credential refused after it) is custody at the holder: treated as `outcome-unknown`, resolved at that holder's `receipt`, never re-forwarded; a receipt of `admitted`/`held`/`partial` there is `owned-by-holder` (its own recovery delivers) and keeps the row without re-sending (finding 4). Only `no-record` (a new distinct receipt answer) / `not-admitted` / terminal non-delivery frees a re-forward; `origin-receipt-unavailable` is ambiguous (a store outage looks like absence) and keeps waiting.

## 3. Level-of-abstraction fit

Right layer. The forward rides the EXISTING signed origin mesh (`capabilities` + `submit` + `receipt`) and the EXISTING durable operation-state writer, recovery scheduler, expiry path and attention queue; nothing is re-implemented. `submitOriginToHolder` is ONE transport shared by the request-time relay (`relayOriginBot`) and the recovery re-forward, so "what a refusal means" cannot drift between the two. The holder-side lease check is the SAME predicate the direct path uses (`holdsLease` threaded from boot). The quick ladder is scheduling state stored in the row's own `holdDetail`, not a second queue.

## 5. Interactions

- **Shadowing:** the holder-side lease check runs AFTER `execution-owner-mismatch` (kept — the receipt dedupe depends on it) and BEFORE `origin-plan-invalid` / store writes, so a refused submit leaves NO record at the holder. The standby's `#holdUnauthorized` runs only where `destination-not-authorized` ran before; with `holdsLease()` true (single machine, no coordinator) behaviour is byte-identical.
- **Double-fire:** `runForwardLadder` (5 s tick) and `recoverHeld` (sentinel, ~5 min) share the runtime's `recovering` flag (single-flight). Both can select the same `held` row (the ladder never calls `reserveRecoveryAttempt`), but the holder dedupes on operation id (idempotent `admitInternal` + its claim fence), so a double visit costs one refused RPC at most, never a second post. The LOCAL send path (lease came back here) has no holder fence, so it writes a durable `local-sent` marker BEFORE sending and refuses any later send of that row — a failed `resolve` or a crash after the send can only ever leave an honest "may or may not" expiry, never a second copy (finding 2). The notice is sent through the holder and is itself an origin operation (dedup + policy at the holder).
- **Races:** the holder change is ONE store transaction (`supersedeWithAdmission`): refuse unless the old row is still plainly held (every child `queued`, nothing `claimed`), admit the NEW sealed admission, record it `held` with its reason + detail, mark the old row `superseded` linked to the new id. Either both rows move or neither does; a refused supersede admits the new operation NOWHERE and forwards nothing (finding 5). `recordForwardedAcceptance` refuses non-queued children. The in-request `hold` returns the state writer's boolean; a `false` (claimed entry) is audited `hold-not-recorded` and the row stays `admitted` for the ordinary replay (finding 6).
- **Feedback loops:** a held forward raises one deduped item and the fixed notice ONCE at hold time (never on each ladder step). Attempts are bounded by the ladder (2) + the 15-min schedule + the 6 h deadline (modeled in `selfActionRegistry` as `telegram-origin-forward-ladder`, bound 27 per operation, horizon-independent).

## 6. External surfaces

- **Other agents / users:** a user on a standby-owned topic now receives their reply (via the holder) instead of silence; on failure they receive the fixed notice (through the holder, so it carries the normal origin footer), never free text.
- **External systems:** Telegram sees the SAME single `sendMessage`, executed by the holder's token. A new mesh refusal reason (`not-lease-holder`) and two new response fields (`deliveryMachineId`, `forwardedFromMachine`) are ADDITIVE; an older peer ignores them. An older standby that never sends `expectedOwner` is still answered correctly.
- **Persistent state:** three additive nullable columns on `telegram_origin_operations` (`hold_reason`, `hold_detail_json`, `hold_expiry_reported_at`; `ALTER TABLE … ADD COLUMN` guarded by `PRAGMA table_info`), a new terminal operation state `superseded` (archivable/cleanable like `expired`), new child/entry state `superseded`. An older reader treats a held row with an unknown reason as the ordinary hold (additive). Audit file `logs/telegram-origin-held.jsonl` (content-free rows: ids, reasons, phases).
- **Timing:** the reply request is bounded at settle (≤15 s) + one attempt (≤30 s: per-RPC timeout clamped to 15 s so `capabilities`+`submit` fit) + the notice through the holder (three RPCs at 5 s each — `relayOriginBot`'s own `capabilities` plus `submitOriginToHolder`'s `capabilities`+`submit` — ≤15 s): worst case ≈60 s, under the route's 120 s, so a caller never times out before the hold is written and re-runs with a NEW operation id (finding 7).
- **Operator surface (Mobile-Complete):** no new operator ACTION. The read surfaces are the existing dashboard Message-origins tab (held listing now carries `hold_reason`) and the attention hub item. Nothing PIN-gated was added.

## 6b. Operator-surface quality

No operator surface file touched — not applicable (no `dashboard/*` markup changed; the held listing's new fields ride the existing status JSON the tab already renders).

## 7. Multi-machine posture (Cross-Machine Coherence)

**proxied-on-read + machine-local durable state by design.** The held row lives on the machine that authored the reply (it is that machine's outbox; the sealed payload is there), and the pool-wide answer is the existing `?scope=pool` origin status/audit merge plus the attention hub. The forward itself IS the multi-machine mechanism. One-voice: the notice and the item are raised by the holding standby ONCE per hold (collapsed per machine per hour); the holder sends nothing of its own. Topic transfer: a held row is bound to an execution owner, and a lease move is handled by the supersede path (old row terminal + linked, new row bound to the new owner); it does not strand. URLs: none generated. Single machine: `holdsLease()` is true with no coordinator, `holderForwardPolicy` is only wired when a mesh identity exists → strict no-op.

## 8. Rollback cost

- **Kill switch (live, no restart):** `messaging[].config.messageOrigin.forwardToHolder.enabled: false` → no forward; a non-holder reply becomes today's local wait, now DURABLE (`held`, `lease-not-held`), reported once, and RE-ADMITTED + replayed when the lease returns (the pre-PR row was replayed every 15 min and refused while standby; the behaviour is unchanged, the silence is gone). The holder-side lease check stays (it is a safety floor; the direct path already ran it).
- **Code revert:** pure revert + patch release. The three columns are nullable and ignored by older code; `superseded` rows are terminal and archived by the existing retention; `logs/telegram-origin-held.jsonl` is append-only observability. No data migration, no agent state repair. Rows held `lease-not-held` at revert time stay listed in the durable held listing and expire on their normal deadline.

## Conclusion

The review changed two things in the inherited draft: (1) the in-request ladder (3×10 s inside the reply request) was moved OUT of the request to the recovery tick, per the converged §4.2 wording (one attempt in-request, +10 s/+20 s from the tick, then the schedule), so the route can never time out before the hold is written; (2) the holder-change path was changed from "re-bind the same operation id" to the spec's supersede, which keeps the holder-side `execution-owner-mismatch` guard meaningful. The integration tier surfaced a pre-existing reconstruction bug that made every store-replayed forward fail the holder's seal; fixed. The second-pass review then found three blocking defects and three cheap ones (below); all six were fixed before commit: the direct-path hold is re-admitted when the lease returns and reported once; the local re-send is fenced by a durable `local-sent` marker; a skipped gate is re-run through a typed error; holder custody is receipt-first with a distinct `no-record` answer; the supersede is one atomic store transaction that forwards nothing when refused; the hold boolean is honoured and audited; the notice RPCs are shortened. Tests cover each (unit + the integration direct-path/supersede/custody scenarios).

## Class-Closure Declaration (display-only mirror)
