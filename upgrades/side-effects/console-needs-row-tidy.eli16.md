# A tidier home screen and menus

This is a set of small clean-ups found by looking at the dashboard on a phone-sized screen.

On the home screen, each item in "What needs you" said the machine's name twice: once in the title ("Mac Lan: Remote Desktop needs…") and again in the grey line underneath ("Mac Lan · high"). The grey line now only adds what the title does not already say, and it no longer shows the word "high", which is the queue's own bookkeeping rather than anything you need. The card also ended with an empty band and a divider line; empty footers are now hidden.

On the Work page, the Pace card listed each queue with its coloured dot on a line of its own above the text, which looked broken on a phone; the dot now sits beside its text. It also listed queues on machines where nothing is happening at all ("0 a day in, 0 done"); those are left out.

Finally, the menus said "each of these opens in the dashboard, where everything works exactly as before" and "everything is still in the legacy dashboard". Neither is true any more, because most pages now open inside the new console. The first sentence is gone, and the second now simply says the older dashboard is still available, with a link.
