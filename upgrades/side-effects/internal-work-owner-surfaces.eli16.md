# Internal Work Stays With The Agent

Some routine jobs the agent created for itself appeared as conversations for the owner. Opening one could show an empty chat, and the same job could increase Needs you even while its card said the agent was responsible. This made the app ask for attention that the owner did not actually owe.

The repair uses the recorded facts of each card to separate purely internal work from requests involving people. Internal work has no requester, outward action, person waiting, person question or owner-confirmed completion criterion. Such cards stay out of the ordinary work board, projects, conversations, saved pins, search and unread counts. They are still available through a deliberate link to the agent's internal cards. Real requests, spending approvals, questions and completion confirmations remain visible. A future owner-confirmed completion criterion is conservatively treated as person-facing, even before the final claim.

Creating an internal card no longer creates an empty owner chat. When an allowed progress note or actual execution needs history, that history is created and retained. Existing empty chats are kept in storage but excluded from owner discovery. Nothing is deleted. If the board cannot classify the history yet, the list reports that it is unavailable rather than briefly displaying everything. An older computer's unread hint is marked incomplete when it cannot prove the new owner projection.

This repair does not grant permission to start work. The current start policy, approved-device checks and limits still apply. It changes what the owner sees and the wording of the agent's status. The separate policy-approval wiring needs its own change and proof. The candidate is tested locally with focused logic, HTTP, startup and invented-data browser checks; complete verification and publication remain with the normal coordinator. A source preview is not a claim that the installed app has already changed.
