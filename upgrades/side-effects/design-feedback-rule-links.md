## 5. Interactions

## 6. External surfaces

No new endpoint, command, messaging or external call. Results do not echo arbitrary source-header prose or matched non-synthetic email addresses. Filenames and identifiers remain diagnostic inputs, not a guarantee that the document is safe to publish.

## 7. Multi-machine posture

Pure function by design; equal standard/documents produce equal observations. Markdown options are pinned explicitly. No machine-local authority or state can strand on transfer.
