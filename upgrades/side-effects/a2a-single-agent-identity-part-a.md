## Summary of the change

A joined machine with no agent identity now NEVER mints one at either mint site (`createUnifiedTrustSystem` was unguarded — that is how the Studio minted `afd256bc…` on 2026-08-26); it boots in a named `identity-not-provisioned` posture (relay not attempted, `agent-info.json` not rewritten, lease yielded only to a live provisioned sibling), and ADOPTS the identity its siblings publish over two new signed mesh verbs (`agent-identity-observe` / `agent-identity-request`, sealed to the requester's REGISTRY X25519 key under an AAD-bound, domain-separated variant of the sync sealing) under the `reachable-agree` rule pinned by local memory (`threadline/agent-info.json`): one witness with memory, two without. The identity is installed under a pid+start-time lock, the running process reloads it without a restart (`POST /agent-identity/reload`, local effect only), and a `medium` item announces the copy. Replacement of an EXISTING identity is a CLI run by the agent after a human yes (`instar identity adopt --from <sibling>`, `instar identity init --standalone --i-understand-peers-must-repin`), with both files moved aside (never deleted) and restored on refusal. The legacy `threadline/identity.json` becomes a mirror repaired at first load; the listener daemon loads the canonical file first and is SIGTERMed when it is alive under a superseded key. §2 fixes the detector: ONE formula (the routing fingerprint — `fingerprintOf` is changed to it), every 5 min, self contributes disk AND `connectedAs`, offline-by-presence is `unobserved`, one `high` item per sorted-set episode raised AND resolved by the lease holder only, resolution requires re-observing every still-active split member while a sleeping non-member never blocks, `cannot-tell` never pages (10 online-unanswering rounds → one `medium` item after a presence re-pull), and `/threadline/health` carries `identityCoherence`.

## 1. Over-block

- **A single-machine agent with no identity AND a corrupt registry** now refuses to mint (it minted before). It boots loud with the lever named (`instar identity init --standalone --i-understand-peers-must-repin`). Accepted and argued in spec §1.1: a registry file's existence is pairing evidence, and the alternative (minting) is exactly the split.
- **A present-but-invalid or encrypted identity file** is never written over by adoption (`identity-file-invalid` every round). The CLI moves it aside deliberately. Accepted: a silent overwrite of a key file is the irreversible act this spec exists to stop.
- **One reachable witness with no local memory** refuses (`single-witness`) even when that witness is right. Accepted cost stated in §1.3; the operator's yes + `adopt --from` is the path.
- **A split item on a 1-vs-1 / 2-vs-2 fleet proposes NO direction** — it asks which address peers have pinned. Accepted: a count cannot pick; the human is the arbiter.
- **The lease-yield rule** can leave an unprovisioned machine holding the lease when its only sibling is asleep. That is the designed direction (The Agent Is Always Reachable); the alternative holds every reply — including the item asking for the fix — behind `TelegramOriginBoot.authorize`.

## 2. Under-block

- **A fleet whose reachable witnesses are ALL split adopts the split value** (two witnesses, or one plus local memory, all wrong). Stated accepted cost (§1.3) — §2 is loud about that state within 10 min and §1.4 is the fix.
- **A legitimate sibling already holds the private key** (the parent's compatibility bridge). Rotation remains the only revocation and is out of scope (§Out of scope) with its trigger named. This spec adds adoption, operator adoption and the mirror repair on top of that bridge for routing continuity only.
- **An older sibling (`no-handler`)** is unreachable for adoption; the item names "update <nick>". A rolling update is therefore the normal path to a second witness.
- **The detector relies on each sibling's `/provenance` self-report**; out-of-process corroboration (relay registry lookup) is out of scope with its carrier named — a unanimous-but-wrong fleet is reported by the PEER's §3 dark notice on their side.
- **`cannot-tell` for an online peer pages only after 10 consecutive rounds (~50 min)** — a slow peer can hide a split for that long. Bounded, named, and the health read shows it immediately.

## 3. Level-of-abstraction fit

Right layer. The mint guard sits at the two PRIMITIVES (the only places a key is generated for the agent), enforced by a census test so a third site cannot appear unlisted. Adoption rides the existing signed `/mesh/rpc` transport (verifyEnvelope proves a registered ACTIVE sibling; the handler is the real gate), seals with its own HKDF domain (the `AccountCredentialShare` precedent — never `encryptForSync`, which is forbidden for credential-class payloads), and installs through the existing atomic owner-only writer. The detector replaces the one-shot boot task in `AgentServer` with the same `/provenance` read on a cadence, and publishes on the existing `/threadline/health`. The reload lever is the one path that can change the relay client's key (`reconnectRelay` reuses the client built at construction) and is shared by adoption, the CLI and the mirror repair. No new LLM loop; no new attention channel (items ride the existing hub with the existing dedupe).

## 5. Interactions

- **Shadows the parent's boot detector**: the one-shot task in `AgentServer.ts` is REPLACED (same `/provenance` read, same item store), so there is no double-fire. `evaluateDivergence`/`divergenceNotice` remain exported for their unit tests but are no longer called at boot.
- **Relay displacement**: the reload lever on a STANDBY never connects (the bootstrap's standby + daemon predicate is re-run before any connect), so a rebuilt standby cannot displace the awake machine. On the awake machine the old client is disconnected before the new one connects (one connection per identity).
- **Lease**: `isLeaseEligible` is read live inside `acquireIfEligible`; a holder that becomes ineligible releases at the next tick with a signed tombstone (`relinquishAndBroadcast`), so peers stop deferring at once instead of at TTL expiry. The rule itself is the pure `identityLeaseEligible` predicate. The kill switch stops requesting and installing but NOT observing, so the yield still works with adoption switched off. The eligibility function references the adoption loop's latest observe round (a live provisioned sibling by presence AND signed `provisioned`) — a sleeping sibling never makes a lone machine yield.
- **Mirror repair vs listener daemon**: the repair runs under the identity lock at first load; when a daemon is alive under the superseded key the server SIGTERMs it (launchd does not relaunch a clean exit) and re-runs `daemonHandlingRelay`, taking the relay itself; a daemon that is not running is left alone.
- **Attention items**: `agent-identity-missing:*` / `agent-identity-adopted:*` / `agent-identity-split:*` / `agent-identity-cannot-tell:*` go through `IdentityAttentionItems` (`src/core/AgentIdentityItems.ts`). The attention store answers a create for any id it has EVER held with the old row (a resolved one included) and never updates a body, so each logical item is carried by one store row per EPISODE (`<base>`, then `<base>#2`, …), derived from the store on every call (no in-memory state, restart-safe): an open row with the same text is left alone, an open row with different text is closed and replaced (the open item always names the current refusal reason), and a recurrence after a resolve opens a new row. Split and cannot-tell items are RAISED by the lease holder only; each is RESOLVED by the machine that raised it whether or not it still holds the lease (the item lives only in that machine's store, and resolving a local row posts nothing). A restarted detector re-adopts split items still open in its store, with the conservative member set (self + every active sibling).
- **Race**: adoption and the CLI both take `.instar/identity.lock` (pid + process start time; stale after 60 s when the pair no longer matches a live process, 10-min ceiling regardless; broken by rename-to-unique so two breakers cannot both win). `installAdoptedIdentity` re-reads disk under the lock, so an identity installed by the CLI meanwhile is an idempotent no-op or `identity-present`.

## 6. External surfaces

- **Mesh**: two new verbs (`agent-identity-observe`, `agent-identity-request`) in the registered-peer RBAC class; an older sibling answers `no-handler`, which the requester treats as unreachable and names. Responses (grants AND refusals) are signed by the responder's registry-pinned machine key — the first signed mesh responses in the codebase, needed because LAN ropes are plain HTTP and mesh responses are otherwise unsigned.
- **HTTP**: `POST /agent-identity/reload` (Bearer; single-flight, 60 s cooldown → 429; local effect only, carries no key, installs nothing), `GET /agent-identity/audit` (Bearer, read-only, `?scope=pool`, `?limit`), `/threadline/health` gains `identityCoherence` and relay state `not-provisioned`. `/provenance` reads the public key on every request (it used to be captured at boot) so a reload changes the published value without a restart.
- **CLI**: new `instar identity` group; `instar machines remove` prints the still-signs notice.
- **Files**: `.instar/identity.lock`, `logs/agent-identity.jsonl` (key-free by a byte-scan on every row), `identity.json.superseded-<ISO>` / `.invalid-<ISO>` / `threadline/agent-info.json.superseded-<ISO>` / `threadline/identity.json.superseded-<ISO>` backups (renamed, never unlinked — L12).
- **Timing**: adoption observes every 30 s backing off to 5 min after 1 h; the detector runs 90 s after boot then every 5 min with an in-flight guard and 8 s per-peer timeouts (one request per ONLINE active sibling — roughly a tenth of the presence pull's cost).

## 7. Multi-machine posture (Cross-Machine Coherence)

Per spec `## Multi-machine posture`: the agent identity is `unified` (one keypair, replicated by the sealed handover + adoption; verified equal by §2 every 5 min). The `identity-not-provisioned` posture is unified by construction (exists only on the machine lacking the identity). `logs/agent-identity.jsonl`, `.instar/identity.lock`, `threadline/agent-info.json` and the `*.superseded-*` backups are facts about the key copy on THIS disk (`machine-local-justification: physical-credential-locality` in the spec), mitigated by the pool-scope `GET /agent-identity/audit?scope=pool` read (dark-peer tolerant). The verdict is computed on every machine; items are raised by the lease holder only and resolved by their raiser; the health read is per machine. User-facing notices are one-voice by that rule. Nothing strands on a topic transfer (no topic-scoped state). Generated URLs: none.

## 8. Rollback cost

- Adoption: `agentIdentity.adoption.enabled:false` (read live) → a joined identity-less machine stays unprovisioned and loud, still observes its siblings and still yields the lease to a live provisioned one (strictly better than minting); the CLI still works. Reverting §1.1 re-opens silent minting.
- A wrong `identity adopt` is reversed by a second one from the right machine or by restoring the superseded backup; nothing ever deletes an identity file.
- §2: reverting the formula restores the false alarm; nothing else to unwind (items resolve on their own rule).
- No data migration; the one config default is additive and the CLAUDE.md sections are content-sniffed.
