# Side effects of the dormant native consent preview

The signed intake reserves a separate read-only preview path. It requires an active owner phone signature, a purpose-bound sealed channel request, the live Work Board association, the saved preview and an independently resolved source capture for its target. Only the sealed response carries selected project paths, counts and the destination pin.

- Admission requires the current exact card revision and capture. A card edit, re-open or stale capture requires a fresh read with current evidence.
- The resolver must verify independently established ownership, source enrollment and destination scope. Identity concurred with that contract. Production supplies no resolver or preview service.
- The route reuses the signed intake and channel registry. It reads staging and Work Board under the importer exchange lease. The response omits raw capture hashes, legacy confirmation challenges and internal first-claim planning.
- The response is disclosure only. Reading it grants no approval or copy authority. The synthetic pilot continues to use its existing preview.
- The path is machine-local by design because the sealed preview and channel belong to the target machine and boot. The route exposes no new live capability to other machines or users.
- Reverting drops the dormant path and projection without a data migration. No copy operation runs.
