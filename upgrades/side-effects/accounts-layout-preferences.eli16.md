# Choose how Accounts is organized

Accounts starts with separate groups for each provider, such as Claude and Codex. Within each group, accounts with an earlier weekly reset appear first. Accounts without a known weekly reset appear after those with a known reset.

Use Group by to choose Provider, Machine, or None. Use Sort by to choose Soonest weekly reset, Most used, or Name. Most used follows the largest percentage shown for a currently applicable allowance. The dashboard remembers your choices on this browser, and changing the controls rearranges the list you already loaded without fetching it again.

An account that is present on two machines can appear under both machine headings. Opening it from a machine group shows that machine's details. Switch to Provider or None to see the account together with all its machine details and setup options. Grouping does not change account assignments, sign-ins, allowance readings, or the forecast.
