## What Changed

The feature maturation page has a sort choice next to its search box: worst first (default), most
overdue, longest stuck, or A–Z. Its top line now says "Couldn't check <machines>" instead of
claiming those machines didn't answer.

## What to Tell Your User

You can now sort the feature maturation page by what's most overdue, what's been stuck longest, or
by name.

## Summary of New Capabilities

A sort control on the feature maturation board.

## Evidence

Rendered against the live board through the tunnel with the changed files: "Sort: Most overdue"
persists in the address as `?sort=overdue`; unit tests cover all four orders and the fallback.
