## What Changed

The app keeps the newest usable reading for each account limit across machines. Accounts keeps the quota area visible when a machine needs sign-in or no machine has returned a reading.

## What to Tell Your User

Open Accounts for each available limit and its reset time. If the account needs sign-in on a machine, its usage can still appear. If there is no reading yet, Accounts says so and shows when it last checked when that time is available.

## Summary of New Capabilities

The app can keep separate limits reported by different machines, and Accounts can show the last check time when no usable limit is available.
