## What Changed

Feedback readiness calls can now run on a chosen subscription account. A new setting, `feedbackFactory.readiness.account`, pins readiness to one pool account. Readiness then uses exactly that account after the same fresh, local verification feedback triage uses (an active subscription-only Codex login, the same credential before and after a live quota read, quota under 75%), or refuses with a named reason and never falls back to the shared default. Without a pin, readiness follows `feedbackFactory.triage.accountSelectionMode` (`off`, the default, keeps the shared provider exactly as before). `GET /feedback-factory/drain/status` gains `readinessAccount`: the mode, the pinned account id, and which account served the last call with a `verified` flag, or why it was refused. A refusal reserves no spend, defers the waiting groups for 15 minutes and never demotes the readiness authority. The authority's provider, model family, batch and spend bounds are unchanged.

## What to Tell Your User

When your feedback sorting runs out of room on one AI account, I can now point it at a specific account that still has room, and I check that account is signed in and under its limit before every call. If it isn't, sorting waits instead of quietly using a different account, and the status shows exactly why.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- `feedbackFactory.readiness.account`: pin feedback readiness to one verified subscription account, read live.
- `GET /feedback-factory/drain/status` → `readinessAccount` { mode, pinnedAccountId, last }.

## Evidence

New unit tests cover every admission path (off, observe, on, pin, each named refusal, credential change after launch) and the drain loop (served override, refusal with no spend and no demotion, shared). A new integration test drives the production AgentServer over HTTP: a pin to a missing account is refused by name with no model call, then a corrected pin read live serves readiness on exactly that account with `verified: true`. The production-path e2e confirms the field is live and unconfigured readiness stays shared. 69 related unit files (1,071 tests) pass. Full shared verification is pending.
