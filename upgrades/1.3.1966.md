## What Changed

Quota pacing step 3 (docs/specs/quota-pacing-spend-by-account.md), read-only and dev-gated (`usage.ledger.servedAccount`; live on development agents, dark on the fleet). The usage ledger now records, on every request row, which subscription account actually served it. Before, the only record was the session's account tag.

- **Slot and account.** The slot (config home) comes from the folder the transcript was read from. The account in that slot comes from the credential ledger when re-pointing is in use, and only if verified at or before the row (a session that began before the tenant entered the slot reads `slot-changed`). Otherwise it comes from the enrollment home, judged against a new per-slot record of the quota poller's sign-in checks at the row's own time.
- **Codex.** Codex sign-ins are never identity-checked, so Codex rows are labelled unconfirmed.
- **One-off internal calls** are their own `no-transcript` bucket.
- **Basis and tag.** Every row carries its basis, and the tag stays beside it, never overwritten.

Three read routes:
- `GET /usage/accounts`: spend per login, merged across machines by login identity, with unidentified spend kept apart.
- `GET /usage/account-mismatches`: sessions whose tag and served account differ, paged, confirmed vs unconfirmed.
- `GET /usage/accounts/size`: cost per point over closed cycles, from step 1's readings.

A pool read fans out to every machine over its raw rows and names a machine that did not answer or runs an older build. `/usage/coverage` gains the mismatch total. Four nullable columns are added to the raw-row table only, and older rows read `before-attribution`. The rollups, their replication, and `/usage/tree` and `/usage/top` are unchanged.

## What to Tell Your User

I can now tell you which subscription account each piece of work actually ran on, not just which one it was supposed to use, and flag any session where the two disagree. I can also give a first rough measure of how much work one percent of each account's week buys. It's measurement only: nothing about which account gets picked changes.

## Summary of New Capabilities

| Capability | How to Use |
|---|---|
| Spend by the account that actually ran it | Ask which account this week's work came from; I read GET /usage/accounts. |
| Mislabelled sessions | Ask whether any session ran on a different account than intended; I read GET /usage/account-mismatches. |
| Size of a point | Ask how much work 1% of an account's week is; I read GET /usage/accounts/size (a lower bound, or "can't tell yet" with the reason). |

## Evidence

Unit tests:
- resolver: every basis, including a row written while drifted and scanned after recovery, a verification only after the row, and a session older than its tenant
- identity-check log: a day of confirmations, restart, delayed scan, closed by an unavailable check
- ledger columns on Claude, Codex and invocation rows; switch off; migration run twice on an existing database
- mismatch paging with no skipped rows
- cycle segmentation at a credit; every size-of-a-point verdict
- switch history; cross-machine cursor merge

Integration test (real HTTP): a holder merges a login with a live peer and names a down peer and a 404 older-build peer; a standby proxies; `scope=local` answers locally; 503 on every route when off; bad queries refused; `/usage/tree?by=account` still refused.

E2E alive test: a development agent with no config key attributes a Codex rollout under an enrolled home and answers all three routes with 200; switched off, it answers 503.
