## What Changed

The fallback that estimates Claude weekly usage from local conversation files now discovers and parses those files in a worker thread. A bounded worker failure flows through the existing quota collection failure path.

## What to Tell Your User

Instar can answer requests while it estimates Claude usage from a large local conversation history. Official readings still take precedence, and the fallback remains an estimate.

## Summary of New Capabilities

The Claude usage fallback scans transcripts outside the server's main thread.

## Evidence

The Laptop warm-up profile identified line parsing, decoding, and readline as the largest sampled quota-scan costs. Focused checks cover collection and fallback behavior. A synthetic Laptop-scale scan measures the heartbeat while the compiled collector estimates usage.
