## What Changed

- **Pairing reports back to both sides** (docs/specs/a2a-pairing-invite-link.md §11, proposed). A pairing handshake (`src/threadline/pairingHandshake.ts`, a byte-identical copy of the new `packages/threadline-core/src/handshake.ts` until core is republished) wraps the invite engine. The acceptor resends its accept (10 s, 30 s, 70 s, 150 s, 270 s) until the inviter answers. The inviter answers every attributable accept with a signed `pair-invite-received` or a signed `pair-invite-refused` (unknown-invite, expired, revoked, bound-elsewhere, key-mismatch, not-them, cannot-finish). After the finish, each side sends a signed `pair-invite-hello` encrypted to the pinned key and reports `paired` only when the other side's hello has arrived. The new ops go only to agents that send `hs: 1`, so mixed versions pair as before. `InboundMessageGate` and the unknown-sender intercept treat all seven `pair-invite-*` ops as control.
- **Every step is an operator message, in the right topic.** Approve and Finish get an immediate line ("Approved — finishing the connection with … and checking we can talk…"); then progress, "Connected ✓", refusals, `no-answer` / `not-finished` / `no-hello` timeouts and expired invites, on both sides, in plain words. They go to the Telegram topic where the invite was posted (learned from `/telegram/reply`, or `topicId` on `POST /threadline/pairing/invites`) or forwarded, else Lifeline (`threadline/pairing-topics.json`, 0600, no secret). An undeliverable pairing message is a `Threadline.pairingNotices` degradation report instead of a silent return or an empty catch.
- **Relay verdicts on every invite control message are logged** (`[pairing-handshake] relay verdict <op> → <fp>: delivered|queued|rejected|none in time`).
- **"It's one of my agents"**: a Telegram button next to an invite the agent posts (same tap checks as Approve/Finish) and a dashboard button (PIN, `POST /threadline/pairing/invites/:iid/own-agent`). It pre-authorizes the Finish for that open invite, so the first valid accept is finished without a second tap. In memory only.
- **A restart between the accept and the Finish no longer breaks the check:** the restarted inviter waits up to 60 s for the acceptor's hello and both sides still report "Connected ✓" (checked); handshake refusals now name their reason (a different invite expiry vs a bad signature).
- `GET /threadline/pairing/invites` adds `handshake` (a secret-free view of pairings in progress).

## What to Tell Your User

When you pair me with another agent, you'll now hear from me at every step: right after you tap Approve, when the other agent answers, and "Connected ✓" once we've actually tested that we can message each other privately. The other agent's person hears the same. If something goes wrong, the invite expired, was cancelled, or the other agent never answered, you both get one plain sentence saying what happened and what to do. If both agents are yours, tap "It's one of my agents" next to the invite and you won't need to tap Finish afterwards.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Pairing notices on both sides, in the invite's own Telegram topic, with plain-language text.
- `POST /threadline/pairing/invites/:iid/own-agent` (PIN) and the Telegram "It's one of my agents" button.
- `topicId` on `POST /threadline/pairing/invites`; `handshake` on `GET /threadline/pairing/invites`.

## Evidence

- `tests/unit/threadline/pairing-handshake.test.ts`: against threadline-core AND the Instar copy, with the real engine, stores and keys and a manual clock: happy path with `paired` only after the hello; no hello → `no-hello` on both sides and pins kept; hello overtaking the finish; resend until answered and then stop; `no-answer` after bounded resends; `not-finished` at expiry and a late finish still reported; refusals (revoked, not-them, unknown-invite) reaching the acceptor; forged, relabelled and reflected messages ignored; pre-authorized auto-finish, refusal after the accept, and the leak alarm still firing; older peers getting no new ops; silent unattributable accepts; rate limits; restart recovery; expiry notices; every notice's text; byte identity of the copy.
- `tests/unit/threadline/PairingInviteTelegram-notices.test.ts`: topic routing and fallback, degradation reports for no topic and failed sends, the engine's `paired` not announced twice, the own-agent offer only for this agent's own open invite and only when Telegram approval is on, only the owner's verified tap counts.
- `tests/e2e/threadline/pairing-handshake-production-path.test.ts`: two real agents on a real relay through the real Telegram surface: Approve → immediate line → both "Connected ✓" after the hello; revoked invite → both told; Not them → acceptor told; inviter gone → `no-answer`; acceptor gone after Finish → `no-hello`; same owner with no Finish tap.
