## What Changed

In the dashboard's Conversations list, Grouped view now has two sections: "Projects" (groups linked to a project the Chief of Staff tracks, with the project mark and status chip) and "Groups" (plain groups, folder and count). A group with no conversations is never shown, and an empty section has no header. A project group shows the tracked project's own title when there is one, otherwise its own title without a leading "Project:" or "Project –" ("Projector…" is left alone). Same rules as the iPhone app.

Creating an agent now asks which account of the chosen AI to use when one or more are already signed in on the computer. Setup lists them by email, including accounts used by other agents on the same machine, plus "sign in to a different account". The chosen account becomes the new agent's account: shared folders are marked as shared, and a new sign-in gets its own folder. The Mac app answers through the install answers file (`account`), and an app that doesn't answer gets today's behaviour.

The Work page offers Done (with a closeout note) and Not important (with a reason) in place of Archive on cards from a server that records closes, asks for the note before the PIN, shows who closed a card and why, and reopens a closed card by naming its close. Cards are coloured by whose turn it is. Older servers keep the plain Archive.

Cards created from a message now show **From: Original message** in the console. The link opens the existing conversation reader and highlights the exact message. Older history can be searched in further batches, with failed or unavailable reads stated clearly.

If the Needs-you response contains fewer rows than its total count, the strip states how many rows are available and omitted. Expansion counts only received rows and keeps source failures visible.

The Work page uses the shared console name validator for project headings. Missing or ID-only titles display “Project name unavailable”; original keys and conversation destinations remain intact.

The Work page's project rows show the server's latest report, why a project waits on you or stopped, when it was and will be checked, each done item's state in words, and an Ask for an update button. Pause, Mark checked and Finish are named as phone actions (Face ID) and not offered on the dashboard. Older servers show the rows as before.

The opened card shows Why you under the recommendation and an About line (pull requests by number, plans by kind); the Chief of Staff's own cards are tagged. Needs-you rows linked to a card show a link to it beside the row.

The Work board starts Done and Archived folded with their totals visible. Expanding a history lane reveals its cards and paging together. An explicit later Done page opens automatically; refresh preserves a user's expansion choice.

## What to Tell Your User

- **Projects apart**: "In the grouped conversation list, projects now sit in their own Projects section above your other groups."
- **No empty groups**: "Groups with nothing in them no longer show up."
- **Cleaner names**: "Project groups use the project's own name, without a 'Project:' in front."

When you create a new agent, you can now choose which Claude or ChatGPT account it uses, including one you already use elsewhere, or sign in to a different one.

- **Closing cards**: "On the Work page you can close a card as Done with a short note, or as Not important with a reason. I read those reasons before I raise a new card."
- **Colours**: "Light red means a card is waiting on you; light blue means it's moving."

Open a card and tap **Original message** to return to the request that started it. **Keep looking** checks more history when needed. Your card's own conversation stays separate.

A long Needs-you list now tells you when some items are missing from the displayed list. Its expansion button counts only the items it can show.

A project whose name cannot be read now says “Project name unavailable” instead of showing an internal ID. You can still open its conversation.

- **Projects**: "Each project on the Work page now tells you where it stands and why it needs you. Tap Ask for an update and I'll check it now."
- **Phone actions**: "Pausing, checking off items only you can judge, and finishing a project are on your phone, with Face ID."

- **Why you**: "Every card I raise now says why it needs you, right under my recommendation."
- **Chief of Staff cards**: "When a project is waiting on you, the Chief of Staff opens a card for it, marked as its own."
- **Needs you**: "If something in Needs you has a card, there's a link to it next to the row."

Done and Archived now start folded on Work. Open either heading when you want to look back at finished work.
