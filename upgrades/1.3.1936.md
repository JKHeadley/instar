## What Changed

Internal groundwork for the identity and security plan. Each of your agent's machines now keeps a signed owner record and keeps it in step with the others. At first it only records the machines: nothing about how you sign in or what the agent may do changes yet.

## What to Tell Your User

- **Owner record on every machine**: "Nothing changes for you yet. Your agent's computers now share a signed record of who owns the agent, which later steps use for your devices and security level."

## Summary of New Capabilities

| Capability | How to Use |
|-----------|-----------|
| Owner record status | The agent reads it with `GET /owner-record` |
