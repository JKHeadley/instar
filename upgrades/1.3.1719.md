## What Changed

When a sandboxed command in a feedback attempt hits its time limit, its process tree is killed and its clones are deleted, so a silent stall left no evidence. The runner now adds when the command first printed, or that it never did, and a short process listing, taken just before the kill. The timeout itself is unchanged.

## What to Tell Your User

When a feedback fix check gets stuck, I can now see what it was stuck on.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- A timed-out sandboxed check records its first-output time and running processes.
