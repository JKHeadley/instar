## What Changed

Fifth increment of the approved agent-held Google passkeys spec
(`docs/specs/agent-held-google-passkey.md` §3.2 / §3.3). Before a passkey can ever be minted or loaded
on a machine, that machine must hold a GRANT for the account written on that machine by the operator's
PIN or by a signed instruction from a machine the operator has confirmed as trusted. This update adds
that authority layer: the per-machine grant record (with a revoke high-water mark a backup restore can't
roll back), the list of machines this machine trusts to send passkey instructions (no trust on first
use), a replay ledger, the signed `passkey-cell` instruction itself (15-minute expiry, revokes never
expire), and the routes to grant, revoke and manage trusted machines — locally or on a peer.

Nothing mints or loads a passkey yet. Grants are inert until the enrollment increment lands.

## What to Tell Your User

Nothing changes for you in this update. It's the permission layer for passkeys: your agent can now
record, per account and per machine, that you allowed a passkey there — and take it back — but no
passkey is created or used yet.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Per-account, per-machine passkey grants with a revoke that a backup restore cannot undo.
- A trusted-machines list for passkey instructions, confirmed on each machine's own dashboard.
- Signed cross-machine passkey instructions with replay and expiry protection, isolated from the
  existing account-follow-me mandate.
- `GET /passkeys/grants`, `POST /passkeys/{grant,revoke,issuer-add,issuer-remove}` (dashboard PIN),
  `POST /passkeys/cell-action` (the signed receiver). Dark on the fleet.
