## What Changed

The web dashboard requests the new Needs-you response format. Home and the Needs-you strip use the server’s owner count and show verified instructions in place. The list explains when checks could not finish and how many items were left out of the owner list.

## What to Tell Your User

The web dashboard can show your Needs-you list when owner filtering is enabled. You can read supported instructions on the card, and the page tells you when it can’t complete all its checks.

## Summary of New Capabilities

The web dashboard supports the server-filtered Needs-you list and its in-place instructions.
