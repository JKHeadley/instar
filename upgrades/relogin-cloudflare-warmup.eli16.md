# Sign-in repair: getting past a stuck "checking your browser" screen — plain English

Claude's sign-in page sits behind Cloudflare, which sometimes shows a "Just a moment… checking your browser" screen first. Usually it clears by itself. On one of our saved browsers it never cleared while Instar was controlling the browser, even after twelve minutes, while the very same browser opened normally passed in seconds, and afterwards Instar's controlled visits went straight through too. So the repair now does exactly that: if the check hasn't cleared within its waiting time, Instar closes its controlled browser, opens that browser normally on the sign-in link for 45 seconds so the check can pass on its own, closes it again, and continues the repair. It does this at most once per attempt and within the same hard time limit, and nothing is clicked or typed during those 45 seconds.
