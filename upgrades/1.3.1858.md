## What Changed

Conversations → Review suggestions: "Accept all projects" now shows what it is doing. A line under the button names the project being accepted and its step ("Accepting 3 of 21: … Moving conversation 1 of 1…"), and once a step takes more than a few seconds it says what it is waiting for and for how long. A project that cannot finish no longer blocks the others: Accept all moves on and reports how many were accepted and how many are left. If two projects in a row are still waiting on the machines it stops with a plain line. In every case (waiting, refused, unlock expired, page error) the button returns as "Continue accepting projects"; it can no longer stay greyed out without words. A single card's Accept shows the same waiting words.

## What to Tell Your User

- "Accept all now tells you which project it is on and what it is waiting for, right under the button. If some can't finish it carries on with the rest, tells you how many are left, and gives you a Continue button."

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Accept all shows per-project progress and waiting reasons beside the button, continues past a project that cannot finish, and always ends with a plain line and a retry.

## Evidence

- `tests/unit/console-conversations-dom.test.ts` (new: progress line beside the button, continues past a stopped project, shows what a slow change waits for, stops with words after two waits in a row, never stays disabled after a page error).
- `tests/unit/console-conversation-suggestions.test.ts` (new: a slow step reports what it waits for; stops carry a kind).
