## What Changed

Needs You shows the recorded ask on a Chief of Staff card when one is available. Each request that opens another dashboard page labels the link on the row. Permission requests open the matching request, with a shorter message when the Instar app needs approval setup.

## What to Tell Your User

You can see what a project is asking you to do before opening it. Tap Open card or Open details to review the request.

## Summary of New Capabilities

The dashboard shows the recorded ask and makes the next step visible in the Needs You list.
