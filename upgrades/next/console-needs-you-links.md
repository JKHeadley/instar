## What Changed

- Every item in the dashboard's "What needs you" card now links to where you act on it: its own conversation, the Desktop page for that machine, the Accounts page for a sign-in, or otherwise the Attention topic where it was posted. Before, items without a conversation were plain text.

## What to Tell Your User

Everything in "What needs you" is now a link. Tap an item and you land where you can deal with it, for example the remote-desktop setup for that exact machine.

## Evidence

- On a live agent, both items in "What needs you" (remote-desktop permission on two machines) showed as plain text with no link. On the preview, each now links to the Desktop page with that machine selected.

## Summary of New Capabilities

None. This completes the existing "needs you" card.
