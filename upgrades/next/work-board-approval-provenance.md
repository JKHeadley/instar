## What Changed

The Work Board card detail identifies whether a verified owner request, a phone approval or a console approval authorized the card. The decision history retains the original request after a later withdrawal.

## What to Tell Your User

Open a card’s Details to see why the work was approved. You can distinguish a card that came from your original request from a card you approved later.

## Summary of New Capabilities

A Work Board card's details show where its approval came from: a verified owner request, a phone approval or a console approval. The original request stays in the card's decision history after a later withdrawal.

## Evidence

The card view and console tests verify the source wording and retention after withdrawal.
