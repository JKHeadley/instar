## What Changed

Plan approval requests now name the work in the list and the approval panel. If an earlier approval finishes after you have opened another dialog, the newer dialog stays open.

## What to Tell Your User

You can read what a plan approval covers before opening it. Approving gives permission for the displayed step. Starting the work remains a separate action.

## Summary of New Capabilities

This improves the existing Plans screen and adds no new settings.
