## What Changed

The Instar iPhone app can now show paired phones, held messages and insights natively. Two new read-only routes answer a paired owner phone (or the agent's token): `GET /mobile/messages` (whether the agent's replies are held and why: held sends grouped by conversation with the console's fixed reason words and the oldest wait, the expired count, sends Telegram never confirmed, whether message recording is fully set up, and each other machine's held count and groups, read from each online machine's `?scope=local` answer) and `GET /mobile/insights` (each insight page's headline, lines, next steps and numbers from this machine and every online machine). They are trimmed summaries: no operation, origin, topic, chat, message, account or machine ids, no message text, no dashboard deep links, and all shown text is cleaned and scrubbed. Insights are read cached-only: a phone read serves an AI summary only when the console already made one, otherwise the plain summary, and never starts a model call here or on a peer. Messages reads a lighter `deliverySummaryInputs()` on the origin runtime instead of the full status. Only owner phones may read them (an MSP phone gets 403 `owner-phone-required`); they share their own 20-per-minute allowance and each answer is built at most once every 15 seconds; at most 16 peers are read. The raw `/telegram/origins/*`, `/telegram/origin-display` and `/insights` routes stay closed to phones, and the origin routes keep their PIN-unlock gate. `POST /mobile/devices/:id/revoke` now also lets an active owner phone remove another phone held on the same machine (what the console's Phones panel already allows); any other phone may still remove only itself and gets 403 `owner-phone-required` for another phone, and a device request is never forwarded to other machines. The removal record names the phone that asked. A failed read records a `MobilePhonesMessages.read` degradation.

## What to Tell Your User

Once the app update with these screens is on your phone, your agent's page in the app has a "Phones and messages" section. Phones lists the phones paired with that machine and marks the one you're holding; you can remove another phone after a confirmation, and removing the phone you're holding asks you clearly first. Messages to you shows whether any of my replies to you are stuck, why, and for how long, across your machines. Insights shows the plain-language takeaways from the console's pages. Only the owner's phone sees these.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- The owner's phone can list paired phones and remove one, with a stronger warning before removing itself.
- The owner's phone can see held replies grouped by conversation with the reason in words, across machines.
- The owner's phone can read each insight page's headline, lines and numbers, without starting a model call.

## Evidence

Unit tests cover both sides of each rule: grouping of held sends (named conversations apart, unnamed ones by cause, largest first), the reply/automatic headline and its plain-count fallback, reasons only from fixed words, expired and unconfirmed lines only when their counters say so, readiness items cleaned and capped, an unreadable status worded as held forwards only and never as "none held", peers' groups named by machine, a peer that answered "can't tell" listed as such rather than zero, a malformed peer body counted as not answering, group and page caps, the insights state table (off only when every source is known off), source words, severity and trend clamping, no deep link or page id returned, planted credentials scrubbed; the insight engine's cachedOnly read never calls the model. Integration tests go through the real device check: an owner phone is served with no planted id or token; the token is served; `?scope=local` never reads peers; an MSP, demoted, removed-person, revoked, unsigned or tampered request, the notification token and a POST are refused, and the 21st read in a minute gets 429; a real origin runtime wired through createRoutes shows `deliverySummaryInputs()` equals the matching parts of `status()` and the phone's held count matches the PIN-gated route. Revoke tests: an active owner phone removes another phone; a pending or MSP phone cannot; an id held elsewhere gets 404 without forwarding. A production-path test boots the server, pairs two phones, reads both routes, checks the raw routes stay closed, and removes the second phone from the first. A migration test confirms existing agents get the awareness line once.
