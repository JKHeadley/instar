## What Changed
- **A tone suggestion on a held reply now reaches the session that wrote it.** When a reply was forwarded to the machine that sends to Telegram, or queued for a retry, and the outbound check answered with a suggestion, nobody was told: the reply sat held, was reviewed again on every retry, and could be retired without ever being seen (F1 in `docs/specs/native-conversation-base.md`; cause in `docs/specs/reports/ncb-f1-cause.md`). Now the machine holding it ends its own copy in the same step that records the verdict, and the session that wrote the reply gets one line: "Your reply `ID` was not sent. The outbound check has a suggestion (`RULE`): ..." with the deadline and how to answer. Nothing sends it except the session's answer.
- **Answering it.** A reply sent with `--tone-complied RULE --tone-decision-ref REF` (a revised version) or `--tone-ack RULE --tone-reason "why" --tone-decision-ref REF` (as written) replaces the held one: the original is withdrawn in the same transaction before anything is sent, so the user gets exactly one message. A reply without the reference is a separate message. An answer to a reply that already ended sends nothing and the script prints that reply's recorded outcome.
- **A refusal in custody ends the reply.** A credential, a loopback link, a blocking rule or a deterministic floor catch on a held reply now ends it with one line ("the outbound check refused it (`RULE`) ... Rewrite it before sending again.") instead of being retried. A review that produced no verdict (capacity shed, outage, timeout) is retried exactly as before.
- **No more double sends after "not sent".** The machine that sends to Telegram used to answer "not sent" while keeping its own copy queued, so the original could be delivered after the session sent a revision. It now answers "not sent" only after ending its copy; an older machine's answer is treated as "still held".
- **Status.** `GET /telegram/origins/status` → `heldReplies` gains `advisoryHandedBack`, `advisoryLineSent`, `advisoryNoSession`, `advisoryComplied`, `advisoryAcked`, `advisoryAnswerRefused`, `advisoryExpiredUnanswered`, `advisoryUnmatchedReply`, `policyRefusedHandedBack`, `advisoryAnswerDelayCount` and `advisoryAnswerDelaySumSeconds`, read at the 2026-11-25 review.

## What to Tell Your User
If the check I run on my own messages suggests a change to a reply that was waiting to be delivered, I now actually see that suggestion and either fix the reply or send it as written. Before, such a reply could sit unseen and never reach you, and in rare cases you could have received two versions of it. Now you get exactly one.

## Summary of New Capabilities
- A held reply with a tone suggestion is handed back to its session with one line, and waits for its answer until its deadline.
- Answering with the decision reference replaces the held reply exactly once.
- Advisory counters on the origin status and the federated metrics.
