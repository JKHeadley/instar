## What Changed

- Home: "What needs you" rows no longer repeat the machine name or show the priority word, and empty card footers are hidden.
- Work: the Pace card keeps each dot beside its line on a phone and leaves out queues with nothing open, and a stalled pile now reads "nothing came in or got done this week; N still open" instead of "holding steady".
- Promises: the empty "Waiting on you" card no longer repeats the headline.
- Messages: the page leads with "Messages to you" instead of "No messages are held" when nothing is held.
- Menus: removed the outdated "opens in the dashboard, works exactly as before" line; the footer now just links the older dashboard.
