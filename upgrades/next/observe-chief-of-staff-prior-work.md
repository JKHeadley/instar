## What Changed

Development installations can explicitly enable `chiefOfStaff.observePriorWork`. A successful verified-owner delivery of direct poller text into a live local Chief of Staff Telegram session can then trigger a shadow read of the same public Instar pull request inventory as the ordinary pull request lookup. Only the outcome is recorded. The observer has no tool or permission to change delivery or act on a candidate.

## What to Tell Your User

There is an optional development check that measures whether an existing pull request can be found from a Chief of Staff question. It leaves your conversation and assignments alone.

## Summary of New Capabilities

| Capability | How to use |
|---|---|
| Development-only prior-work measurement | Opt in with `chiefOfStaff.observePriorWork: true`. |

## Evidence

Focused observer and Telegram ingress tests exercise authorization, bounded lookup, provider sharing and unchanged delivery. Queued, lifeline, mesh-forwarded, media and spawn paths remain outside this slice. The original user-facing answer has its own later proof requirement.
