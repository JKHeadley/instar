# Search imported conversations on the computer that holds them

## What Changed

The native owner's keyword search can find a person's previously imported Claude and ChatGPT messages on the selected computer. Each result identifies the provider and the holding machine.

## What to Tell Your User

Search for words on the computer where you brought over the history. Imported results open in the Imported reader. In apps supporting the location check, the owner can check a paired computer for imported history without reading its conversations.

## Summary of New Capabilities

Clients can request newest-first local keyword pages on a signed native owner connection. A date cursor lets them continue across ordinary conversations and imported results. Existing relevance-ranked searches retain their current ordering.

## Evidence

The owner-only route tests cover both providers' result shape, machine-local lookup, date paging, client permissions and refusal of imported peer results.
