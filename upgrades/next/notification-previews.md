# Readable Notification Previews

## What Changed

Long quiet notices start with a short preview and a “Read full notice” control. Short notices remain visible in full. The full text uses the same readable names as its preview.

## What to Tell Your User

Notifications are easier to scan on your phone. Open “Read full notice” whenever you want the complete report. Nothing is marked read until you choose that action.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- None. This changes the presentation of existing notices; routes, permissions and reminder state are unchanged.
