# Upgrade Guide — vNEXT

## What Changed

Shared dashboard footer links and several small filters and disclosure buttons now have larger hit areas. The update applies the same target sizes across pages using those components.

## What to Tell Your User

Small dashboard links and buttons are easier to tap on a phone. Footer links and the Maturation source disclosure also have more room on a laptop.

## Summary of New Capabilities

This improves existing controls. There is no new setting.

## Evidence

The sampled phone and laptop dashboard pages were checked with synthetic data and actual browser hit testing.

The audit reproduced the original 66 findings. This patch clears all 63 assigned findings while leaving the three Settings targets to their separate repair. The audit recorded no unmeasured targets or horizontal overflow on the tested pages.
