# Silent Computer Review

## What Changed

Needs you can show one reminder for computers that have been silent for at least seven days. It opens a review at the beginning of Machines, including when the ordinary machine list cannot be read. Confirmed-gone computers remain visible, and missing evidence is explained.

## What to Tell Your User

You can review silent computers in one place. Open Needs you in the Instar app on your phone to retire a computer or confirm it gone. The dashboard lets you dismiss the current reminder.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- One pooled machine-retirement Needs you source and a matching Machines review.
- Fingerprint-bound reminder dismissal through the dependent server route; owner choices stay on the phone.
