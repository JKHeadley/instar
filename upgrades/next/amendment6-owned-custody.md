## What Changed

The constitution article "An Instar Agent Is Always a Multi-Machine Entity" gains Amendment 6, a narrow Pending exception for the Conversations hierarchy. The agent's own machines hold Pending information by default, an outside host is optional, and the person is told one of three states: Recorded here, Copied independently or Shared commitment. It also sets what happens when the only machine holding an item is lost and how an owned replacement host is accepted. It replaces unmerged PR #2796, which relied on an outside witness.
