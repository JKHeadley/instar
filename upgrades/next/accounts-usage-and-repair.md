## What Changed

Accounts now has a usage list and a separate sign-in repair section. The usage list shows accounts with usage data and keeps the existing grouping and sorting controls. Repair status starts with a summary per machine. Open it to see the affected accounts. Machines with every account signed in share one summary. Routine sign-in gaps stay with the agent.

## What to Tell Your User

The Accounts page shows usage and reset times first. A separate section shows which sign-ins the agent is checking or fixing on each machine.

## Summary of New Capabilities

The repair section can identify a machine that answered with no accounts. A machine that could not be checked is reported separately.
