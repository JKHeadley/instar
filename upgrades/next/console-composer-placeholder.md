## What Changed
The dashboard session view's message box hint now reads "Message…" and stays on one line, instead of "Type a message…" wrapping and being cut off on a phone.

## What to Tell Your User
On your phone, the message box at the bottom of an open session no longer shows a half-cut-off hint. It just says "Message…".

## Summary of New Capabilities
None; a display fix.

## Evidence
Checked in the dashboard preview at 390×844 (phone size): the hint shows on one line, the message box stays at the bottom of the screen, and the open session is 358 px wide with nothing running off the side.
