# Cards have owners across your team, and a card can be blocked by another card (slice 2)

<!-- bump: minor -->

## What Changed

Slice 2 of docs/specs/agent-work-cards-default.md (§8 card owners, §9 blocked-by links; stacked on slice 1):

- **Any team member can own a card.** A card's carrier may be the agent, the team owner or any person with team standing in the owner's replicated team record; the service checks the record at write (`not-a-team-member`, or `team-unavailable` while the record cannot be read, so no card changes hands on a guess). A member's card sits in that member's own "Waiting on you" and in the owner's "Waiting on others"; the soft budget is counted per carrier.
- **Passing a card.** The owner or the card's current carrier passes it to another member or to the agent (`POST /work-board/cards/:id/records {kind:"edited", carrier}` with the PIN, or the owner phone's new signed `pass` action with `to`). The agent passes only a card it carries, only to a person, and only with `recommendation` and `whyYou` (the card becomes an ask); it can never set `carrier: "agent"` (`403 agent-cannot-take-card`). A pass to someone who cannot see the card is refused `409 new-carrier-cannot-see-card` unless the owner makes it. Each pass leaves one line in the card's conversation ("Work board: passed to … by …") and sends nothing else. A card a member handed to the agent still needs the owner's Approve under the strict policy, and a request approval never covers a card a member passed.
- **What a member may do on a card.** Close a card they carry as done (bound to the card's material hash, so a tap after a reassignment is `stale-card`), or answer a question addressed to them (`waiting-on` may now name any team member). Approve, confirm, reopen, hold, archive and remove stay the owner's; the fold ignores any other member tap from any machine.
- **Who sees a card.** The owner and the agent see every card; a person sees a card they may read under the owner's grant, or that they carry, or that they asked for (the durable row named them), while they stand on the team. One function (`cardVisible`) serves every surface; a member's view omits `refs` they cannot read.
- **A carrier who leaves the team.** The lease holder writes `carrier-departed` (once per carrier assignment) from the team-record hook and on its tick; the card reads "… is no longer on the team; this card needs a new owner" in the owner's Waiting on you with a Pass control, and the owner's pass ends it by itself. Re-adding the person hands nothing back.
- **The asks operation.** `POST /work-board/cards/:id/asks {askKey, title, need, carrier, recommendation, whyYou, doneWhen}` from the agent or the session doing the work creates the ask card for a person AND the `blocked-by` link on the parent in one idempotent step (the ask's id is derived from the parent and `askKey`; a retry completes the link; a differing body is `409 ask-exists`). Asks count toward the daily card ceiling and are capped per parent by `maxOpenAsksPerCard` (`429 too-many-open-asks`).
- **Blocked cards.** `blocked-by {blockerCardId, note?}` on the blocked card, by a writer with authority on it (the agent for a card it carries or wrote, the card's person carrier, the owner) who may also read the blocker (otherwise `404`); a self-link or a loop is `409 blocker-cycle`; `unblocked {blockedByRecordId}` clears it (reason `cleared` only). A card with an open link reads `blocked` ("Blocked by: …"), is not workable and is in nobody's Waiting on you; the blocker reads "Blocks: …". Past `softOpenBlockers` the response says "a chain this deep is usually a project" and the write stands.
- **The board hands the card back.** When a blocker is finished, archived, closed or removed, the lease holder writes `unblocked` on each dependent (bound to the link, ids and the reason only), which is the pickup transition; the agent's brief says what unblocked the card and quotes the close-out ("Justin closed it as done: …"). A concurrent cycle ends at the later link; reopening a finished blocker re-blocks nothing and is told once (`blocker-reopened`). Blocker titles and notes resolve under each viewer's access — "a card you cannot see" for a person who may not read it.
- **Awareness and index.** One new awareness entry (`agent-work-cards-owners-blockers`) and the capability index name the routes; the scoped-session allowlist admits `/asks`. No new config keys (`softOpenBlockers`, `maxOpenAsksPerCard` were added by slice 1's migration).

## What to Tell Your User

- **Cards can belong to anyone on your team**: "A card can be mine, yours, or any team member's. You or the person holding a card can pass it along; when I need to hand a card to someone I do it as a proper ask that says what I recommend and why it needs them, and I never take a card from a person. Passing a card to someone who can't see it is refused unless you do it, because your pass is what shares the conversation."
- **When my work is blocked, you see exactly what is blocked**: "If I'm working on a card and need something only you can give, I create a card for you with what I need, and my card shows 'Blocked by: …' until you close yours. Yours shows 'Blocks: …'. When you close it, done or 'Not important', my card comes back to me with your words, and I read them before carrying on."
- **A teammate who leaves hands their cards back to you**: "If someone leaves the team, each card they held appears in your Waiting on you saying it needs a new owner, with a Pass control. Adding them back later doesn't hand the cards back."
- **Nothing leaks across cards**: "If a card is blocked by one you aren't allowed to see, you see 'Blocked by a card you cannot see', never its title."

## Summary of New Capabilities

| Capability | How to Use |
|-----------|-----------|
| Raise an ask for a person and block the work card on it, in one step | `POST /work-board/cards/:id/asks { askKey, title, need, carrier, recommendation, whyYou, doneWhen }` (agent, or the session doing the work) |
| Link a blocker / clear a link | `POST /work-board/cards/:id/records { kind: "blocked-by", blockerCardId, note? }` / `{ kind: "unblocked", blockedByRecordId }` |
| Pass a card (owner or current carrier; the agent as an ask) | `POST /work-board/cards/:id/records { kind: "edited", carrier, pin? , recommendation?, whyYou? }`; owner phone: signed `pass` action with `to` |
| See what blocks a card and what it blocks | `GET /work-board/cards/:id` → `blocked`, `blockedBy`, `blockedWords`, `blocks`, `blocksLine`, `blockerHistory`, `blockerReopened` |
| See a departed carrier's card | `GET /work-board` → status `carrier-departed`, `carrierDeparted.control: "pass"` in the owner's Waiting on you |
