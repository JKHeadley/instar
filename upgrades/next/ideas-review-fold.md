# Clearer Ideas Review

## What Changed

The Ideas page puts suggestions that have not been ranked behind one counted “Not ranked” disclosure. Suggestions worth planning remain visible, and every original suggestion and action remains available when the group is opened.

## What to Tell Your User

Ideas is easier to scan. You can see the useful next step without scrolling through unfinished suggestions. Open “Not ranked” whenever you want to examine them.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- None. This changes the layout of existing suggestions; review decisions, permissions and routes stay the same.
