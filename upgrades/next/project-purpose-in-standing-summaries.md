## What Changed

Standing summaries put the project purpose first when the project registry supplies one for the conversation. A proposal shows its source and confirmation status. Confirmed purposes use the current registry revision. The recovery check rebuilds or falls back when the source, wording, or confirmation status changes.

## What to Tell Your User

Your project can keep its purpose at the top of the summary when the conversation starts again or compresses its memory. A proposed purpose shows where it came from and that it still needs confirmation.
