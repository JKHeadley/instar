# Upgrade Guide — vNEXT

## What Changed

The Files help link, folder permissions button and Inside memory lookup button have a minimum height of 44 pixels on narrow screens.

## What to Tell Your User

These three Settings controls are easier to tap on your phone.

## Summary of New Capabilities

The repair adjusts the layout of existing controls.

## Evidence

Sixteen browser cases covered sample content and fallback states at phone and desktop widths in both themes. Each compact target reached 44 pixels along its vertical centerline. Desktop measurements matched the baseline.
