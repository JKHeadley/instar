# Explain Delayed Telegram Replies

## What Changed

Telegram attempt records can retain the actual capacity refusal cause and reserve/consume timing, separate from the existing retry-scheduling reason. Optional IPC explanations preserve old clients, shared authority and rate limits. Outcome evidence is bounded and secret-free, with an unavailable status when optional diagnostic persistence fails.

## Evidence

The allocated targeted checks passed 86 unit, 20 HTTP and 18 lifecycle cases with no failures or skips. The supported startup-only throwaway check passed with actual exit 0 and verified process, listener and home cleanup. Complete final-head verification and a separately approved installed capacity-refusal positive control remain pending. No live deployment or historical root-cause claim is made.

## What to Tell Your User

The system is gaining better evidence about why a Telegram update waits. This does not itself establish that delayed delivery has been fixed. Existing recovery owns the original message; do not send another copy when acceptance is pending or unknown.

## Summary of New Capabilities

Authorized attempt readback can explain capacity refusals without changing send permission or retry behavior.
