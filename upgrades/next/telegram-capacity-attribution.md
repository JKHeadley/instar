# Explain Delayed Telegram Replies

## What Changed

Telegram attempt records can retain the actual capacity refusal cause and reserve/consume timing, separate from the existing retry-scheduling reason. Optional IPC explanations preserve old clients, shared authority and rate limits. Outcome evidence is bounded and secret-free, with an unavailable status when optional diagnostic persistence fails.

## Evidence

The allocated targeted checks passed 86 unit, 20 HTTP and 18 lifecycle cases with no failures or skips. The supported startup-only throwaway check passed with actual exit 0 and verified process, listener and home cleanup. The corrected real-IPC capacity-refusal positive control is now part of normal repository lifecycle collection, with tracked fixtures, a unique invocation receipt and independent cleanup assertions. Its result is pending complete verification of the new exact head; no standalone result is substituted. The explicit Windows skip reflects the existing POSIX socket dependency and is not a pass. No live deployment or historical root-cause claim is made.

## What to Tell Your User

The system is gaining better evidence about why a Telegram update waits. This does not itself establish that delayed delivery has been fixed. Existing recovery owns the original message; do not send another copy when acceptance is pending or unknown.

## Summary of New Capabilities

Authorized attempt readback can explain capacity refusals without changing send permission or retry behavior.

Capacity evidence can also include the owner process's clock reading when it finished deciding whether to reserve or consume a grant. Older owners omit that reading. The one-worker IPC check passed all 12 cases.
