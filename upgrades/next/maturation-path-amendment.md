## What Changed

The Maturation Path article in the constitution now names default-on as the last rung, requires measured thresholds on every rung, and says the review's model decides every move. A feature can stay off for good only through a recorded user-preference decision. The ladder parser now refuses a preference final with no lower rung. A new article, One Constitution, Two Books Aligned, requires every change to the 1.x registry or the 2.0 rule book to name its counterpart in the other.

## What to Tell Your User

Nothing changes for you. The rule for how switched-off features grow up now says plainly that the last step is being on by default, unless it's truly a matter of your preference. The 1.x and 2.0 rule books must now stay in step.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- A feature can be recorded as staying off by user preference, with who decided and why, once it has proven itself on the lower steps.
