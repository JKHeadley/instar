## What Changed

The existing coverage board can open one work item's recorded measurements. It shows baseline/current values, samples, current targets and numeric history, with outcome and quality measurements separate. Gaps and source/version changes stay visible rather than becoming a continuous progress line.

## What to Tell Your User

Open Show measurements to see what changed and which evidence is missing. A numerical change is not automatically an improvement. The view says when a baseline, source or historical evaluation method cannot be checked.

## Summary of New Capabilities

Bounded numeric history and exact current baseline comparisons on the existing phone/desktop board, selected by work item and machine. No new collector, registry or promotion authority.

## Evidence

Focused ledger, identity/adjacency, peer, source and DOM checks plus real application-route activity tests are recorded in the side-effects review. Production deployment and human intelligibility are separate active run checks.
