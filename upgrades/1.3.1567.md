## What Changed

The pairing link now carries the agent's name (`n=`, display-only), and a paired phone may read `GET /topic/list` so the Instar app can sort conversations by most recent message.

## What to Tell Your User

When you pair a phone, the screen now says which agent you're adding, and the app shows your most recent conversations first.

## Summary of New Capabilities

None new; two improvements to the Instar iPhone app's pairing and conversation list.

## Evidence

Unit test for the link with and without a name; integration test that a paired phone can read the recency list.
