## What Changed

PromiseBeacon is now internal follow-through infrastructure by default. It keeps
commitment cadence, session-loss detection, revival, escalation bookkeeping,
and audits, but sends no summaries, close-outs, escalation statuses, or
Attention items unless a deployment explicitly opts back in.

## What to Tell Your User

- **PromiseBeacon is quiet by default**: "Commitment tracking still works behind the scenes, but PromiseBeacon no longer posts automated progress summaries or escalation notices to you."

## Summary of New Capabilities

| Capability | How to Use |
|---|---|
| Retain internal durable follow-through without automated narration | No action required; missing configuration is silent |
| Deliberately restore historical PromiseBeacon output | Set `promiseBeacon.userOutputEnabled: true` at the deployment level |

## Evidence

Two hundred fourteen focused PromiseBeacon, migration, documentation-parity,
integration, and end-to-end tests pass. The build, full lint suite, and the
hand-authored dark-gate line map also pass.
