## What Changed

When a change passed verification before its target branch moved, the person managing verification can now move it to the front for another run. A queued replacement survives a coordinator restart and the older passing result is withheld while its replacement runs.

## What to Tell Your User

If a previously tested change needs another check against the current main branch, I can put it at the front of the verification queue.

## Summary of New Capabilities

The existing verification priority control can request a new run for a changed base.

## Evidence

The verifier unit regressions cover reuse on an unchanged base, priority admission after the base changes, repeated requests and restoration of a queued replacement.
