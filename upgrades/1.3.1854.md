## What Changed

The dashboard (and every dashboard page shown inside the iPhone and Android apps) no longer sits on a grey loading bar when the connection is bad. Status pages (Needs you, the Work board, Development, Promises, Sessions, Machines and Plans) keep the last answer they got in this browser for up to 7 days. When the agent cannot be reached, the page shows that answer at once with one plain line at the top: "Offline. Showing what I had at 1:52 pm." or "Weak connection. Showing what I had at 1:52 pm. Trying again." The dashboard checks the agent again in the background and refreshes the page when it answers. A page that is just slow on a good connection keeps waiting for the real answer, with no line. With no connection at all, reads and sends answer at once instead of waiting 40 seconds.

Conversation text, topic names, secrets and anything read with the PIN are never kept. Saved copies belong to one sign-in and are wiped on sign-out.

Also fixed: opening the dashboard with no connection showed the PIN screen (it now keeps the sign-in), and the Conversations page could sit on "Checking this browser…" forever when the connection dropped (it now says it could not reach the agent, with Retry).

## What to Tell Your User

- **Bad signal**: "If your phone has a weak connection, the dashboard now shows what it last had and tells you how old it is, instead of a loading bar. It refreshes on its own when the connection is back."
