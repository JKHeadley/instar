# Model list review, explained simply

Instar keeps a list of the AI models it uses and refuses any code change once nobody has checked that list for 45 days. That limit was reached today, so every change was being refused. I checked each model on the list by sending it a one-line message; all of them answered. Five newer models answered too, and I wrote them down without switching anything over to them. Nothing about which model does what has changed.

## Why the list has a deadline at all

AI providers retire models without much warning. In September, every background check that used one provider broke at once because the model it named had been switched off, and nothing noticed until things failed. The 45-day limit forces someone to look at the list regularly, so a retired model is caught by a routine check rather than by an outage.

## What "recorded, not switched" means

A newer model being available does not make it the right one for a given job; that is decided by testing it on the work, not by its release date. So the five newer models are now on the list as known and working, and choosing whether any of them should take over a job is a separate change with its own testing.
