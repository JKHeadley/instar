## What Changed

The proactive subscription monitor now protects an untagged interactive session without changing that live session's account binding. When the account currently served by the default login slot is near its quota limit and a fresher same-framework account is locally available, the monitor selects the account with the most remaining quota and routes the intended change through the existing set-default credential exchange.

This capability ships dark and in dry-run. Development agents record the exact account-to-account flip they would make, while performing no credential write. A real flip still requires deliberate promotion and retains the existing credential executor's identity checks, single-mover funnel, cooldown, audit scrubbing, and dark gate.

## What to Tell Your User

The default interactive session can now be rescued before its subscription account fills without restarting it or changing its session binding. The mechanism is initially observation-only, so no account is changed automatically until it is explicitly promoted.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- A dark, dry-run-first default-login rescue chooses the locally usable same-framework account with the most remaining quota.
- Untagged live sessions are structurally excluded from normal proactive session swapping.

## Evidence

- Unit coverage proves dry-run immutability, live slot-level actuation, Q3 session-binding preservation, freshest-account selection, and no-eligible-target hold behavior.
- Integration and end-to-end HTTP coverage prove the monitor route drives the default-slot path while never invoking the session-swap callback.
