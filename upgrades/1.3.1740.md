## What Changed

- **Chief of Staff state is now saved and shared across machines.** Its records (owner check-ins, project bindings, notes, last-check marks) go through the shared record store, whose `multiMachine.stateSync.chiefOfStaff` switch was never set, so every record was refused as `disabled` on every machine and an owner check-in never started it. The switch is now forced on whenever `chiefOfStaff.enabled` resolves on (spec §1.2), the same way other always-shared stores are set.

## What to Tell Your User

If you checked in to the Chief of Staff conversation and it stayed paused, that was a bug: your check-ins were not being saved on any machine. After this update, your next message there switches it on.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Chief of Staff check-ins, notes and bindings are kept and seen from every machine of the agent.

## Evidence

- `tests/unit/chief-of-staff-store-wiring.test.ts` section 4: a renewal is refused (`disabled`) with the old stores map and written to the real journal with the fixed one; server.ts forces the store on in the map the emitter reads.
