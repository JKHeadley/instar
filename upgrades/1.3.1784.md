## What Changed

Local Verify keeps a clock for "the oldest merge with no passing check of main after it" and stops all merging when that passes three hours. The clock was cleared only when a passing check of main was for main's exact current commit. A check takes about 35 minutes; once changes merged more often than that, no check was ever of the current commit, the clock never cleared, and three hours after the last server restart every merge stopped although main had been checked green repeatedly. Now a passing check of an older commit moves the clock forward to the moment that check started, provided the check started on main as it stood then, because every merge before that moment is covered by it. Fix in `src/localVerify/mainHealth.ts` (`oldestUnverifiedAfterGreen`) and `LocalVerifyService.ts`.

## What to Tell Your User

Nothing changes for you. A fault that could silently stop new changes from merging on busy days is fixed.

## Summary of New Capabilities

| Capability | How to Use |
|-----------|------------|
| Merging keeps going at any merge rate as long as main keeps passing its checks | Automatic |

## Evidence

New unit tests in `tests/unit/localVerify/mainHealth.test.ts`: eight back-to-back 35-minute checks with a merge during each stay healthy for 4 h 40 min (fails under the old behaviour); the alarm still fires 3 h after the start of the last check that covered main; a check that started on an already-outdated commit does not move the clock.
