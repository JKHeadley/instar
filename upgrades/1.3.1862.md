## What Changed

Test-only: `tests/unit/DesktopStreamManager.test.ts` "asks a faster video session for pictures more often" now waits until the first stream has stopped scheduling frame timers (no pace timer for 250 ms, capped at 5 s) instead of a fixed 150 ms pause. On loaded CI runners the closed socket's close event arrived late, the first stream kept ticking, and its 67 ms timers were counted in the faster measurement (`expected 67 to be less than or equal to 50`). That failed unit shard 2/4 on PR 3255 three times in a row. No runtime code changes.

Machine care now reads, for each machine, whether it could come back on its own after a restart (on a Mac: no startup password and automatic login; on Linux: no disk password and the agent set to start), and whether it may be offered for self-maintenance at all (never a test agent, a machine marked as not ours, or while someone else is using it). When something is missing, it names the one setting in plain words. Nothing is switched on or restarted by this.

The agent can now ask the owner, once per machine, whether it may keep that machine healthy by restarting it about weekly in the small hours and when it gets stuck. The message explains what that means in plain words, lists what is open on the machine, and has two buttons: "Yes, keep it healthy" and "Not this machine". Only the owner's tap counts. On a yes, the agent gives itself permission to restart that machine and nothing else, and records the owner's choice. On "Not this machine" it never asks again for it. Nothing restarts yet: the restart itself and its Skip and Restart now controls come next.

## What to Tell Your User

Nothing changes for you. This removes a flaky test that was holding back unrelated fixes.

- **Machine care readiness**: "I can now tell you, per machine, whether it could restart and come back by itself, and if not, the one setting that's missing."

- **Machine opt-in**: "I can ask you once per machine whether I may keep it healthy with occasional restarts. You answer with one tap in our chat, and 'Not this machine' means I never ask again."
