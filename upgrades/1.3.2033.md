## What Changed

The notification helper now propagates an explicit input refusal instead of returning a successful-looking reference. Its existing observed handler reports failure before checking for an absent marker. Successful notification references remain unchanged. Direct callers contain refusal without undoing stored secret submissions, spending a refused notice’s hourly slot, crashing a delayed advisory callback, or marking a refused paste notified.

## What to Tell Your User

An agent notification that was refused is no longer reported as submitted just because nothing appeared at the prompt. A secret you successfully submitted stays saved even if its later notification cannot be delivered.

## Summary of New Capabilities

None. This repairs existing receipt behavior without adding settings or automatic retries.
