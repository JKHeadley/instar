## What Changed

Ideas decision confirmations follow the lifetime of the card that opened them. Late preparation or address-check responses no longer open a PIN box after that card leaves the screen. A saved decision still completes, while its delayed refresh skips replaced views. Existing callers of the shared PIN helper retain their behavior unless they supply the optional source check.

## What to Tell Your User

An old Ideas confirmation no longer pops up after you leave the page. Choices you already confirmed still finish normally.

## Summary of New Capabilities

None — this repairs existing decision dialogs and refresh timing.
