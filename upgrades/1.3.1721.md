## What Changed

Adds the Context Budget layer store and transcript archive (`src/monitoring/contextLayers/`; context-budget-layered-summaries §9.7, Phase 1). Each topic gets a store for its derived summaries (H1–H3, chunks, C2, referent stubs, probe records) and for row references to the operator's own words (Anchor, ledger, U). The words themselves are never copied: the store keeps only (message id, sha256, length) and renders from TopicMemory. Each topic also gets a compressed archive of transcript spans, deduplicated by entry id and indexed by entry id and message id.

Every string written to either store is scrubbed field by field with `scrubForStore`, then passed through a live-value pass against the agent's own secrets (auth token, PIN, lifeline secret, secret-store values). An oversize field is kept as a pointer with its size. Archive reads are scrubbed again. Directories are 0700 and files 0600.

Every summary record carries its author and an "as of" stamp, and is offered to the replicated `context-layer-record` kind. That kind stays dark until `multiMachine.stateSync.contextLayers` is on.

Retention is a new Disk Steward category, `context-archive`. Over its limit (default 2 GiB), it evicts superseded spans first, then the oldest, and never a span the current phase points at. An evicted span reads `raw-evicted`.

While the layers are on, backups include the layer store and `topic-memory.db*`. Fold holds, fold markers and seed markers are now never-backup segments.

The switch is `contextBudget.enabled`. It is dev-gated: live on a development agent, dark on the fleet. `GET /context-budget/layers` shows the store's state and returns 503 when the switch is off.

## What to Tell Your User

Nothing changes for you yet. This is the storage for the conversation summaries I'll keep next. It's locked down and cleaned of secrets, it never holds a copy of your own words, and it's only switched on for development agents for now.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Per-topic layer store and transcript archive, with per-field secret scrubbing, row references for protected content, and replication through the context-layer kind.
- `GET /context-budget/layers`: roots and modes, topic count, bytes, replication outcomes.
- Disk Steward category `context-archive`.
