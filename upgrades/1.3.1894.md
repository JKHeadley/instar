## What Changed

Second batch of the bounded-input derivation review (11 more baseline entries). Session history handed to a fresh or revived session no longer cuts each message at 2,000 characters: every Telegram message (up to 4,096) now arrives whole, and a longer one says it was cut. Before, a long instruction from the operator could reach the new session half-cut with no sign. The fix covers every place that renders that history. The preload ranker now keeps the file name of a long path instead of its start. The temporal coherence check now keeps the start and the end of a long state document, where the newest thinking usually is, instead of only the start. The topic summarizer, the promise-revival prompt and the helper-death alert now say in the text when they shortened something. The review baseline goes down by 10.

When the agent gives a promise a set time, it sends a reminder in that conversation, and if the time passes without delivery, a "this is late" line. With Quiet by Default on, both were labelled as routine automatic reports. They went into the Notifications feed instead of the chat, while the agent's records said they had been sent. They now carry their own protected label, "Promises made to you", and are delivered to the conversation, with a limit of 6 per conversation per 10 minutes against a runaway loop. A reminder that is held instead of delivered now counts as a failed send. It is retried and then marked undelivered with an alert, never recorded as "the person was told".

Instar now keeps the quota readings it already takes every 15 minutes, instead of keeping only the last two, and turns them into a forecast (`GET /quota-pacing`). For Claude and for Codex it says whether the group of accounts keeps weekly room all week, and for each account where it will land when its quota resets: on track, will leave some unused, will reach its limit, or at its limit. Each login is counted once even when several machines see it. A missing, stale or old reading shows as "can't tell" with the reason, never as empty or full. The forecast only reports; it changes nothing on its own. It is on for development agents and off for other agents for now.

## What to Tell Your User

Nothing to do. When I start a new session for one of our conversations, I now see your longer messages in full instead of only the first half.

- **Promise reminders**: "When I give you a time and it passes, the note telling you it is late now actually reaches you in our conversation. Before, it could end up only in the Notifications list."

- **Quota forecast**: "I can now see ahead on your subscription quota: whether any account will reset with a lot unused, and whether Claude or Codex as a group will run out before the next reset. Ask me any time."

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Fresh sessions receive each recent Telegram message whole.
- Cut inputs to the summarizer, coherence checks and alerts are disclosed to the model.

| Capability | How to Use |
|-----------|-----------|
| Check-in and late-promise lines are delivered under Quiet by Default | Automatic (still behind `commitments.checkInReminder.dryRun`) |

| Capability | How to Use |
|-----------|-----------|
| Quota forecast per provider and account | Ask the agent; it reads `GET /quota-pacing` |
