## What Changed

Increment 1 of `docs/specs/no-data-loss-on-machine-death.md`: the owned-repository registry, read-only, and disk notices that reach a person.

- New owned-repository registry (`noDataLoss.repos.enabled`, development-agent gate: live on development agents, dark on the fleet). It lists every repository the agent creates or works in on each machine: repositories registered at birth (a successful `init` or `clone` through `SafeGitExecutor`), repositories sessions worked in (hook-event paths), and a budgeted scan of the agent home, the project-map roots and declared roots (`noDataLoss.repos.roots`) that asks git for its untracked and ignored folders instead of walking the disk. Each entry shows its class (owned or candidate), its remotes (host and account, never a URL), the commits and branches no off-machine remote has, their age, and uncommitted work. Another registered agent's home is skipped. Every git call is a read through `SafeGitExecutor.readStream`; nothing fetches, pushes or creates a remote.
- `GET /repos` (this machine) and `GET /repos?scope=pool` (every machine: live within 5 s, else from its replicated records, else named unknown).
- New replicated record kind `owned-repo` (store `ownedRepo`, forced on with the feature; `multiMachine.stateSync.ownedRepo.enabled: false` is the kill switch). Records carry state changes only: class, home-relative path, remote hosts, single-copy yes/no, power-of-two count buckets, and whether the grace window has passed.
- A repository with no remote, or unpushed work, older than 24 hours (1 hour when the disk is below its warn line) is a Needs-you row, one per machine per kind of problem. It opens the new Repositories page (`#/capacity/repos`).
- Disk notices are no longer swallowed by quiet-by-default: the `disk-steward` category is pushed unless the owner explicitly set it to quiet, and a post made while it is quiet says so. A quiet hold now counts as not delivered, so it no longer resets the disk repeat clock. The daily summary of quiet notices runs on any day a disk post was held quiet, even with the digest off.

## What to Tell Your User

I now keep a list of every code repository I work in on each of your computers, and I tell you when work exists on only one of them: a repository with no online copy, or changes that were never pushed, older than a day. You'll see it in "Needs you" and on the new Repositories page. Disk space warnings now reach you instead of sitting quietly in the notifications feed, unless you chose to keep them quiet.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Owned-repository registry: `GET /repos[?scope=pool]`, the `owned-repo` journal kind, a `repos` Needs-you source and the Repositories console page (`noDataLoss.repos.enabled`, development-agent gated).
- Disk notices push by default (`push.categories['disk-steward']: false` keeps them quiet).
