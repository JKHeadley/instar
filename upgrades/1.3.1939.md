## What Changed

SEC-086. The WhatsApp Business API webhook (`POST /webhooks/whatsapp`) sits in front of the server's sign-in check, because Meta calls it without one. Until now it accepted any body, so anyone who could reach the agent's public address could post a made-up message "from" any number, including the owner's, and the agent treated it as real.

Every delivery must now carry Meta's `X-Hub-Signature-256` signature over the exact body, made with the app's App Secret (new `businessApi.appSecret` setting). With the App Secret set, a delivery with no signature or a wrong one is refused with 401 and never processed. With no App Secret configured the webhook runs in observe mode (the identity umbrella's rule that new enforcement starts by observing): deliveries keep arriving, the server log says they are unverified at startup and on the first one, and the owner gets ONE notice (once ever, kept by a marker file in the state folder) that WhatsApp Business deliveries are unverified until the App Secret is set. The verification check (GET) now compares the verify token in constant time.

`instar add whatsapp --backend business-api` takes `--app-secret` (or reads `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET`, so the secret need not appear in the command line), and `instar channels doctor` lists the App Secret as a check. Setup no longer prints the verify token. The WhatsApp Web (Baileys) backend is unchanged.

## What to Tell Your User

- **WhatsApp Business needs one more setting**: "If you use WhatsApp through a Meta Business account, I now only accept messages Meta itself signed. I need your Meta app's App Secret once. Until then your WhatsApp Business messages still reach me, but I cannot prove they came from Meta."

## Summary of New Capabilities

| Capability | How to Use |
|-----------|-----------|
| Signed WhatsApp Business deliveries | Set `businessApi.appSecret` to the Meta App Secret; unsigned deliveries are then refused (without it: delivered unverified, owner told once) |

## Evidence

- Unit: `tests/unit/BusinessApiBackend.test.ts` (+5 tests: correct secret; wrong secret, changed body and missing header; no secret; one owner notice not per delivery; verify token), `tests/unit/WhatsAppWebhookRoutes.test.ts` (+3: 401 without processing, no-secret observe mode delivers and notes once, raw body handed over).
- E2E: `tests/e2e/whatsapp-server-e2e.test.ts` (+2): an unsigned delivery naming the owner's number is refused and never reaches the adapter; a delivery signed with another secret is refused; signed deliveries still arrive. `tests/e2e/business-api-e2e.test.ts`: with no secret, two unsigned deliveries both arrive and the owner-notice hook fires once.
