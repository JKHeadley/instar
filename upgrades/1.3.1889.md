## What Changed

An agent can now bring a person's Claude or ChatGPT history into Instar from the app's data-export zip. A preview shows plain counts first and writes nothing. After the person agrees, the chats and Claude projects are copied into a store on this machine, with their original dates, and the agent can search them. Passwords and keys that appear in chats are removed. The zip is read on this machine only, with strict safety checks, and nothing in the imported store is copied to other machines. The agent reads imported text only as quoted history, never as instructions.

## What to Tell Your User

If you've been using the Claude or ChatGPT app, I can bring your old chats over so you don't need to go back to it. Export your data from that app's settings, and I'll show you what I'd copy before copying anything.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- `POST /imported-history/preview` and `POST /imported-history/apply` (apply needs a fresh preview of the same unchanged file).
- `GET /imported-history/conversations`, `/conversations/:ref`, `/projects`, `/search?q=`. The agent gets quoted history; the owner's session gets raw messages.

## Evidence

26 unit tests (zip safety, streaming reader, both parsers, secret removal, splitting, store, facts, preview/apply), 5 integration tests, and 1 E2E alive test.
