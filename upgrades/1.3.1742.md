## What Changed

Documentation only. Two developer architecture pages describe the Conversations graph modules in `src/core/` that shipped without docs: `site/src/content/docs/architecture/conversations-custody.md` (writes, witness, replication, transfer, storage) and `site/src/content/docs/architecture/conversations-recovery-privacy.md` (pending-write recovery, membership, permanent member loss, owner recovery, erasure). Both are written from module header comments and server wiring, including stated limits. The jobs section of the API reference (`site/src/content/docs/reference/api.md`) also now documents the two job supervision-hold routes added by #3071 (CMT-1610), `GET /jobs/:slug/supervision-hold` and `POST /jobs/:slug/supervision-hold/release`: what a hold is, authentication, the release body, the refusal when the hold's cause is unsettled, and every response status, taken from the route handlers and the scheduler. No runtime source, configuration, migration, test, scanner or threshold changed.
