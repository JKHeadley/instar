# Sign-in repair: stop and ask when Claude wants a human check — plain English

When the automatic sign-in repair reaches Claude's final "Authorize" page and presses Authorize, Claude sometimes silently runs a "prove you're human" check (an hCaptcha picture puzzle) before it will continue. An automated browser can't pass that check, and the repair must never try to trick it. Before this change the repair would sit there, give up, try twice more and quietly fail. Now, if the page is still on the Authorize step fifteen seconds after the click, the repair stops at once and sends the operator the usual "a sign-in needs your help" notice, so a person can open the link and tap Authorize themselves. The rest of the repair, checking that the right account came back, still happens automatically afterwards. A visible picture puzzle is also recognised as a CAPTCHA straight away.
