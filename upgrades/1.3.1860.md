## What Changed

The owner can now pause and resume a project the Chief of Staff tracks, and only the owner. When the owner asks in chat, the agent calls `POST /chief-of-staff/projects/:key/pause-ask` citing the owner's own logged Telegram message (checked against the logs: found, written by a person, sent by the owner, not forwarded); the owner gets one Telegram button showing their whole message, and only the owner's tap on that exact message writes an immutable `owner-pause` / `owner-resume` record. A paused project is not checked, restarted, redirected or revived (the action protocol also refuses inside its lock with `owner-paused`, and the Liveness Reconciler and silence backstop stand down with `cos-owner-paused`); the note lists it as "Paused by you"; the dashboard Needs-you list skips it. `GET /chief-of-staff` and the project detail carry `pausedBy`. The Chief of Staff store now keeps lifecycle events it doesn't know instead of rejecting them (a rejected record froze an older machine's whole Chief of Staff stream) and counts them by name in `GET /chief-of-staff` (`unknownLifecycleEvents`). Rollback is `chiefOfStaff.ownerPause.asks: false` (stops new pause asks; resumes keep working), never a revert.

When the agent sends a Threadline message and waits for the reply, the wait was matched to the conversation thread. On a single-person agent any peer that could post into that thread could answer it. The wait now also records the peer the message was routed to, and only a message proven to come from that peer resolves it, on every agent. Multi-person agents keep their existing rule (the addressed peer only, never under post-only) on top. The rule lives in one small module (`src/threadline/replyWaiterMatch.ts`) used by both inbound paths.

## What to Tell Your User

You can now pause a project your Chief of Staff tracks. Tell me in chat, like "pause the 2.0 work until …". I'll send you one button with your own words; tap it and the project is paused: I stop checking it, restarting it or nudging it until you say to resume. Nothing pauses without your tap.

Nothing changes in normal use: replies from the agent you messaged come back as before.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Pause or resume a tracked project with one owner tap on a button that quotes your own message.
- Paused projects show as "Paused by you" and stop asking you for anything.

None. Security hardening of Threadline send-and-wait.
