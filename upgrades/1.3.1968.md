## What Changed

Recommended services, rollout step 4 (`docs/specs/recommended-services.md`, "Re-offering after a no" reason 1): after a no, or an offer never answered, the agent may ask once more, but only when a real problem shows up that the service would fix. Ships off behind `recommendedServices.reoffers.enabled`.

- New `reoffer` row in the signed recommended-services ledger (`event` / `pending` / `offered`). A re-offer never changes the saved choice.
- Deterministic floor in `src/recommended/reoffers.ts`: the problem must come after the no and be under 30 days old; at most one re-offer a day across services; the same problem waits 90, then 180, then 360 days before it can ask about the same service again; spent only when the platform confirms delivery.
- First problem source: `voice-unreadable`, signalled by the shared voice transcription wrapper when nothing on the machine can transcribe a voice note. It re-offers voice transcription.

## What to Tell Your User

Nothing changes yet: this is off by default. When it's on, a service you said no to can come back once, only if the agent hits the exact problem that service fixes (today: a voice note it can't read), and the message says why it's asking. Your no stands unless you say yes.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Re-offers after a real problem (`recommendedServices.reoffers.enabled`, default off). No new routes.
