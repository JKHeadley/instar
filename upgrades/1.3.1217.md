## What Changed

Paired machines can recover from lost or regenerated identity files through a
separately escrowed, keychain-backed recovery root. Exact-epoch replacement
claims prove both possession of the new signing key and continuity with the
pinned recovery root; ambiguity quarantines for a claim-bound PIN decision.

The release also protects identity authority files from bearer file access,
adds crash-atomic recovery-root rotation and signed peer receipts, retains
observed private endpoints as non-authoritative evidence, and bounds automatic
retry through the SelfActionGovernor and a terminal 72-hour horizon.

## What to Tell Your User

If a paired computer loses its local identity files, the mesh can now heal the
pairing without blind trust or manual file edits when the recovery proof is
valid. Unverifiable or conflicting changes wait visibly in the Machines
dashboard for approval or denial.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Keychain-only recovery roots established through explicit pairing.
- Replay-safe, exact-epoch identity re-announcement and peer acknowledgement.
- Crash-atomic root rotation with signed operator grants and receipts.
- Protected identity storage and authenticated pool-wide status.
- Bounded automatic recovery with durable restart reconciliation.
- Mobile dashboard review for quarantined identity changes.
- Fleet-dark, development dry-run-first rollout.
