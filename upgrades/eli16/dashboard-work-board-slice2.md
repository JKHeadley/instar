# Work cards say why they need you

A work card now says **why it needs you**, right under what the agent recommends, and what it is about (for example a pull request number). Cards the Chief of Staff opens for a project waiting on you are marked "From the Chief of Staff". When something in Needs you has a card on the work board, its row shows a small "Has a card on the work board" link next to it, so you can jump to the card while the row still does what it did before.
