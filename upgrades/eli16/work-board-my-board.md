# My board on the Work Board

The Work Board holds one card for each thing the agent needs a person for: an approval, an answer, a sign-off. Until now the board sorted cards into lanes by whose move it is, so everything waiting on you sat in one pile. Luna, another agent, runs a board where each person gets "My board": only the cards that need them, sorted by why. The operator asked for ours to match.

The agent's server now adds those lists to every board read. There are five, always in the same order: cards waiting for your approval (including a done claim you need to confirm), cards waiting for your reply to a question, your own cards (the rest of what is waiting on you), cards you were added to (for a team member, the cards the owner shared with them), and cards on hold. Each card appears once, under the most pressing reason. Inside each list, cards nobody has touched yet come first, then the started ones, newest first, and each card already carries whose turn it is so an app can colour it.

Nothing new is saved and no rule about who may approve anything changes. The lists are worked out fresh on each read from the cards the board already shows, so a team member never learns about a card they could not already see, and the counts of cards the board left out for space only ever count that person's own cards. An app that talks to an older agent, which does not send the lists, keeps drawing the lanes as before. One small fix rides along: when the board leaves cards out for space and two cards have the same time, the card id decides, so every machine leaves out the same ones.
