# Stay signed in when the agent briefly can't be reached

If you open the dashboard while the connection to your agent is down, the dashboard used to forget your sign-in and ask for your PIN again, even though nothing was wrong with it. Now it keeps your sign-in and shows the usual "Still trying to reach the agent" line, then carries on when the connection comes back.

Your sign-in is still dropped when the agent itself says it is wrong. A newly typed sign-in is only accepted after the agent really answers.
