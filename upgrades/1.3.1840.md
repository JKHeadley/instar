## What Changed

Two recommended-service setup recipes passed their live proofs on 2026-11-09 and are now marked proven in the service catalog (`src/recommended/catalog.ts`): Telegram (`telegram-v1`) and email to the owner (`email-v1`). Until now each was available only to an agent that listed it under `recommendedServices.provingRecipes`. Every agent now lists both as available, so a person can say yes to either on the confirm page and the agent sets it up. The proactive offer list itself is unchanged and still behind `recommendedServices.offers.enabled` (default off).

## What to Tell Your User

You can now ask me to set up Telegram or email, and I'll handle it end to end. With Telegram I make the bot and a group for us; with email I can send the reports you choose to your inbox, and only to you. You approve on one page; I do the rest.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Telegram setup recipe available on every agent (was proving-list only).
- Owner email recipe available on every agent (was proving-list only).
