## What Changed

Instar now recognizes OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6.1 Terra on the Codex door. Both were live-checked on the ChatGPT account on 2026-11-15. `gpt-6.1-terra` joins the accepted Codex model list (`gpt-6.1-sol` was already there), and both are listed in the model registry as recognized, not promoted. No default model, tier or pin changes. The daily frontier-model check found them.

## What to Tell Your User

Nothing changes unless you want it to. If you'd like a conversation to use GPT-6.1 Terra, you can now ask for it and it will be accepted.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Pinning a conversation or job to `gpt-6.1-terra` is accepted.
