## What Changed

Five console fixes from a live check of Echo's console. Home "What needs you" collapses rows with the same words into one row that says how many, drops the general "sign-in repair is waiting" notice when the named sign-in rows are listed, no longer repeats the headline item as the first row, and replaces the "partial" corner tag with what did not answer (`dashboard/console/home-model.js` `combineNeeds`, `collapseRepeats`, `needsCardList`, `partialWords`). The "Remote Desktop needs its Mac permission again" notice now opens the Machines page, where a card names the Mac, the permission (Screen Recording and Accessibility) and the one fix (`machines-model.js` `desktopPermissionFixes`, `machines.js`). Work page tiles line up in equal-height rows and the conversation-graph tile is called "Efforts". Promise list rows show plain words in place of feedback, promise, thread, topic and machine ids, with conversation names where known (`display-identity.js` `plainReferenceText`). The Accounts header counts ready accounts and accounts that need a sign-in the same way the rows do (`accounts-model.js` `accountCounts`).

## What to Tell Your User

The console's front page is shorter: each thing that needs you appears once, and the top item is not listed twice. When remote desktop on one of your Macs loses its permission, the link now opens a card that says which Mac, which setting and what to do there. Promises read in plain words, and the Accounts page header matches its list, for example "11 of 14 ready" and "3 need you to sign in".

## Summary of New Capabilities

| Capability | How to Use |
|-----------|------------|
| One line per thing that needs you on Home, with a count when several share a name | Automatic |
| A Mac permission fix card on the Machines page | Tap the Remote Desktop permission item on Home |
| Promise rows in plain words; full text in the promise details | Automatic |
| Accounts header with ready and needs-sign-in counts that match the list | Automatic |
