## What Changed

`SessionMigrator`'s no-alternative enforcement tiers (`enforced_pause` ≥90%,
`enforced_kill` ≥95% of the 5-hour rate) paused the scheduler with no release
path, so every scheduled job stayed silently skipped until a server restart
(originating agent, 2026-09-24: ~12h). The migrator now tracks that it took the
pause and `releaseEnforcementPauseIfRecovered()` resumes the scheduler when the
5-hour rate (when known) is below the migration threshold minus 5 points and
weekly usage is below its threshold. It runs on every quota collection
(before the estimated-data early return) and at the top of `checkAndMigrate`
(before the cooldown), emits `enforced_resume`, and sends one notification.
Only a pause the migrator took is ever released. The per-job quota gate is
unchanged. The kill notice no longer says "Manual intervention required".

## What to Tell Your User

If I briefly hit my usage limit, my scheduled tasks pause to save what's left.
Before this fix they stayed paused until I was restarted. Now they pick back
up by themselves once usage recovers, and I'll send a short note when that
happens.

## Summary of New Capabilities

- Quota-enforcement scheduler pauses release automatically on recovery, with a
  5-point hysteresis, and announce it (`enforced_resume`).

## Evidence

`tests/unit/quota-enforcement.test.ts` — eight new tests: release after
enforced_kill and enforced_pause; holding inside the hysteresis band and while
weekly is over threshold; unknown 5-hour treated as recovered when weekly is
healthy; never resuming a pause it did not take; releasing inside the cooldown;
releasing once. `tests/integration/quota-enforcement-wiring.test.ts` — the real
`QuotaManager` collection path releases on estimated data and sends the
resume notice. `tests/e2e/quota-enforcement-lifecycle.test.ts` — a real
`JobScheduler` paused by enforcement skips triggers, then runs them after a
recovered collection with no restart.
