# A2 — Check existing work before starting or dispatching it

[A1](canonical-records-and-work-discovery.md) provides authorized read-only evidence and canonical record contracts. The original permanent-address answer treated work in an existing pull request as new. A2 applies the read-only evidence to ordinary replies and durable work starts. A supported entry point is a particular delivery or creation route whose real path has proved the policy. Its audience-visible revision tracks only the records the current person and destination may see. The contention domain names the group of starts that can compete, such as the same repository and action family. An atomic compare-and-set changes a record only if its expected revision still matches. It adds explicit admission and retry fences to avoid duplicating work or exposing hidden work during arbitration. It does not change owning service access or actuation authority.

## Frontloaded decisions

1. Route work-related answers and recommendations through a bounded, destination-authorized evidence lookup before the response. Preserve normal live delivery when the classifier or lookup is unavailable, and qualify statements that depend on missing coverage.
2. Record an authorized discovery observation and disposition before starting durable work on an enrolled surface. An exact source occurrence and effect have one durable ID and one authoritative result. Similarity is evidence for judgment.
3. The owner validates admission under its own serializer using an authorization-filtered current start and reservation index with audience-local revisions. A hidden backlog cannot consume the caller's visible budget or force a visible restart. The owner still validates internal invariants without inserting hidden rows into the caller's result.
4. For non-idempotent external dispatch, perform a fenced durable dispatch-started write before any irreversible call. A timeout, expiry or restart grants no second attempt. Safe retry requires proof that the previous claimant cannot dispatch and did not cause the effect.
5. Enroll supported seams independently after normal user-path proof. A1 read paths, ordinary continuations and existing safety controls remain available even when a new initiation gate is disabled.

## Identify what the user authorized

The authenticated intake owner assigns stable occurrence IDs under a CAS of native source revision and occurrence-set revision before fan-out. A worker that loses the CAS rereads and uses the winning assignments. A source can carry many accepted action occurrences with separate source span or owner-verified bindings. Store the accepted occurrence's original effect key and digest as an immutable owner binding. Edits use explicit supersession and do not mint or redirect an accepted effect by changing a destination or payload. A send to a new destination needs a newly authorized occurrence under the existing owner policy, while the original attempt remains findable and unknown if necessary. Test a destination edit after a lost dispatch response and prove it cannot re-key the original. Existing verifiable native IDs remain the roots. The owning mutation service derives effect identity from origin, occurrence, tenant/scope, operation family and destination where part of the effect. Manual alternatives and automated handoffs for the same authorized operation preserve that identity across frameworks. A distinct related action can create its own ID, independent of optional cross-linking.

The delivery owner classifies prior-work and status questions, recommendations and potential novelty/absence claims using the existing bounded judgment path. Run observation in the verified-owner Telegram delivery, new or queued conversation and forwarded paths, after reauthorization and before a delivery receipt. A native app path uses its authenticated pre-input delivery. Unproven paths remain advisory. An unavailable classifier has no authority to prevent planned delivery. Outbound judgment may propose a verified statement, a bounded statement of actual search coverage, or uncertainty. At send, the existing authorized adapter revalidates final destination, audience and currently authorized dependencies before releasing a drafted sourced reply. A draft whose source or destination became invalid is discarded or reduced to content authorized for the new audience. The sender cannot rely on the earlier prompt authorization. Pause after lookup and revoke the grant or widen the destination in the canary. Untrusted source content never supplies destination, permission or instructions.

## Admission without disclosing hidden work

POST /work/discovery-receipts issues an opaque random action-observation handle. The digest covers target, payload and action fields under the owner’s canonical encoding. The client-held handle contains no query, evidence or identity material. Apply source authorization and erasure to the owner-side record before use, and retain the minimal effect replay proof separately. Forgetting the source invalidates any outstanding observation and cannot be reversed by presenting its handle. Check current destination and source/erasure/authority generation again during acceptance. A collision with changed parameters fails via the ordinary generic action conflict without disclosing a prior result. An owning service derives the intent and mandatory providers from the verified concrete operation. A caller cannot nominate a weaker profile. Required provider absence and omitted lookup are distinct outcomes. Normal durable initiation needs verified origin and owner evidence. For repository starts consult Git/PR or versioned spec evidence when available. For a reversible locally authorized effect whose distinct purpose a current owner can establish from the verified roster, the profile may permit an explicitly degraded disposition through its existing policy. Consequential or external actions need their own verified authority and applicable evidence floor; a missing provider alone neither grants nor removes that authority. A job-owned operation needs its authoritative occurrence. If minimum evidence is missing, coordinator and provider outage use the same bounded pending/retry path.

Use a separate authorized capability-partitioned index for active starts and reservations. Projection-only filtering after an unscoped scan is inadequate. Index by tenant, mutation family, contention domain, eligible audience scope, operation identity and lifecycle. Exact owner epoch, current authorization/erasure epoch and audience-visible roster revision are the transaction boundaries. The receipt records which visible active domain and applied head it actually observed. At accept, query the authorized indexed delta or current bounded active roster directly, including active reservations and already accepted work whose discovery capture is pending. Keep an authorized completion delta for a start that finishes before its evidence is verifiably discoverable. The owner compares that delta as well as currently active starts. Retain it for outstanding observations whose verified provider coverage predates that record. Once source application is proven, the owner can discard the delta only after the last relevant observation expires or by atomically fencing its minimum accepted coverage frontier and making older authorized snapshots refresh. A previously issued frozen result never counts as coverage of later revisions. Test a second observation followed by capture completion and eviction of the first completed delta before acceptance. If capacity or custody cannot preserve the delta, the affected acceptance remains explicitly pending. Test creation and completion between lookup and acceptance while capture is paused, with two different source occurrences. Bind disposition to the exact authorized roster revision actually considered. Under the short owner transaction compare the current revision and scope. A relevant visible change returns the newly authorized delta to repeat judgment outside the transaction within the existing budget. The owner may accept only the resulting current disposition. An incomplete bounded roster that could omit a material candidate takes the pending path with explicit coverage. Test two distinct source occurrences and show the second adjudication sees the first newly reserved start. Keep canonical cross-audience exact-effect deduplication inside the owner without exposing otherwise inadmissible work. The initial effect key includes the authorization domain. Two distinct authorized audiences have disjoint visible effect identities unless the owner proves their preexisting logical effect has the same visible authority. For a seam with unavoidable shared physical effect identity, only enroll when paired hidden-committed, hidden-reserved and absent requests produce the same authorized submission and status result, handling and schedule. Return the same opaque owner-scoped accepted handle and public pending shape in each permitted case, and reveal completion evidence only from the authorized audience's own observable operation. If visible execution, ID, eventual poll result, delay class or allowed behavior inherently differs, the seam stays advisory and keeps its existing core action authorization. Do not use a generic wording or an error status to mask an otherwise detectable effect. If a service cannot maintain the audience-specific equivalent or reserve scope-specific capacity outside an unscoped lock/scan delay, its start path remains advisory. Controller availability is a generic service condition and cannot vary with a hidden-only input. Independent queue and capacity tests verify isolation under hidden backlog.

The origin, owner, current permissions, action authorization and idempotency are hard floors. A verified authorized prior effect continues or returns its result. For a new allowed effect, record continue/link, distinct purpose, or scope-limited unavailable disposition when authorized and safe. ## Fenced receiver and effect recovery

Each enrolled receiver atomically claims its operation ID and carries the same stable key to every downstream that supports idempotent acceptance and authoritative result lookup. The connector must declare its downstream's enforceable deduplication window and action scope. Require a receiver-enforced request expiry or a verified upper bound for arrival before the deduplication window closes, including queued and proxied retries. A caller timeout alone cannot bound delivery. A retry cannot rely on a key after that guaranteed window. If the receiver cannot enforce an appropriate bound, use the one-shot path instead. Test a retry submitted shortly before expiry and delivered afterwards. If the downstream outcome stays unknown after key expiry, resolve through authoritative evidence or hold until independently verified safe retry. Test an accepted call with a lost reply recovered after key expiry and count actual effects. Process crash before local commit leaves the effect pending. A completed local mutation records the key and result in one transaction; downstream reconciliation attaches a proof when completion is external. A retry returns the recorded result or pending state while result evidence is retained and currently authorized. A scoped closed-origin watermark establishes that no new action may happen, even when the outcome was intentionally retained for less time. A replay that encounters only closure proof returns “closed, result unavailable” to an authorized caller. It never synthesizes success or reruns. If a service promises exact-result replay beyond its own retention it must retain a scoped durable result reference under the relevant policy. Test two compacted occurrences with different historical outcomes.

For a downstream without enforceable idempotency, use the hierarchy's single-claimant precedents. Under the current owner fence, persist the immutable dispatch-started marker with operation ID, claimant epoch, intended destination and opaque attempt generation, using the enrolled authenticated failover custody contract before the irrevocable boundary. A local append alone cannot authorize an external call on a pooled owner. An enrolled member must recover the fenced head through the marker and any re-arm before issuing or accepting a permit. The actual sending gateway exclusively holds the credential or connector-enforced capability binding the send to operation, destination and generation. Workers never receive a reusable raw credential or independent send path. At the existing authorized effect gateway, consume a single-use operation/attempt permit atomically and durably under the same custody proof. Consume only under current caller, destination, payload digest, owner epoch and authority/erasure fence. The original claimant or a restarted worker cannot reconsume. Disable automatic retries in the SDK, transport and forwarding proxy for non-idempotent paths. Alternative send routes share the permit boundary or the connector remains ineligible. Serialize the current authorization and fence with handoff at the last controllable irrevocable boundary. A revocation before that boundary prevents the send. An accepted handoff remains irrevocable. A later revocation can prevent subsequent operations. Allow exactly one downstream call per spent permit. If an adapter cannot make that boundary enforceable against a paused worker, keep it ineligible. Once dispatch-started is committed, successors do not send. They can query trusted effect evidence or hold the operation unknown. Re-arm by invalidating the old permit under the current authority and committing a new attempt generation with durable proof. A successor retries only when independent positive proof shows both no effect and that the original claimant cannot now dispatch, for example a fenced gateway veto proving no bytes issued and a terminated earlier worker. Neither a stale heartbeat nor missing result is positive proof. If the client has no reliable fencing, preserve unknown and route owner-visible reconciliation with the existing urgency and access controls. Unproven connectors remain advisory. Late completion by the predecessor attaches evidence without creating another effect. Expiring a reservation releases review work, not the dispatch permission or durable result.

Keep durable effect markers while the canonical source can replay. Closed-watermark proof binds origin generation and verified contiguous scope, not only a largest observed offset. Holes remain explicit unresolved or exception records. An erased origin retains the minimal scoped closure proof already authorized under the source policy; if replay proof cannot lawfully survive, close the generation to new admission. Any restored origin that lacks positive current status or usable proof is refused or held by the owner. Disabling this A2 observation gate cannot bypass spent, closed or unknown effect checks in the action owner. A monotonic source can compact verified closed origins to a generation-scoped contiguous watermark and explicit exceptions. Non-monotonic open origins retain bounded terminal markers. Authorized work backpressures before admission rather than dropping live replay proof. No mandatory enforcement where this storage or proof cannot be provisioned.

## Decision points touched

- Origin identity, observation authentication and exact effect replay — invariant; basis: irreversible-consequence; evidence: a recycled operation key can execute a second external action or admit work without verified scope. Require current authorities and atomic owner keys.
- Authorization isolation and disposition expansion — invariant; basis: irreversible-consequence; evidence: a hidden population can reveal itself through a restart or admission decision even without returned fields. Use the authorized per-audience index and unchanged observable outcomes under equivalent scoped inputs.
- Relevance, uncertainty and legitimate distinctness — judgment-candidate. Use the existing owner-scoped agent and outbound arbiter. It can link, propose distinctness, narrow a claim or state uncertainty. It cannot widen source access, issuer permission or existing action authority. On arbiter failure default to a scoped answer or owner pending path.
- Non-idempotent dispatch takeover — invariant; basis: irreversible-consequence; evidence: a paused worker can resume after its lease expired and send a duplicate external effect. Block second sends after dispatch-started without positive prior no-dispatch proof and fenced termination.
- Recovery from unknown outcome — judgment-candidate. Authoritative reconciliation may attach positive effect proof, preserve unknown or re-arm an eligible operation after positive no-dispatch evidence. Conservative fallback retains unknown with existing owner routing.

## Multi-machine posture

The existing canonical mutation owner holds active starts, observations and the operation result journal under its declared authenticated custody. Deployment rollback can disable new observation enforcement by seam. It keeps source/destination authorization, erasure, existing canonical writer fences and committed/unknown effect proof.
