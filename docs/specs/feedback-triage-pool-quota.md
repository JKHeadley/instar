# Feedback triage account-aware quota admission

## Problem statement

The registered feedback triage authority is `codex-cli` / `gpt-6-astra`. Triage reads the ambient Codex login's quota once per tick. On 2026-10-31 that login was at 78% of its weekly allowance, beyond the existing 75% pause floor, so the sorter made zero calls even though four active local Codex logins had fresh readings of 20%, 10%, 3% and 1%. An unchecked router fallback can also spend on another account or provider after the gate, even if the arbiter rejects its answer. This repair must pair a verified eligible account with every primary triage invocation while preserving the approved provider/model, owner fencing, daily cap, prompt, output checks and disposition floors.

## Proposed design

1. **Account-specific live evidence.** On the canonical owner, enumerate only trusted `SubscriptionPool` Codex accounts that are `active`, not identity-drifted, and have a present local credential home. `isLocallyExecutable` also admits `warming` and checks only a nonempty path, so those checks are additional. Before and after a quota read, verify the local login identity against the pool record and fingerprint the credential file without logging its contents; a changed identity/version invalidates the read. Read through the account-specific live app-server reader with that account's `codexHome`. Its parser already selects the Astra/Sol `codex` limit family, including its documented legacy absent-family case; preserve that parser contract and return verified-family provenance to the selector. A different family's favorable 0% cannot admit. The generic pool snapshot and historical rollout reader lack a sound binding between quota evidence and the current credential version, so **neither is an admission fallback in `on` mode**. A live transport/auth failure, deliberate `subscriptionPool.codexLiveQuota: false`, or missing identity proof makes that candidate unreadable; no older favorable value may override it. `off` mode retains ambient account choice, subject to the live, same-subscription evidence and strict provider route below; cached or rollout quota fallback cannot admit spend.
2. **Bounded selection.** Accept the provider's documented window structure: weekly primary alone is legitimate, at least one real window is required, and every present percentage must be finite and within 0–100. Reject `windowsUnavailable`, invalid timestamps, any returned exhausted state, and readings whose `codex` family cannot be verified. The highest used percentage of every reported window must be **strictly below 75**; exactly 75 pauses. The live acquisition timestamp must be no more than 60 seconds old at invocation and no more than one minute in the future. Probe a bounded two-account wave at a time; order candidates by a rotating start index across ticks, using pool snapshots only for non-authoritative ordering if desired. Continue to later accounts when an early wave is unreadable so three slow accounts cannot starve a fourth. When a wave has eligible completed readings and sufficient model-call time remains, cancel/reap outstanding reads and choose the lowest used percentage in that completed set, then stable account ID; a slow sibling does not force a pause. Otherwise continue within an aggregate selection budget of at most 30 seconds and the tick's remaining model-call budget. When the budget expires, stop launching reads, cancel and reap in-flight app-server children, then use any eligible completed reading still within the 60-second admission age; pause only if none is valid. Propagate cancellation and the remaining deadline into the live reader, retain concurrency slots until child exit, and use bounded termination escalation. An observation-mode selector follows the same bounds and cannot outlive its tick. Never use `Promise.race` alone as a resource-lifetime bound.
3. **Bind the actual call.** Keep a local-only immutable attempt record containing pool account ID, credential home, verified login identity/version, `codex` family provenance, bounded usage, source and acquisition time. Immediately after asynchronous selection, recheck canonical owner, epoch, exact authority generation/binding, remaining model-call time budget, and that the selected account remains active, non-drifted, with the same home and credential identity/version. Extend `FeedbackDrainStore.reserveAuthorityCalls` so expected owner host/epoch, exact active authority binding, and daily cap are checked in the **same database transaction** as the slot increment; a separate precheck is insufficient. If ownership/account/authority changes after a successful reservation but before child launch, conditionally release that unstarted reservation in the same fenced ledger and abort. On a lost owner before reservation, emit zero model calls, slots or pause notices. A launched call cannot be unwound; its slot remains consumed and the event is audited. Pass `IntelligenceOptions.accountOverride` to `FeedbackTriageArbiter.decideBatch` or its canary. The provider must fail closed if that explicit override becomes invalid rather than falling back to ambient credentials. Recheck credential fingerprint, owner and authority immediately before child launch; retain existing result-write fencing. A credential replacement or ownership change after child launch cannot be made atomically safe with the current CLI home interface; record that residual rather than claiming a distributed spend lock. The audit names the **admitted** account, not a proven eventual CLI identity; a subsequent changed fingerprint marks execution identity unverified. Do not replicate the local attempt record. Serialize a separate allowlisted audit projection containing only opaque account ID, authority generation, limit family, bounded usage, timestamps and outcome; exclude login identity, credential version, home path and report text from replication.
4. **Make authority strict before spend.** Add a triage per-call strict-route constraint to `IntelligenceRouter`: before *any* provider invocation, resolve and verify the registered `codex-cli` provider and **exact `gpt-6-astra` model ID** (or a specifically approved equivalent alias), then prohibit default-provider degradation, missing-config default routing, incompatible nature-plan routing, sibling-account retry and cross-framework failure swap. Sharing the `codex` quota family does not authorize Sol as the decision model. Set `IntelligenceOptions.strictModelPin: true` on primary batches, explicit retries and canaries in **all** account-selection modes: `CodexCliIntelligenceProvider` otherwise makes its own unsupported-model fallback call after the router check, spending on another model without a second reservation. A missing primary binary or incompatible model returns a contract error without spending; an unsupported Astra response causes one failed CLI launch and no provider-internal model retry. Strict execution applies inside the provider as well as the router. The router currently has early default-provider exits before its retry tails, so disabling tails alone is insufficient. The arbiter's post-response provider/model check stays as defense in depth. The triage service alone owns its existing second attempt, repeating account selection, owner/authority checks and cap reservation. This strict-route safety check is always active for triage, including when pool selection is disabled or rolled back.
5. **Preserve budgets and make refusal a pause.** Select and revalidate before each `reserveCall`, including the first batch, explicit retry and self-heal canary. If selection finishes without eligible capacity, classify it as `quota-pause` when a verified reading is >=75 or `quota-unreadable-pause` when no trustworthy reading exists. This ends the tick, keeps the pause episode/attention logic and pending items, and consumes no further slot. A timed-out sibling does not block an eligible completed reading. A first model failure followed by an ineligible retry is audited as a failed first attempt plus quota pause; it does not become `batch-unusable`, increment unusable counters or start/advance self-healing. An ineligible due canary leaves its healing attempt count and next schedule unchanged. The daily 150-call cap, retriage subcap, time budget, owner epoch and all disposition floors remain. Existing second-opinion and weekly-grading providers are separate auxiliary call sites: keep their current independent admission/cap and routing contracts, do not pass the triage Codex override to them, and test that this change neither bypasses nor accidentally broadens their authority. The primary selector does not claim to govern every auxiliary provider's allowance.
6. **Modes and rollout.** `feedbackFactory.triage.accountSelectionMode` is `off | observe | on`, missing/invalid value `off`; migrate missing config idempotently and set new-agent defaults explicitly. The strict-route constraint in item 4 is always on. `off` uses the ambient account with live, same-subscription quota admission and strict route; `observe` does the same model behavior and separately logs the account a pool selector *would* choose, without using its credentials; `on` uses only verified live account-specific readings. If live reads are disabled, `on` pauses rather than switching to a cached route. `observe` uses its own bounded reader lifecycle and cannot delay or change an ambient model call. Evaluate mode at each tick start, with one consistent mode through that tick. Reverting to `off` restores ambient account choice but never restores cached admission or hidden credential/provider fallbacks. Logs contain account ID, family, bounded percentages, source/age, model and outcome; never email, credential home, tokens or report text. Prior decisions and audit remain immutable.

## Review addendum: execution and evidence contracts

The account-specific quota reader and the triage model child must use the **same verified subscription authentication source**, not merely the same `CODEX_HOME` string. Construct a dedicated scrubbed environment for both children and reject admission if the effective source cannot be proved. Remove `OPENAI_API_KEY`, organization/project billing overrides, and incompatible auth/configuration overrides even when `INSTAR_DISABLE_RULE1_OPENAI=1`; do not pass an explicit provider API key on this path. The generic Codex child environment escape hatch does not apply to pool-admitted triage. Check effective source before and after the read and immediately before launch. A present credential file is insufficient when another source can take precedence.

Bound each two-account probe wave, including cancellation and child reap, to five seconds within the 30-second aggregate budget. A hung first wave must leave time for later candidates. Check tick cancellation before the transactional reservation and again at the model launch barrier. A cancelled launched child is terminated with bounded escalation, while its reserved daily slot stays consumed and its outcome is audited.

Give each reservation an opaque unique token and an atomic `reserved → launched` transition. Release only that exact token while it is still `reserved`, idempotently, even after an owner handoff. A release racing with launch must have exactly one winner; a launched reservation is never refunded. Preserve the owner/epoch/authority check for creation and launch. The local attempt and allowlisted replicated audit share opaque attempt and reservation IDs; replicated lifecycle events record reservation, launch, release, completion and handoff observations with owner host/epoch and authority generation. This is execution provenance, not credential metadata.

The live reader must return both acquisition and underlying observation timestamps, or establish that the provider measurement is made during that specific read. Require each available timestamp to be no more than 60 seconds old and no more than one minute in the future at the launch barrier. Recheck after sibling cancellation/reaping and reservation. Newly acquired stale observations cannot admit spend.

Acceptance tests for this contract must cover an inherited `OPENAI_API_KEY`, the enabled `INSTAR_DISABLE_RULE1_OPENAI` escape hatch, an explicit provider API key, and reader/model child environments that differ. Each must either use the verified subscription for both operations or pause before model spend. Exercise two hung first-wave readers followed by a healthy later account, cancellation before reservation and at the launch barrier, cancellation after launch, concurrent launch/release and repeated release of one token, and an owner handoff with an in-flight call reconstructed from replicated lifecycle events. Exercise a newly acquired stale observation and an otherwise fresh reading that expires while a sibling is reaped or a reservation is awaited.

The effective-authentication-source rule also applies in `off` and `observe` modes. Their account choice stays ambient, but their quota read must be live and bound to the same verified ambient subscription as the model child; a rollout or cached quota value cannot authorize a call. If ambient subscription identity or effective source cannot be established, pause before spend. This safety floor remains when account selection is rolled back.

The live reader must distinguish a genuinely absent window from a present malformed one before normalizing its response. A valid primary with a reported secondary that lacks a required field is invalid as a whole; dropping the malformed secondary is forbidden. Test the 10% primary plus 100% secondary with missing `resetsAt` case.

The reservation token covers both the daily call count and any retriage subcap increment. Record the original UTC accounting day and subcap kind/amount with the token. Create, launch, and idempotently release these debits through one transaction on the shared database, including across owner handoff; a released unstarted retry restores both counters for their original day, and a launched retry restores neither. Test an aborted retriage at a UTC day boundary and a launch/release race.

## Decision points touched

- **Spend admission — invariant:** the existing deterministic 75% floor applies to the exact account and `codex` limit family that will serve a primary triage attempt. It is an explicit resource guard, not a judgment about feedback meaning; no model may reinterpret it.
- **Owner and account route — invariant:** a bounded selector chooses local eligible capacity, followed by owner/epoch/authority/account revalidation and an owner-fenced transactional call reservation before invocation. If any required evidence fails, no model starts. The ownership and capacity floor is deterministic because an unverified route must not spend.
- **Provider/model route — invariant:** the registered Astra authority is verified before execution; no router fallback can spend on a different account/provider under this decision point. The registered authority is an exact execution constraint, not a preference to arbitrate.
- **Disposition — judgment-candidate:** the existing triage arbiter chooses within the bounded `work`/`hold`/`ignore` action space, subject to the existing deterministic floors and closed output schema. Unusable or unverified answers fall to the existing conservative hold/pause path, ending at deterministic refusal to spend when quota or authority evidence is missing; the account selector has no authority over report meaning.

## Multi-machine posture

Triage runs on the canonical drain owner. Pool metadata can arrive from peers, but credentials and fresh per-account quota reads are physically local. Only owner-local active accounts enter selection; a new owner reselects on its machine rather than replaying another host's choice. The triage disposition and allowlisted audit projection follow the existing drain replication path; the local execution attempt record does not. The rotating candidate cursor is machine-local, advisory ordering state; a new owner starts its own cursor without affecting admission or duplicating decisions. The owner-fenced pause notification remains one voice per episode. No credential paths, login identities, credential versions, selectors or quota caches replicate. If the new owner lacks eligible local capacity it pauses; it never spends through a metadata-only peer account.

machine-local-justification: physical-credential-locality — the login, its credential version and its account-specific live quota reading exist only where that login can execute.

## Maturation plan

- **test-agent-live:** after full three-tier and exact-head aggregate tests, turn `on` for the supervised test-agent class and exercise under a low call cap; capture successful selections and refusals in its own audit.
- **dev-agent-live:** within 48 hours of test-agent evidence, turn `on` for development agents, including Echo as an agent in that class, if the own logged decisions show selected account, quota family/age, zero unchecked fallback, and no owner-handoff spend. Do not create an Echo-only permanent allowlist.
- **fleet:** within seven days of dev-agent activation, graduate if two daily windows show real triage decisions, eligible-account selection and quota pauses without wrong-family/owner spending; otherwise leave the current rung and record a named repair/rollback action with a due date.
- **graduation criterion:** own logged selections, refusals, provider calls, owner handoffs and pause episodes match this contract. No call may start after an invalid owner/authority fence is observed; a call that started before a handoff and finishes afterward is logged separately as the acknowledged residual, not counted as a new stale-owner start. Tests and configuration presence alone do not graduate it. The rollout owner is Echo; the feature maturation tracker records each rung and its evidence.
- **dark-window:** at most seven days after merge before a test-agent `on` decision, then the class deadlines above. A missed deadline is surfaced by the maturation tracker and resolved as a repair or explicit rollback, not silently extended.

This is a repair to a user-visible queue stall, but its new credential/spend route warrants staged proof. Existing authorization covers Echo's test and development rollout once the spec itself has been reviewed and approved under repository rules; changing the 75% threshold or registered model would require a separate authority decision.

## Verification and acceptance

- Unit: 74/75 boundary; weekly-only legitimate window; missing/invalid window, NaN, negative, >100, future timestamp, 60-second live age boundary, wrong-family 0% versus correct-family >=75%, live unavailable or exhausted despite an older favorable pool/rollout value; active/disabled/warming/rate-limited/drifted/metadata-only homes; home removed or credential identity changed during read; hung app-server, SIGTERM-resistant child, cancellation during handoff and next-tick child-count bound; four-account case with three unreadable ahead of a healthy fourth; eligible partial wave with a hung sibling; deterministic tie, override binding, mode matrix, cap preservation and audit redaction. Missing config, missing primary provider, default provider, nature-plan mismatch, sibling and cross-framework tails must produce zero unauthorized invocations. Unsupported Astra must launch exactly one CLI process, use one reservation, and never fall back to another model in any mode or canary. A suspended reader followed by owner or authority handoff must produce zero calls, slots and notices; a handoff after reservation but before launch conditionally releases the unstarted slot. First/retry/canary quota refusal must not increment unusable or healing counters. Auxiliary provider calls retain their independent admission behavior.
- Integration: real HTTP triage tick with over-limit ambient login and eligible spare; verify the selected credential home, `codex` family, decision and audit. Repeat with all accounts ineligible, retry after a provider failure, a due canary, a slow selector exhausting tick budget, disabled live reader pausing without cached fallback, owner/authority handoff during suspended read, and owner change after reservation before launch, and `off`/`observe`/`on` modes.
- E2E: production `server.ts` construction and authenticated status, enabled route/tick on local pool accounts, actual spare-account decision while ambient is over limit, default dark mode, owner transfer and no local credential pause. Build and full unit, integration, E2E and aggregate suite must have zero failures at the exact head.
- Live: capture registered authority generation, per-call selected account ID, `codex` family/source/age, one successful decision, a later continuation, and safe pauses. The acceptance claim is that **each admitted attempt had a valid below-75% reading at admission**, not that shared provider usage can never cross 75% while a call runs. Measure executor merges separately; do not call the queue sustainable until high-priority merged-fix throughput exceeds arrivals over a measured window.
