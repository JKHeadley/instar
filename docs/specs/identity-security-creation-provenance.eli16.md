# A change you ask for is confirmed where you asked it, in plain words

Sometimes you ask your agent, in one conversation, to start a separate one, and later ask for something about it, like which model it should use. Until now, the new conversation only learned who its owner was from your first message typed there, so your model request was refused.

With this change, your agent shows you the exact change in the conversation you're talking in ("For the conversation 'Scaling': switch the model to Opus. Reply yes"), and your "yes" right there makes the change. No guessing from an old message: it's your live yes to exactly what you were shown. It only works for a conversation that's yours (not one someone else runs, and not a team member's), and that's checked again the moment you say yes. It's switched on for the developer's own agent first, and turned on for everyone only after it has proven itself.

When the agent starts a conversation for you, it also keeps a private note of which message it says it started from. The note is the agent's own claim: it isn't posted anywhere, and it doesn't count as your approval of anything. Recording your actual approval for starting a conversation comes in a later step.
