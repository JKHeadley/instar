# ELI16: opening your agent to the internet now needs you (SEC-163)

Your agent can be reached from outside your home in two ways: a public web address (the tunnel), and a network where it talks to other people's agents (the relay). Until now, anything holding the agent's key could switch either of those on, without asking anyone.

Now switching either on is treated like other risky changes. Nothing changes until you approve it on your approval card. That includes asking your agent to "join the agent network": it sends you the card and continues once you say yes. On a phone set up to approve, the card is one tap. Switching these off, or making your agent less visible, still needs nobody. Setting up a new agent works exactly as before, because setup writes its settings directly.
