# Internal Codex Checks Use Expiring Allowance First

Instar makes internal model calls for checks and other background reasoning. These calls already choose an enrolled local account rather than always using the computer's default login. However, their separate picker chose whichever account had the most quota left. That could spend a later-reset account while an earlier allowance went unused, even after conversation placement adopted the intended reset-first rule.

This repair applies the existing shared weekly-reset comparison to that internal picker. An eligible Codex account with a fresh, valid weekly deadline comes before an account whose deadline is later or unknown. Equal deadlines keep the existing order by available room. Accounts that are full on either applicable window, inactive, missing a local home, or affected by identity drift remain excluded. Other frameworks keep their existing ordering. The picker still returns the selected account and its actual local home; the provider passes that home to its child process. No credentials move and no model changes.

The repair depends on the earlier conversation-routing change and will follow its normal verification and merge gates. It does not restart running conversations, enable jobs, or change the disabled follow-through job. Targeted tests demonstrate the ordering defect before the fix, the real provider-to-child home after it, and reconstruction from the durable account pool after a restart. These tests are supporting evidence, not a complete verification or live-install claim. Reverting the small picker change restores the previous behavior without any data migration.
