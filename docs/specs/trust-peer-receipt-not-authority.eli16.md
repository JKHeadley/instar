# Another of my machines can no longer fake your approval (ELI16)

When the agent wants more freedom, for example to send email without asking, it asks you in your private chat and you reply "approve" with a code. The agent runs on several computers, and only one of them, the holder, is in charge of approvals.

If your reply landed on another computer, that computer wrote it down in a shared notebook and the holder acted on it. The holder could not tell whether you really wrote it or that computer made it up. So a stolen laptop still linked to the agent could fake your approval.

Now the holder only believes replies it receives from the chat itself. If your reply reaches a different computer, nothing changes and that computer tells you to send the same reply again in a minute. The holder then gets it first-hand.
