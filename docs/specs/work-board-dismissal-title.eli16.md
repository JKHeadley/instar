# A "not important" reason stays attached to the title the person closed (ELI16)

When you close a Work Board card as "not important", you give a reason. The agent reads those reasons before it raises a new card, so it does not keep bringing up things you already said no to.

The reason was shown next to the card's current title. If the card's title was changed after you closed it, your reason appeared to be about the new title, which you never saw or judged. Now the reason stays with the title you actually closed, and if the card was renamed later, the agent sees both titles and knows the reason was about the old one.

There is one more safeguard. When you tap to close a card from the console, your screen also sends a fingerprint of the title you were looking at. The agent uses that fingerprint to find exactly which title you closed, even if someone renamed the card a moment before your tap. If your app did not send the fingerprint, or it matches no title the card ever had, the agent marks that reason as "title not confirmed" instead of pretending to know.
