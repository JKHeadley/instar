# In plain words

Today, when someone on your team writes to your agent in Telegram, their message goes into the same conversation you use. That conversation can run commands, open files and use your accounts. So a team member, or anyone who gets hold of their Telegram, can steer your agent with your full power.

After this change, a team member gets a conversation of their own. The agent still answers them, but in that conversation it has no tools: it can talk and explain, but it can't run anything, open files or use your accounts, and it says so if asked. What they say is kept in their own private record. It doesn't go into your conversations, your agent's memory or its scheduled jobs. You still see their messages in Telegram, and if you want the agent to act on one, you bring it in yourself.

It turns on for development agents first. On everyone else's agents nothing changes yet; the owner decides when to switch it on.
