Status: reviewable experiment design; not executed, not published and not a complete-verification result. Chief requested batching on 2026-11-24 at 00:22 UTC. This experiment uses the existing single-candidate verifier and ordinary merge gate. It does not add authority to the verifier.

## Purpose and success

Run one complete gate for two compatible queued changes and merge the exact cumulative tree that was checked. Measure complete successful constituent changes delivered per hour and publication-to-final-verdict latency, alongside full gates per hour. A two-member train needs one full gate when green, rather than two independent full gates. A red train can cost more, so retain the work/time of failed batches and bisects in the denominator.

The initial target is one batch of two. Do not change ordinary priority or safety holds to make the experiment possible. The main repair and Chief fronted list finish first. The candidate pair is provisional until both become adjacent eligible choices in the live order. Never skip an earlier eligible change to assemble this pair.

## Metrics and stop conditions

Collect publication time, eligibility time, admission time, execution start/end, verdict, merge time, member count, all preparation/bisect machine-minutes, lane walls and all failure counts. Report every two hours using the same window as the independent baseline. Separately report gates/hour, successful delivered changes/hour and median/p95 publish-to-verdict. Include queue-held time rather than hiding it. Do not claim a multiplier until an actual successful exact-tree merge lands and end-to-end cost is measured.

Stop this experiment on ambiguous membership, unknown signed attribution, overlapping active run, changed input, unmet hold, inventory mismatch, lost diagnostics or inability to merge the tested tree. Rollback is to abandon the unmerged train and restore original candidates' train-created queue custody, with their original checks still required. No runtime or installed-agent state changes are introduced by this design.
