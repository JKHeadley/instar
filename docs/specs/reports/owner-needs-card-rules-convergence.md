# Needs You admission convergence

**External review degraded in every round.** The Codex reviewer was available and returned an error twice without findings. Gemini was unauthenticated and Grok was disabled. A separate Claude CLI attempt was refused by its usage limit. The Chief of Staff authorized relying on the internal rounds and Dashboard's independent read for the dark checkpoint under those conditions.

## Iteration Summary

1. Standards-Conformance Gate ran against 102 standards. Independent reviews covered security, adversarial behavior, integration, scalability, decision completeness and the owner experience. Findings included legacy merge-before-admission, audience leakage through counts, client compatibility, existing detail scope and the canary.
2. The conformance report informed the revised contract. The specification distinguishes audience proof from action availability, defines a named default and migration, preserves exact source identities and gives Home and the strips one authoritative read. Reviewers identified the last ambiguities in the separate detail scope, source retention, bounded scans and enrollment recovery.
3. The final internal delta reviewed the nine retained source records, bounded request budget, provenance and spoof resistance, action recovery after accepted-device enrollment, and first-slice scope. All three reviewers concurred with the design of the dark checkpoint. Dashboard independently confirmed the reviewed-copy and temporary-unavailability fixes and supplied a provisional client compatibility checkpoint.

## Experience fit

The current audited Work Board policy request stays pending until an eligible device can act. An authorized request with unavailable verification is visible as an incomplete check without an executable action. An owner who cannot enroll an eligible device is not treated as having an empty complete list. The test cases distinguish an exact accepted-device request, an undeclared agent alert, and an unrelated owner who is entitled to no row details.

## Left behind

Production provider installation, migration, bounded scan continuation, source delivery, real supported client proof and default-on activation remain release acceptance. The dark checkpoint contains the typed contract and local implementation evidence. It does not certify those production outcomes.
