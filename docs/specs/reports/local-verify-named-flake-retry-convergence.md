# Named flake retry review

Five cross-model rounds examined retry authority and usefulness. Early reviews found that a filtered pass could miss a sibling dependency, and that aggregate counts could hide a changed test identity or skipped assertion. The design now requires exact identities and outcomes for whole-file proof, and complete singleton inventory.

Later rounds clarified how Vitest attributes per-test hook failures, when quarantine takes effect, and how to measure recovery without attributing counterfactual savings. The final round returned minor issues. Its identity, rollout comparison and outcome-evidence clarifications are in the spec.

A separate source reviewer concurred after checking the outcome proof, strict unhandled-error report, signed cap and unreadable state. The regression tests cover the inventory change, swapped sibling outcomes and fail-closed quarantine. The focused set passed 267 cases, the quarantine test passed, and the production lifecycle check passed.
