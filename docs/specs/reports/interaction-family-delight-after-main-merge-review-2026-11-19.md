1. **Contradiction or duplicated obligation: none. Where the two articles meet, their duties add up.**
   - They share surfaces. *Delight the User* covers every screen a person sees, including approval and secret-drop pages and any dashboard page. *Documents Stand on Their Own* covers every document written for a decision or for review, approval asks included. An approval page or a document page on the dashboard is under both.
   - On those surfaces they ask different things. *Delight the User* asks how the screen looks and reads at a glance (visual hierarchy, one obvious next action, calm spacing and type). *Documents Stand on Their Own* asks whether the text can be followed without the session's context (each term defined at first use, nothing that relies on "as discussed"). A page can do both, and must.
   - One small wording point, not a conflict: *Delight the User*'s check against internal ids shown as content, and *Documents Stand on Their Own*'s allowance to give an internal name after describing what the thing does. On a document page the internal name is part of the document's own text, which *Documents Stand on Their Own* governs. The parent already allows identifiers "as de-emphasized support metadata". A later edit could say this in one line; nothing here needs it now.
   - *Documents Stand on Their Own*'s four-part approval ask keeps the authority statement server-authored under *Agent Proposes, Operator Approves*. That sits alongside that article; it does not override it. The overlap is intended.
   - No conflict with any other Interaction article.
2. **Change of meaning elsewhere: none.** *Documents Stand on Their Own* changes no other article. *Delight the User* changes how the parent is enforced (screen files reach the commit gate; a §6b answer must be the author's own words), not what the parent requires, and the parent's Applied through line says so. The wider reach of *Delight the User* (the website as well as operator surfaces) was already accepted in the 2026-11-18 review; the merge does not change it.

No field moved between articles and no block is doubled. The hierarchy check is clean: 47 relations, 26 parents.
