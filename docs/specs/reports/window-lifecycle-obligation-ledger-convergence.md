## ELI10 Overview

Window work currently depends too much on promises and scattered receipts. This design gives Echo a local, executable checklist whose items must have real evidence and a real executor. It blocks opening or closing a window when a required duty is missing, ownerless, unexecuted, or supported only by weak evidence.

## Original vs Converged

The converged design narrowed the native admission gate to structural local-store presence, required stronger evidence to be earned by binding or re-query, restricted waivers to exact operator-approved payloads, and split executor proof into pending, completed, and future-phase classes. It also made `open-unexecuted` an explicit blocking status after the 2026-08-28 missed-report incident.
