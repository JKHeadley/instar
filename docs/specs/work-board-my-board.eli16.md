# My board, in plain words

## What this is

The Work Board holds cards: one card for each thing the agent needs a person to decide, answer or sign off on. Today the board shows them in lanes by whose move it is: "Waiting on you", "With the agent", "Waiting on others", "Done". That works, but when a lot is waiting it is still one pile. Luna, another agent, runs a board for her team where each person gets "My board": only the cards that need that person, sorted by why they need them. Justin liked it and asked for ours to match.

## What changes

When the dashboard, the iPhone or the Mac asks for the board, the answer now also says which of the cards need this person and why, in five short lists, always in this order:

1. "Waiting for your approval": the agent wants to start, or says it finished and wants a sign-off.
2. "Waiting for your reply": the agent asked you a question.
3. "Your cards": cards you carry yourself, and anything else stuck on you, such as a card the agent could not pick up.
4. "Cards you were added to": for a team member, cards the owner shared with them.
5. "Held for later": cards someone put on hold.

Each card appears once, under the most pressing of these reasons. Inside each list, cards nobody has touched yet come first (marked "Not started"), then cards someone has started; newest first in each group. Each card already says whose turn it is, which the apps show as a colour.

## What stays the same

Nothing is saved or changed. The lists are worked out fresh each time the board is read, from the same cards the board already shows. Nobody sees a card they could not see before, and the counts never include hidden cards. Cards that are moving along with the agent stay in the old lanes below My board. An app talking to an older agent that does not send these lists just shows the lanes as before.

## What to decide

Nothing new needs a decision: Justin already approved matching Luna's board (17 November, "Please move forward with all of this"), and this changes no rule about who may approve what. It touches no constitution file.
