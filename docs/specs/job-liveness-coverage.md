# B1 — Show what the scheduler has covered

The Jobs surface can report that a job is enabled and give its most recent run without answering whether its schedule is being served. An enabled job with no recorded run could be new, paused, on another machine, overdue, or missing retained history. A manual run does not prove that a schedule ran. An independent server observer can show what the scheduler did or what the available evidence leaves unknown. This first increment of [Jobs, Projects, and Standing Responsibilities](jobs-projects-standing-responsibilities.md) uses [A1's separation of source and read projection](canonical-records-and-work-discovery.md). It introduces no new authority, writable job store, repair, or work-start gate.

## Which record to use

A **job** has a definition for repeatable scheduled execution. It exists independently of the outcomes a project owns. A **finite project** makes progress toward an accepted and verifiable finish, such as moving a service to another disk. A **standing project** owns a recurring responsibility with review windows and named responsibility for what happens after each review, such as Machine Stewardship. Its disk health checks are jobs. A substantial repair can become a finite project. Marking a review complete does not close the standing responsibility or establish that health is good. This taxonomy informs the status surface and existing guidance. Creating or migrating those project modes, job definitions, and stewardship defaults requires their own reviewed write design.

## Frontloaded decisions

1. The server owns a process-memory observation view. It evaluates effective definitions outside cron callbacks and reads existing evidence. It never starts, retries, pauses, or repairs a job.
2. Only a proven same-boot, locally eligible due opportunity can report no observed scheduler callback. Older and ambiguous intervals stay unknown. This increment does not reconstruct a history of missed runs.
3. Keep the existing local and pool Jobs APIs and authorization. Add coverage to local job rows and display it in Jobs. Old peers and unavailable machines remain explicitly unknown.

## Observation

Run a cancellable, non-reentrant watcher with the AgentServer job-health lifecycle, using the existing periodic job-supervision reconcile timer as the cadence precedent. It starts and stops with the server, outside JobScheduler.start and callback execution. A paused or wedged scheduler must leave the independent observer available when the server is healthy. If the observer stops ticking, an otherwise reachable server labels the observation stale or unavailable. The existing peer health path reports an unreachable server.

Read loaded effective definitions from JobScheduler.getJobs and use the scheduler's existing local-target resolution. Add a narrow read-only scheduler observation of the in-force definition generation, schedule/options, registration identity and eligibility. A generation changes when a reload, pause, enable, placement, holder transition, or other unverified transition invalidates continuity. Snapshot generation is verified before publication. Holders without continuous authoritative lease evidence, missing machine identity and unobserved eligibility are unknown. Today's placement does not establish yesterday's.

Use a paused, callback-free Croner instance with the scheduler's actual options. Keep the current upcoming and most recent witnessed opportunity while either affects the present status. Coalesce older observed opportunities under the same bounded health budget. Expose coverage when the evaluation is unable to keep pace. Arm a future due instant only after observing stable authorized eligibility and an entry counter sample strictly before the earliest scheduler callback for that instant under the same Croner semantics. If the available runtime cannot establish that bound, keep the opportunity unknown. Clear the witness on generation change, restart, host suspension, observation discontinuity, relevant clock correction or lease gap. Require a healthy post-due tick after the applicable existing scheduler grace. Reuse the existing JobScheduler scheduled-continuity tolerance based on schedule intervals where suitable, with its real options and timezone. It is separate from startup grace. If the selected shape cannot enroll that tolerance precisely, leave it unknown. Without an applicable existing tolerance, do not claim missed scheduled coverage. A bounded witnessed timestamp can still be shown as observation context without qualifying the interval as a verified overdue opportunity. Unsupported schedules, mismatched options, ambiguous daylight-saving evaluation and budget exhaustion cannot create a positive past verdict. The UTC due instant distinguishes repeated local times when the producer and observer use the same Croner semantics. A sparse schedule whose witnessed interval brackets the due instant and whose post-grace sample arrives before another eligible occurrence can yield a specific absence verdict from a stable counter. On frequent schedules or long grace windows, another occurrence can enter during that interval. A stable counter still proves no entry throughout a fully verified interval, while any advance is ambiguous without an exact occurrence ID. An arming or post-grace sample missed by the bounded cursor leaves that opportunity unknown.

Use already-produced Jobs activity display when available. B1 makes no additional per-job history query, ledger scan or file read. The current JobRunHistory limit is applied after collecting and sorting retained rows, and cannot bound a new observer tick. An existing skip or run row can contribute an exact handling witness only if a future enrolled bounded source supplies the native scheduled occurrence, definition generation, scope and trigger. This first increment leaves older ambiguous history unknown and shows existing recorded activity separately. It does not infer an execution result from timestamp proximity or manual activity.

Add a process-memory instrumentation counter at entry to the existing scheduled registration callback. Count the registration generation plus monotonic entry sequence, before callback work queues or can block. It writes observer evidence only, with no change to scheduling, run history, claims or execution. The independent observer records the generation and counter around the witnessed due interval. No change to the counter throughout continuous eligibility and after applicable grace proves no callback reached the registration entry, even though it cannot establish why. The three evidence outcomes are distinct. A stable counter after grace with continuous custody supports no entry by the sample time. A changed counter alone means handling is unknown for that exact occurrence. Also show the registration-level fact “A scheduled callback entered between these samples” as contextual information, with its observed interval and no credit to a specific due instant. Any later advance invalidates the current missing-callback label and becomes due with unknown handling until an exact event establishes more. Test a recurring schedule with delivery suppressed before callback entry while leaving registration and observer live, then a separate case holding work after entry. ## Jobs read and display

Add optional scheduleCoverage to existing authorized GET /jobs rows. Report machine-local scope, observation time, known due instant, state and controlled reason code. Bind cached evidence to the exact loaded definition and registration generation. Verify that generation and scope against the row again during serialization. A reload can turn old evidence unknown immediately, even before the next observer tick. The row's primary coverage describes the latest witnessed due opportunity until the observer sees a newer opportunity reach its own post-grace state or the previous evidence becomes unavailable. The next scheduled instant is an independent optional field and cannot hide the preceding unresolved opportunity. Include each displayed verdict's own due and original sample times. Keep separate watcher heartbeat and row sample deadlines. The former uses the existing job-health worker cadence and tolerated missed interval. The latter uses that cadence and the declared maximum accepted revisit interval for the scoped batch budget. Record and verify the calibration against the existing Jobs freshness objective before enabling. Once the newer due instant and elapsed grace are known, that newer observation controls the row even if its arming sample was missed and the verdict is unknown. The earlier dated result may remain as history; it cannot masquerade as today's finding. Verify this with both the watcher and row fresh. A read checks the watcher deadline and the specific row deadline independently, using monotonic local age within the observed server generation. A timely heartbeat or transport fetch cannot renew an older row. An overdue watcher or overdue row makes its coverage stale or unavailable with a controlled reason. Test the normal Jobs view across successive recurring opportunities. States distinguish upcoming, exactly observed handling, due with unknown handling, no callback observed, stale/unavailable, paused, disabled and not placed here. Recorded success or failure remains a recorded outcome. The Jobs screen shows one short explanation on the existing row or its standard detail, without another fetch.

Pool Jobs reuses the current merge of authenticated local Jobs responses. The current API concatenates machine-tagged rows. Preserve separate coverage for each authenticated machine and slug, even when the screen groups rows by slug. Bind a remote scheduleCoverage payload to a machine-verified transport or signature checked against the expected registered peer, requested scope and fresh single-use request nonce. Reject a same-peer valid signed replay from a previous request. The proof covers the scheduleCoverage bytes, original evidence sample, and source serialization times. A machine-signed catalog alone cannot attest the volatile observation. A shared bearer authenticates access, not the source machine. If the existing Jobs response cannot prove its machine-bound payload, retain the old Jobs row and report peer schedule coverage unknown. Never use a payload-supplied machineId alone. Re-evaluate every serialized machine row, including outer pool cache fast returns. Test a validly admitted response for a different registered machine and the same slug on both peers. Do not emit an aggregate fleet health or missed-run count. Retain source due and sample times. The remote verdict is explicitly “as of source serialization,” with that separate verification time. A later local callback can withdraw the local label before the pool cache refresh without falsifying the earlier dated remote verdict. Test a late entry after the pool response was cached and before it expires. Peer responses carry their source freshness verdict and remaining freshness at response time; the receiver ages it by bounded transport/cache elapsed time and existing peer staleness, without renewing it during a merge. ## Resource and failure behavior

Run bounded non-overlapping evaluation batches within the existing health tick and resource budgets. A health owner may use additional capacity within the same measured ceiling. Spread work over ticks using the existing job-health budget and keep a bounded observation per effective job plus a cursor for unfinished work. Skip overlap. Read existing in-memory scheduler and placement observations through bounded interfaces. A measured authorized and constant-cost native direct lookup can be enrolled when it exists. If an optional exact forensic provider is enrolled later, it has a native occurrence key and bounded direct lookup with explicit unread coverage. Neither observer tick nor API request scans any ledger in this increment. Missing retained spans cannot certify a negative. On capped or failed evaluation, return stale/unknown for affected rows. Use the existing health worker backoff and breaker for repeated failures. Report sustained degradation once in operational health evidence, without a new notice or job action. Measure tick age, examined rows, exact-match/unknown reasons and tick and HTTP duration in existing health instrumentation without creating a forensic ledger.

B1 writes only local volatile observation data. Name the Structure beats Willpower and Verify the State, Not Its Symbol contracts at the callback-entry observation and link the code location from the A1 census or owning feature index after wiring. Job definitions, scheduler execution state, claim, dispatch, notification, run history, skip ledger and forensic records retain existing writers. It never calls the scheduler's continuity alert path. A rollback removes the additive projection and timer and requires no state migration.

## Decision points touched

- Eligibility and exact evidence join — invariant; basis: enumerable-domain; evidence: generation, authorized placement, native occurrence and trigger establish schedule membership. Similar slug and time cannot establish identity.
- Due and observed progress — invariant; basis: enumerable-domain; evidence: the witnessed instant, applicable existing grace and continuous registered-Cron observation distinguish exact progress from an unproven past event.
- Evidence visibility — invariant; basis: irreversible-consequence; evidence: an unauthorized response can expose private job information that cannot be recalled. Reuse Jobs authorization and a controlled field projection.
- Observer capacity and fallback — judgment-candidate. The existing server job-health policy allocates read work within measured budgets. Allowed outcomes are refresh, postpone observation for some rows or serve stale/unavailable. On weak evidence fall back to unknown. This does not affect execution.

## Multi-machine posture

The process-memory view describes an actual local boot, definition generation and authorized placement. Use the existing authenticated pool Jobs read for a joined projection. Peers do not treat the view as durable history or infer actual execution on a disconnected machine. A restart rebuilds from present local state and leaves earlier opportunities unknown.

## Maturation plan

- **test-agent-live:** Boot production wiring in an isolated agent. Witness an ordinary local schedule before its due instant. Suppress a production callback before entry while leaving its registration and observation server healthy. In a separate run, allow entry and hold subsequent work. Verify the normal Jobs screen reports due without an observed callback where exact evidence supports it. A callback known only to have incremented the counter reports due with unknown handling, with recorded activity shown separately. Reserve exact handling for a native occurrence fixture. Release the delayed callback after publication and verify a new Jobs read cannot still call it missing. Repeat for a manual run, pause, restart, stale peer and missing history. Keep the watcher heartbeat advancing through a backlog while one untouched row exceeds its sample deadline. Verify that row alone goes stale and keeps its original sample time. Present the same slug from two authenticated machines with different source-local verdicts, then independently age one peer cache. Demonstrate that the observer produces no launch, mutation or notice.
- **dev-agent-live:** Compare the Jobs view with real effective schedules and retained evidence on the authorized development agent. Before the canary record the existing Jobs route latency budget and the named fixture classes that must be observable, including real same-generation post-grace silence and counter advance. Check those classes in the development harness, enumerate supported real schedules with known and unknown evidence, and hold fleet default if the positive classes are absent or measurements exceed the existing Jobs and health cadence budgets. Check that absence of last-run metadata never becomes a failure.
- **fleet:** Enable the additive read-only view after test and development evidence, recurring model maturation review and its two independent readings authorizing fleet default. An older peer keeps its existing Jobs row and shows unknown coverage.
- **graduation criterion:** Unit, HTTP integration, and production lifecycle prove Croner and DST behavior, generation change, stable witnessed stall, callback entry without false stall (including delayed A with suppressed B), generation replacement and per-row freshness at route read, current Jobs permissions, separate same-slug pool observations and independent peer aging, and bounded read cost. Record the normal Jobs view and the absence of side effects in the held-callback case.
