# In plain words

Your agent can read messages from the Messages app on your Mac and answer people on your contact list. Real iMessages are tied to the sender's Apple account, so they really come from that person. Ordinary text messages (SMS) aren't. Anyone can send a text that shows one of your contacts' numbers. Today the agent treats such a text as if your contact sent it, and starts working on it.

After this change, the agent only acts on real iMessages. A text message that claims to be from one of your contacts is left alone: no session starts, nothing is answered, and its words aren't passed to the agent's conversations. You still have it in your Messages app.

Once a day the agent tells you how many texts it set aside, and which numbers they claimed to come from. If one of them was you (your phone sometimes sends a text instead of an iMessage when iMessage is down), send it again as an iMessage.

People who can only text, like friends with Android phones, couldn't really chat with the agent before either: its replies only go out as iMessages. A way for you to accept specific numbers over SMS will come later, from your own phone.
