# Bringing your Claude and ChatGPT history into Instar (ELI16)

## The problem

People who switch to Instar leave years of chats, projects and saved memories behind in the Claude and ChatGPT apps. If those stay behind, people keep going back to the old app to look something up, and the switch never really happens.

## What this builds

You download your data from the old app (both apps have an export button) and drop the file into Instar. Before anything is saved, Instar shows a plain summary, something like "1,240 chats, 6 projects, 12 things that looked like passwords were removed", and you confirm it.

Your old chats appear under "Imported", with their original dates, and the agent's search can find them. Your old projects show up with their instructions and file names. The agent suggests facts it learned about you, each one next to where it came from, and stores only the ones you tick.

## How it stays safe

The file is read on your computer only, and nothing is uploaded. Passwords and keys are removed. The old chats are history, never orders: the agent only sees them as quoted text when it searches, and it never loads them on its own.

For now, imported chats stay on the computer that imported them. If you want to carry on an old chat, Instar starts a new one with a short quote from the end of the old one.
