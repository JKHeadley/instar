# Jobs, projects, and standing responsibilities

Instar needs jobs for scheduled checks and projects for work with an outcome. A job can currently exist in the old job file and a schedule manifest. The scheduler chooses which definition wins while some updates still edit the old file. A project can enter the project registry or the Chief of Staff roster through separate writers. The foundation makes each record editable in one place and discoverable from existing evidence. The separate gate must prove that a new automated action cannot run twice or reveal private work. These are [A1, the authority and read-only discovery foundation](canonical-records-and-work-discovery.md) and [A2, the start-and-dispatch gate](check-existing-work-before-dispatch.md). The dependent migrations require the authority protocol to clear its own review. The independent read-only inventory and job-health increments may be tested without moving write authority. Machine Stewardship gets a permanent home for an enduring responsibility, with ordinary jobs for the checks and finite work for substantial fixes.

## Constitutional fit

**Cross-Store Coherence Is an Invariant** governs authority and migration. **Structure beats Willpower** governs independent job liveness and project continuation. **Cross-Machine Coherence** governs placement, scope and transfer. **Signal vs. Authority** and **Judgment Within Floors** separate observations from permission to act. **Self-Heal Before Notify** governs escalation. **Attributed Statements Trace to Their Source** governs findings sent to people. **An Instar Agent Always Serves a Team** governs accountable people, membership and source/destination access. This spec applies existing standards.

## Frontloaded decisions

1. A finite project has a verifiable finish. A standing project holds a recurring responsibility and uses verified review windows. A job owns a repeatable execution definition. Job attempts and findings retain separate identities under their owners.
2. Project authority belongs to the InitiativeTracker project service on a single adapter per installed authority epoch. Use TaskFlow on the target when available. Chief of Staff owns supervision cadence, evidence and notes, and joins the project by immutable ID. Its binding does not create a second roster.
3. Job definitions belong to the canonical per-slug schedule manifest service. Extend the existing definition contract to preserve script, skill, prompt and agentmd execution types, placement and effective behavior. Compatibility writes route to it after fenced cutover.
4. Each job has an accountable human principal, a work owner and a finding audience. An authorized principal may assign the job to a finite or standing project or commitment. Finding visibility is the intersection of source and destination policy. Unknown legacy ownership stays unresolved with existing execution and access preserved until decided.
5. Stage independent health evidence and safe job migration first, then bounded turn-end continuation and one project roster, then standing mode, findings, review and expiry, and role-based defaults. Stage write authority separately for jobs and projects.

## Choosing a record

| Record | Use it for | Key state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job | Repeatable action whose run can be understood on its own | Definition, schedule, executor, placement, owner, cadence and expiry |
| Finite project | Outcome with acceptance evidence and an accountable person | Goal, status, tasks, barriers and completion evidence |
| Standing project | Recurring responsibility with reviews and follow-up that needs judgment | Project ID, linked jobs, review evidence, findings and follow-up |
| Job finding | One operational event or a continuing condition | Job revision and scope, run or condition key, evidence, disposition and audience |

A disk check is a job. A finding for a full drive belongs with the Machine Stewardship responsibility and the authorized owner of that machine. A substantial repair can become a finite project linked to that standing responsibility. The standing project persists between reviews without keeping a session alive all the time.

For the initial migration map agentmd to its stable body/lock reference, script to canonical deployed runner path and arguments, skill to the authorized skill handler and input, and prompt to the existing legacy prompt runner and payload. A coverage record never grants an execution lease. Bind any privilege-expanding change to the existing activation authority and the exact effective executable identity, trusted options, placement, audience and revision. A definition editor, signed import, restore or installer cannot grant new execution rights. At dispatch, recheck exact resolved script/skill/body, activation, current placement and current holder before acquisition and again at the side-effect boundary. The runner must consume immutable authorized bytes or an already verified snapshot/handle. A mutable path re-resolution between check and execution is not sufficient. If the executor cannot bind the actual artifact, preserve its proven legacy authorization without promoting that path. Test replacing a script or skill target in the verification-to-launch window. A changed implementation behind the same reference needs the owning policy to authorize the new resolved identity. Carry forward proven legacy authority for the unchanged effective job; a previously inert or untrusted input remains inert.

Inventory legacy and schedule inputs by identity and effective revision. Snapshot originals, body references, disabled/enabled state, overrides, imported/pinned state, and applicable defaults. Produce a dry-run mapping for each known slug. Keep every distinct non-effective variant as archived evidence. A conflicting slug stays on the previously validated effective variant under the live legacy epoch. The first cutover uses one kind-wide epoch because current legacy writers and rollback replace or remove whole shared inputs and old startup builds eligibility for the entire file. A per-job transition would first require all surviving readers and writers to honor an isolated authority namespace and the rollback to leave other migrated jobs intact. A later independently reviewed isolated route may use a narrower epoch. An unresolved material conflict holds that promotion while inventory and health work continue; there is no per-slug split authority fallback. Keep the presently validated effective choice until the owner approves how it maps. Fence legacy PATCH, per-slug save and enable/disable/override routes, built-in installers, legacy migration cancel/abandon, portable import, both the stopped-agent CLI and live HTTP portable rollback, and configured state writers. After canonical publication the two portable rollback routes delegate to the current definition owner or refuse before quarantining files. Test both against a proven import, retaining alias, payload and enabled state consistency. The existing legacy PATCH route is a compatibility adapter after publication. Quiesce or exclude older binaries that cannot execute the new epoch contract. They cannot run a cached scheduled callback, accept a claim, or write after the epoch changes. Capture a final high-water only after both mutation and execution fences hold. Drain late writes, compare complete effective definitions, and publish one authority pointer under Spec A. Keep manifest-only jobs. Canceling must preserve both originals and target-only records. A post-cutover compatibility write uses the canonical version check. The reconciler understands which executors need separate Markdown and distinguishes archival residue from competing writers. Report counts for effective, placed, intentional archive and verified overlap.

Portability/export remains governed by the existing authored source policy and signed definition transport. Credential and hardware access stay scoped. A backup, export or observation cannot activate a job. Cron and holder catch-up share the existing occurrence claim and current policy. The execution identity is canonical job ID plus placement scope and native occurrence. For a holder job the scope is the lease domain; for a per-machine job it includes eligible machine ID. Definition and authority revisions are independently checked provenance. Editing a definition never creates a second claim for the same due instant. If placement or holder domain changes, fence and drain the old scope and record which outstanding occurrences represent the same logical obligation. Carry their accepted/consumed markers into the new equivalence mapping, including holder versus per-machine transitions. Independent per-machine obligations remain separate. Starting a genuinely new schedule requires an authorized boundary and cannot cause catch-up to replay a prior accepted effect. A distinct additional trigger requires its own explicitly authorized occurrence. Pause, expiry and revocation fence future claims and are rechecked before side effects. For in-flight external effects the recorded result or unknown-outcome reconciliation controls retries. Restoration cannot silently enable an imported or retired job.

## Independent job liveness

The existing server scheduler-health owner runs an independent due-window evaluator on the health cadence, outside job execution. Read the effective definition and its enable/disable and authority timeline, timezone and scheduling semantics including daylight-saving folds, current placement and retained run evidence. Track both the latest observed interval and the contiguous completeness watermark per placement scope and authority generation. Record unresolved or permanently unavailable intervals separately. Later eligible intervals keep their verdicts across restart and cannot close an older gap by advancing observation. Reconcile retained gaps when new authoritative history arrives. Reuse the existing occurrence and lease grace rules. Schedule on the existing health cadence. Allocate lookback, batch size and catch-up over separate capacity measured against current health and request SLOs, with reserved current-interval checks. Keep a cursor and lag indication, and prioritize current checks over historical reconstruction. A history gap, unverified ownership interval, sleeping placement or pre-enable time cannot become a missed opportunity. When no attempt is emitted during a stalled scheduler, the independent evaluator can derive an overdue eligible occurrence with the established scope, revision and schedule basis.

Join scheduled opportunities to attempt, skip, verified deferral and result. Manual attempts are visible and cannot satisfy an unrelated schedule. Separate attempt acknowledged, result recorded, result independently verified, and verification unavailable. Expose freshness, evidence and explanation through jobs and health. The existing server or peer health watchdog observes scheduler and evaluator watermarks independently and can show stale evaluator evidence. Recovery rechecks placement, claims and live process evidence before any approved action. Reuse existing bounded attempts, wall time, backoff, flapping breaker and deduplicated owner-routed attention. An unavailable machine raises the appropriate health status, not an invented run failure. Retire monitoring jobs only after a real stall replay meets the same scope and coverage. Test long retention gaps, time-zone and definition changes, manual runs, one job warming during a holder switch, no emitted scheduler events and load large enough to exercise batching.
## Canonical project roster

The project service owns the stable ID, mode, principal, goal, phase, state, completion checklist revision, per-item verification/delegation policy and accepted evidence. Migrate the checklist and each delegation attestation together. Group all verified bindings by canonical project ID first. For each conversation, fold its authorized lifecycle up to the final fence. Merge compatible check and closure evidence for the same checklist revision. When two valid bindings disagree on current goal, closure state, checklist or delegated authority at that revision, retain both and hold affected cutover for an owner decision. For an unambiguous paired record, replay current authorized lifecycle from its winning Chief of Staff closure binding. Its binding is the prior closure authority even when its status mirror lagged. A closure invalidated by a subsequent checklist revision stays superseded. Test a valid closure with failed mirror and a stale closure after checklist edit. Owner confirmation or delegated evidence is bound to exact canonical checklist revision and item. A later edit supersedes the earlier item head and makes stale confirmation a conflict. Test an edit in one linked conversation racing confirmation in another. Chief of Staff holds a separate supervision record keyed by its own occurrence and references the canonical ID. It may supervise a conversation without turning it into a project. A project-only row has a valid project ID and may have no supervision. A verified paired row keeps the project's ID and separate supervision record. A conversation-only binding keeps a stable supervision ID and native conversation key; clients receive an explicit type and no invented project ID. A formerly conflicting legacy pair is retained until proof resolves it. PlanDoc creation uses the project service; existing Chief of Staff bind only writes a supervision relation, and a tap with project effects invokes the canonical checklist revision. The Work surface references this same roster. Conversation-only supervision is labeled as such. The owning Project service handles goal and status updates. Project, Work and Chief of Staff views present authorized subsets of the same canonical project roster with a clear inclusion rule. A project can have multiple role-labeled conversation links. Mere conversations, cards and auto-registered specs cannot silently create projects.

Preview exact ID and verified conversation joins across current TaskFlow/legacy and Chief of Staff records. Ambiguous or inconsistent matches go to the accountable owner for confirmation with both originals retained. A title is insufficient. Under the final writer fence, drain old bindings and legacy project mutations to the verified high-water. Move all compatible clients to the single published authority epoch. Retain the previous verified view under uncertain publication and refuse stale mutations. Replaying old supervision cannot insert or revise a project after cutover. TaskFlow SQLite materializes the project owner's locally readable revision. Before cutover, enroll signed complete project revisions with canonical goal, mode, current and future review obligation revisions, status, checklist and delegations, ownership/audience, linked authorized references, and effect result records in the existing replicated-record carrier under a distinct project-authority contract, using the current holder and authorized peer custody. A pooled holder acknowledges a shared project mutation only after the full signed accepted revision and its durable commit certificate are persisted by every installed agent member authorized for the project scope, as recorded in a current authenticated membership epoch. The certificate binds the canonical epoch, prior accepted head, exact new revision/hash and signed per-member durability acknowledgments. Members hold the verified record and certificate locally. A verified all-member certificate can be reconstructed from the same durable acknowledgments; a later successor must verify the current membership and accepted head, and is unable to infer acceptance from a timestamp, an uncertified payload or a local append. This includes an independent failure domain when the set has more than one member. A member that is removed or excluded under an independently authorized membership transition cannot retain stale writer or exposure authority. While this proof is absent, continue to serve the last accepted revision with its truthful coverage. A local staged write uses current recoverable retry identity and cannot report shared success. Shared current state is not claimed for a pooled installation until the required member set attests. A member with missing replication requests authorized current state through the owner and labels an unavailable scope if disconnected. Offline tolerance is not treated as proof of current shared acceptance. A local flush without that proof is explicitly unavailable for a durable success claim and retries the same mutation identity idempotently. A single-machine installation reports its actual local durability and no independent copy. A new holder must verify fenced custody through the authoritative head before accepting new writes. A member lacking the required recovery coverage reads through an authorized owning source when reachable; otherwise its verified stale/unavailable view carries a visible gap. Check crashes after local flush, after independent persistence before acknowledgement, and immediately after confirmed success. A missing proof serves the last authorized verified projection as stale or unavailable and refuses mutations. Simulate loss of the holder immediately after it acknowledges a revision; the successor recovers that exact durable revision. An existing Chief of Staff binding is migration evidence, not a second writable status store.

## Turn-end continuation

Subscribe the project service's continuation consumer to the existing verified framework turn-end events, including authenticated Codex task completion, Claude Stop/hook event and equivalent normal lifecycle routes for other supported adapters. Bind each signal to canonical project, run, turn and current step revision. Each adapter must expose a replayable event cursor or reconcile durable run history by run/turn IDs. Advance the applied cursor only when the continuation consumer records either a terminal/parked disposition or a durable pending dispatch in the same commit as the reservation. The pending dispatch remains discoverable by its owner after restart until claimed, verified or parked with a reason. Test a crash after cursor advancement and before any outbound claim. A crash before the consumer's first write must recover the event once. An adapter without verified delivery, restart identity and replay stays advisory until test-agent proof. Graduation covers each supported engine's normal path. Approval/blocked state comes from the canonical project service. Before dispatch, atomically reserve the eligible logical step under the project owner's revision and lease. A second linked conversation, manual action or supervisory check competing for the same effect reads the recorded reservation/result. A Stop event is not progress. Use committed step evidence, a verified external state change or the recorded authorized next-action disposition.

The existing lifecycle arbiter can continue, accept completion evidence, park behind an explicit barrier, or request a needed decision. A finite project can finish on its current goal/checklist revision. For standing mode, the same seam closes the verified window ID and leaves the project active with its next review obligation. Only a deliberate authorized retirement can close the standing project. At the project owner's commit, recheck current initiating authority for either finite closure or standing window closure. A Chief of Staff–started run can finish collecting evidence after its operator lease expires, but the expired lease cannot authorize a new closure. Persist evidence with pending review until an independent current owner/lease approves. Test the final evidence arriving after lease expiry. Test two successive completed windows through the existing supervision path. A bounded workflow can include several internal checks in one admitted effect. Each externally observable action stays individually identified and verified. Apply the existing supervision wall/usage budgets, named no-progress and backoff policies appropriate to the workload, and a breaker. Persist parked barrier, wake evidence, next review and progress with the owner. Recheck current lease, project revision and barrier at the side-effect boundary. Carry the original run's initiation authority, project reservation and any Chief of Staff operator lease into the same conversation spawn/inject admission. An expired Chief of Staff authorizing lease permits the already accepted bounded run to finish as established policy allows, but requires current restart authority for a new run. Another linked conversation cannot manufacture it. Record a fenced outbound claim/result. New automatic external actions and new governed work initiation remain advisory until A2's authorization and safe dispatch fences clear review, unless the named existing owner ingress already proves the exact outcome and idempotency contract. A1 registry/discovery approval cannot activate A2 behavior. An unknown acknowledgement uses existing effect reconciliation and cannot be treated as proof of no execution. Restart/handoff replays durable pending signals by effect identity. Chief of Staff observes the same parked state and cannot supply another automatic nudge for a reserved action. Tracking-only projects stay passive until an authorized start. Include ordinary chat, stale approval, repeated no-progress, lost acknowledgement and a real converted/resumed Codex run in lifecycle evidence.
## Standing responsibilities, finding ownership and expiry

Standing mode attaches ordinary project identity to an enduring responsibility with defined review windows and checkable conditions. The owning service records review state per interval, job links, due work and evidence. Freeze the eligible machine, volume and job obligation set per review window under the owner revision. A justified change records a new obligation revision or takes effect next window. Removing an unavailable machine cannot turn the same unresolved window healthy. Track two outcomes per review window. Review completion records whether due checks were observed and findings assigned or scheduled for review. Condition health derives from current measurements and per-check thresholds. A full drive remains degraded even when triage is complete. Only fresh verified recovery can change its health. Missing required measurements stay unknown. Show unknown with cause for a sleeping machine or unavailable evidence. Owners can pause or retire the responsibility without losing history.

The owning human principal sets or delegates finding access under the existing authority. The work owner takes operational follow-up. A local system job defaults to the configured owner, owner-only finding access, and the machine scope it actually runs on. A team job needs an authorized principal and explicit audience before audience expansion. Narrower source or destination policy wins. Raw diagnostics and private source data stay under their own policies. A legacy job with unresolved ownership retains existing authorized behavior. New summaries stay on the narrowest already authorized surface and the designated steward resolves ownership through the existing attention path. Reauthorize each finding render, aggregate, fan-out, card link or update, and delayed notice against current role and source/destination epochs. Reassignment, move and revision change do not copy private evidence forward. A shared card loses inaccessible derived evidence and can keep its separately authorized content and a generic source-unavailable state without exposing the old finding. Test owner, ordinary member and guest on the same machine and scope.

A finding carries canonical job, revision, scope and verified occurrence or continuing condition key. Repeated observations for an open condition update the same episode and linked card. Order only verified observations within the same originating scope and authoritative evaluator generation. The owner's acceptance revision compares condition head and native source sequence/freshness. An older result cannot close a newer finding. On a holder transfer, the successor adopts the verified condition head, then takes a fresh observation explicitly referencing that head. Its scoped agreement can close the episode. Delayed results from the former holder cannot update or reopen the condition. Confirmed healthy evidence later than the current triggering observation closes that episode. A recurrence starts a new occurrence against the same stable condition key and links the historical episode. Only reuse a card if its current owner/audience and explicit reopen policy still allow it. Carry no prior suppression into a recurrence without a current policy decision. A changed definition that watches the same condition does not create a second active episode; a changed monitored scope cannot overwrite evidence for the old condition. Use the existing source-verified Work Board card ingress with exact origin idempotency if it already supports the finding and audience. Otherwise retain a linked candidate in the finding's authorized view. Any new automatic governed card ingress waits for separately reviewed A2 admission. The read-only discovery foundation is not an execution permission. Proposed action remains evidence for existing authorities. Cleanup, spending, access changes and external messages require their ordinary authorization. A resolved condition closes on verified evidence. Suppression carries actor, reason and review condition.

Definition expiry is optional. Closing a finite project schedules an owner review of its dependent jobs. An explicit expiry moves the definition to no new claims only when that policy was owner-authorized. Already admitted work drains or is canceled by the existing safe cancellation policy with state and evidence recorded. Restore an expired or retired definition as disabled unless a newly authorized revision explicitly reactivates it. Pause, expiry and retirement each have actor, reason and evidence. The owning adapter checks pending claims and safe disposition before changing execution eligibility. A standing job uses review cadence without a fabricated end date. Do not silently re-enable, delete or reassign a job.

## Defaults

Initialize one Machine Stewardship standing responsibility for every new agent. Use the configured human owner, owner-only findings by default, and existing applicable system checks for capacity, backup freshness, machine health and eligible volumes including Windows when present. Expose coverage for absent checks rather than inventing a passing finding. Use a stable role/agent seed identity and idempotent canonical creation. Existing agents receive a preview in the Work/app or authorized CLI home, even when Telegram is unavailable. A safe exact verified existing match is adopted idempotently without duplicating a project or job; a verified configured owner receives one idempotent responsibility with the same review cadence as a new install if there is no match and attaching existing evidence needs no execution or access change. It begins evaluating the existing health evidence. Missing sources stay unknown rather than enabling new jobs. A change in authority, enabled schedule or audience still needs its ordinary approval. Ambiguous candidates, missing verified owner, or any schedule, audience or execution change remain pending in the existing setup/attention flow requiring its owner. Expose the pending stewardship state in Work so older installations can become covered rather than waiting silently. A conversation can link later. Observation-only checks remain available when owner policy pauses repair. Prefer applicable independent health services over adding a cron fallback; any added fallback runs only under explicit current authority.

Stewardship reviews follow the existing machine-health reporting cadence, linked to scheduler health, DiskPressure/capacity evidence, backup health and eligible volume data. Use the configured owner review cadence where one already exists and expose the current basis. This first enrollment auto-seeds only Machine Stewardship. For Chief of Staff, promises, self-health, and memory/learning, show existing role and capability mappings as preview suggestions and link verified existing duties. A separate authorized configuration activates any additional standing default. No default finite project. Show effective changes, owners and audiences in the incoming migration preview.

## Multi-machine posture

Job identity, definition revision metadata, placement and authorization pointers use the agent-scoped canonical definition metadata and existing authenticated replicated-record carrier. The per-slug manifest is the materialized execution definition. Source-bound or nonportable payload remains with its authorized source machine and backup policy; replication of metadata or a coverage snapshot grants no executable copy or new rights. Portable executable transfer still uses JobPortableSync's explicit export/recipient authorization, validated revision and import journal. An owner transfer with only metadata reports definition payload unavailable and refuses a fresh claim. Stage and validate the exact payload on its authorized source before publishing canonical metadata. Metadata names the content digest, definition revision, source and custody/authority epoch. Scheduler and compatibility readers use only a materialization matching those fields. Absent or stale bytes produce unavailable, never a fallback execution. Reconcile idempotently after a crash on either side of publication and test a stale manifest under the same live slug. Per-machine definitions retain independently scoped revision provenance. The owner lease gates version checkout, effective edits and execution. Projects use their registered project-authority carrier. Definition placement is separate from runtime provenance and effect claims. Pooled status joins authorized intended schedules with actual machine observations. When a machine is unavailable, expose unknown coverage. Catch-up follows authorized lease and occurrence policy. An obsolete authority or audience epoch cannot mutate or expose the current record. Do not graduate a project adapter that requires a live peer for an already acknowledged revision on an installed eligible member. Explicitly unauthorized members receive no protected state. Be explicit about an offline current member blocking shared acceptance and which local prior revision remains recoverable. Test handoff, fallback and restore with retained old-writer state.

## Decision points touched

- Job and project authority epoch and legacy mutation routing — invariant; basis: irreversible-consequence; evidence: accepting a mutation after retirement can lose the owner’s job or project revision. Check current owner and epoch transactionally.
- Scheduler occurrence and compatibility identity — invariant; basis: enumerable-domain; evidence: verified definition, scope and native schedule occurrence map to one claim. Use existing distinct manual and intentional retry identities. Definition revision is checked metadata and cannot grant another scheduled claim.
- Finding access — invariant; basis: irreversible-consequence; evidence: an unauthorized shared finding cannot be recalled. Check current source and destination audience before exposure or routing.
- Alias, ownership and expiry resolution — judgment-candidate. The owning principal or authorized service uses source evidence to adopt a verified match, retain a separate item, reassign within authority, or hold with a reason. Conservative fallback retains originals and resolves ambiguity before risky mutation.
- Health and continuation decisions — judgment-candidate. The existing lifecycle/health arbiter uses actual occurrence evidence and may continue, repair, park or route the existing escalation after exhausted retries. On uncertainty keep state intact, report the coverage gap and preserve effect dedupe.
- Standing health and defaults enrollment — judgment-candidate. The scoped owner and existing role policy use verified review evidence, compatible seed matches and audience checks. Preserve unresolved existing records and show the preview for uncertain conversion.
