# Keep the Useful Next Step Visible on Ideas

The Ideas page shows suggestions from other projects, grouped by the server's review status. The running dashboard had one suggestion worth planning and150 suggestions that were not ranked yet. Every unranked suggestion was expanded, including its explanation and three decision buttons. That buried the project list beneath many screens of unfinished review material, especially on a phone.

This change puts only the unranked group inside one native disclosure labelled with its count. It starts closed. Ready-to-act, worth-planning and the other existing groups keep their previous presentation. Opening the unranked group reveals every original suggestion, explanation, warning and action. Nothing is deleted, reclassified or automatically decided. The existing plan, PIN and commit flow still owns every decision.

The disclosure is an information hierarchy choice based on the server's explicit band. It never guesses importance from a title or description. The group's explanation remains available inside it. Opening it holds the dashboard's existing automatic refresh guard, preserving reading position until the person closes it. The project list is reachable immediately after the closed group.

Focused tests cover no unranked suggestions, one suggestion and the live-size150suggestion case, including all450existing actions and safe text rendering. Browser checks at phone and laptop widths confirm that the closed group stays readable, keyboard opening works and the project list is reachable without reading every unfinished suggestion. Normal complete verification and release remain separate from these development checks.
