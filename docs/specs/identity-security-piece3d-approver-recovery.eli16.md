# If you lose every phone that can approve (piece 3d), in plain words

Only a phone that can already approve can let a new one in. So if you lose every one of them, you need a way back that a thief can't use.

Here it is. On your new phone you ask to approve, and it shows a six-digit code. You type `/approver_reset` and that code into your private chat with the agent, and tap Start. Then nothing happens for 72 hours. During that time the agent messages you at the start, halfway and in the last hour.

If any phone that can still approve does anything at all in that time, the recovery stops. So does a Stop from one of them, or your own Stop tap in the chat. After 72 hours you tap Finish on the new phone and type a fresh code from the chat. The new phone then signs itself into the ownership record. Every one of your computers checks for itself that the wait really happened and that nobody approved anything since it started.

What it can't stop: someone who has taken over your Telegram account, if none of your approving devices does anything for three days. The approved design accepts that.
