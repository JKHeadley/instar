# PROPOSED STANDARD — not yet ratified

**Status:** PROPOSAL. Agent proposes, operator ratifies. Deliberately NOT written into the registry.
**Proposed by:** Echo — Pathway, 2026-08-05, **derived from my own failures across six gate rounds.**
**Proposed family:** The Root (candidate sibling to *Structure beats Willpower*).

---

### Remove What Demands Attention. Do Not Supply More Attention.

**Rule.** When a defect recurs despite genuine effort to prevent it, the correct response is to
**eliminate the structure that requires the effort** — not to apply more effort, more care, or more
review. A structure that must be held in agreement by attention will drift, and the drift rate is a
property of the structure, not of the person attending to it. **Duplication is the canonical instance:
four copies of a fact cannot be kept consistent by diligence; three of them must be deleted.**

**In practice.** The trigger is **recurrence under effort** — a defect that returns *after* you tried to
prevent it. That is the signal that attention is the wrong instrument. Three moves, in order: **(A)
Count the copies.** If a fact, number, list, or claim exists in more than one place, the extras are the
defect. **(B) Delete, do not synchronise.** Leave one authoritative source and have every other location
*point* to it — a pointer cannot disagree. **(C) If it cannot be deduplicated, make the wrong state
unrepresentable** rather than forbidden: delete the value from the type union, remove the fallback,
mint the capability narrowly. A prohibition is attention with extra steps.

**Earned from.** **Six adversarial passes over one plan document, five of which I failed the same way.**
Rounds 1–5 each returned INCOHERENT, and each failure was me *fixing one site and leaving another*: a
duplicate node id; then my repair of it recreating the identical collision one number over; a factual
claim corrected in three places and left standing in two; a count reading 13 in the header and 14 in the
body. **I was trying hard at every round.** Round six passed only after I stopped synchronising the
count and **deleted three of its four copies**, leaving one source and pointers.

**The distinguishing evidence, and the reason this is a standard rather than a lesson:** in the same
window, ~4 of the ~14 instances of the defect the plan was *auditing* were committed **by me, in the
document about that defect, while my entire attention was on not committing it.** If diligence were the
answer, the person hunting the defect would have been immune. **The failure rate under maximum attention
was indistinguishable from the baseline.**

**Traces to the goal.** *Structure beats Willpower* says do not rely on an agent remembering an
instruction. This is its converse and its sharper edge: **when you find yourself relying on care,
that reliance is itself the defect to remove.** A self-evolving agent accumulates artifacts — plans,
registries, manifests, counts — and every duplicated fact inside them is a future contradiction with a
scheduled arrival time. An agent that answers recurrence with more care will keep the defect and lose
the time.

**Applied through.** ENFORCEMENT FIRST, stated honestly: **not enforced today.** Landable surfaces:
1. **A recurrence trigger in review** — when a reviewer names the *same class* of finding twice against
   one artifact, the correct next move is deduplication, not another patch. Cheapest real step.
2. **A duplication lint on plan/registry artifacts** — a count or list appearing in two locations is a
   signal, never a block.
3. **The `/spec-converge` question**: *"does this design require two things to be kept in agreement?
   If so, why can there not be one?"*

**The honest test:** if this is ratified and the next recurring defect is met with another careful
patch, it is prose.

---

## What I am NOT claiming

- **Not that care is worthless.** Care caught plenty this window. It is insufficient *against
  recurrence*, which is a narrower and testable claim.
- **Not that all duplication is defect.** Some is deliberate redundancy with independent sources. The
  rule binds duplication that must be *manually* held in agreement.
- **Not that two instances generalise.** One document, six rounds, one agent. A reviewer may argue this
  is *Structure beats Willpower* restated rather than a distinct standard — and that is the strongest
  objection to it.
