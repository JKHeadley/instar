PROPOSED STANDARD UNDER EXAMINATION:
=== When you send something to check your work, give it the question and withhold your answer ===
If you tell a checker what you expect it to find, it will often hand your own assumption back to you — now carrying the authority of execution ('I did not merely reason about this, I ran it').
Earned three times independently: (a) a dispatched check was given an expectation in its instructions, compared reality against that expectation, and reported a defect that did not exist — the verdict was the dispatcher's own error returned as an execution result; (b) a measurement was sent to a colleague with a guess about its cause bundled into the same message, and the guess was wrong; (c) inverted usefully — a pointer's location was deliberately WITHHELD from a restarted session so the pointer mechanism would be tested rather than bypassed, and it failed the test.
The proposed discipline: a dispatch supplies the QUESTION and withholds the ANSWER. This applies to instruments as well as to delegated work.
