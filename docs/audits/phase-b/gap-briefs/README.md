# GAPS briefs — scoped for parallel drafting (2026-08-07, window 9)

The external family reviews returned five finding classes. Placement, redundancy, overreach and
coherence are closed on the branch. **What remains is GAPS: obligations the families clearly imply
and no article states.** Those are the ones that need writing rather than repairing.

## The split, and the constraint that shaped it

Four gaps, genuinely disjoint by SUBJECT — different families, different articles, different
evidence. But every one of them ultimately edits **the same file**, the registry. Parallel hands
drafting registry edits would collide on one file, which is the thing never to do.

**So the split is by ARTEFACT, not by branch.** Each brief produces a STANDALONE PROPOSAL DOCUMENT.
Nothing in this directory touches `docs/STANDARDS-REGISTRY.md`, the branch, or any guard. Echo
integrates the accepted drafts into the registry serially, one hand on the branch.

| brief | family | the gap, in the reviewer's words |
|---|---|---|
| `gap-1-threshold-of-importance.md` | The Substrate | no article defines the "threshold of importance" |
| `gap-2-user-facing-identification.md` | Building | no authoritative mechanism identifies a user-facing feature |
| `gap-3-who-validates-and-when.md` | Shipping | requires evidence but does not state who validates it or when |
| `gap-4-deferral-lifecycle.md` | Shipping | no obligation to close, expire, or escalate a tracked deferral |

## What a draft must contain, and what it must NOT

**Must:** the obligation in one sentence; why the family implies it; what a real guard would MEASURE
and what it would CERTIFY (the ratified measure-vs-certify clause); at least one input that would
pass the proposed guard while failing the claim; and an honest statement if no guard is currently
buildable — in which case it is a `documented-only` article and needs a countdown date under
ruling 4, not a pretence of enforcement.

**Must not:** invent enforcement that does not exist, or soften the obligation until it is trivially
satisfiable. A gap honestly labelled beats a rule quietly narrowed — that is the whole lesson of the
overreach findings these same reviews produced.

## Standing conditions on anything that reaches the registry

Tree placement (a declared, bidirectional parent), an attached case study, and — because the
per-family enforcement floor trips on the FIRST unguarded addition — a real guard or an explicit
countdown. **The Substrate family currently sits at EXACTLY its floor**, so a new unguarded article
there fails the build immediately.
