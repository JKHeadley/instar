# Source-Bound Holdout Candidates

Eight authored questions are frozen locally with their reference answers and nine original topic-message snapshots. Every answer cites exact supporting messages, including a human instruction or decision. The local files retain full message text and hashes. Only this custody manifest is committed; the questions, answers and captured conversation stay on the originating machine.

The author has seen the questions and answers. They are **prospective holdout candidates**, not an unseen qualification set by assertion. They have not been shown to a future answering model, used to tune a reduction, or scored through any model. The author must not later act as the independent judge or answering-model session. File permissions restrict other local users but do not isolate processes running under the same account; access discipline alone is not a blinded execution boundary.

## What this pack can establish

The questions concern recorded project decisions, not generic relationship arithmetic. Their answers must be justified from original messages rather than reconstructed from a summary. Six deterministic integrity checks verify matching unique identifiers, human-source provenance, verbatim supporting text, hashes, local permissions and absence of trial or qualification claims. These checks do not judge the interpretation of the instructions.

All eight questions come from one conversation. They are one correlated cluster, not eight independent samples, and they do not cover scheduled-job outcomes or open promises. Some may be answerable without context; those cases must remain in the results but cannot count as retained-context evidence. This pack supplements recorded work; it does not replay the exact model inputs from the original decisions and cannot replace that evidence.

The questions were authored **after** the existing job-startup and folding reductions. Consequently they cannot satisfy the governing requirement that qualification checks predate those reductions. They can be exploratory for those implementations, or prospective for a later reduction if the timing and exposure requirements are met. There is no claim that the current implementation is qualified.

## Custody and later use

The private directory is recorded in `manifest.json`. It contains separate sources, questions, labels, an exposure record and integrity results. The manifest hashes freeze the exact files without publishing their content. Do not copy the private files into a public branch, ordinary progress message or reduction-design prompt. A changed case requires a new pack version, with the reason and exposure history preserved; do not replace this pack silently.

Before any trial, an authorized independent reviewer must check the source authority, chronology, each required answer and any ambiguity. Preserve disputed cases and record the disagreement. The reduced-context designer may receive original source material but must not see these questions or labels. Predeclare applicable scope, critical errors, uncertainty method, acceptable bounds, stopping criteria, costs and the identities of the answer and judge models before scoring. Mandatory safety and identity remain exact in every arm; applicable standing decisions must also remain exact in the reduced arm.

A trial route must enforce separation of answers from labels and prove confinement before running. The current offline relationship runner accepts only stale-contact decisions, so it cannot score this richer rubric. Adding a generic source-grounded answer schema and an independently reviewed judge route is separate implementation work. Do not translate these questions into relationship fields and call them tested.

Run paired no-context, reduced and full-context trials with full-context repeats through the same verified reader. Preserve failures, uncertainty and exposure records. Unknown source authority or missing context must lead to an explicit limited answer rather than an invented project decision. Authored cases that no-context solves equally reliably are excluded from evidence of retention. No model calls, activation or measured savings are claimed here.
