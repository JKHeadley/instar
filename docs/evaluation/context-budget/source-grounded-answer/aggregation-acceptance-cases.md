# Offline Judge Aggregation Acceptance Cases

These synthetic cases specify the expected outputs of a future offline aggregator. Use frozen mock criteria, captured answer bytes, mock judge results and mock adjudication receipts. No private question, answer, reference label or live judge is required. The cases extend the [judging protocol](judging-protocol.md#validation-and-aggregation).

Use three criteria: critical criterion C, noncritical criterion N and noncritical criterion M. Assume two scheduled judge families, A and B, and a separately authorized independent adjudicator. Configure one retry for a timed-out judge slot within that family's original blind review. A valid retry can fill that slot under this fixture's frozen policy. Keep agreement, initial critical signals, judge errors, adjudication and candidate eligibility as separate recorded facts.

| Case | Fixture | Expected output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agreement and disagreement | Both judges pass C and fail N. A passes M and B marks M disputed. | C passes, N fails and M is disputed. Retain each finding with its evidence and reason. M contributes no qualification success. |
| Critical disagreement | A fails C and B passes C. Repeat with the families reversed and with B marking C disputed. | C is disputed and the initial critical-failure signal identifies each valid critical fail. The unresolved signal blocks qualification even if all other criteria pass. Processing order does not change the result. |
| Critical agreement | Both families fail C. | C fails. Preserve both critical findings. Candidate qualification is blocked. |
| Failed judge and surviving critical signal | A validly fails C. B times out and its permitted retry still produces no valid result. | Preserve A's initial critical-failure signal and both recorded B errors. The independent pair is incomplete, and B contributes no verdict. Qualification stays blocked. |
| Bounded retry | A passes C. B times out, then validly passes C on its permitted retry. | Keep the first error, both B attempt records and attributable costs. The successful retry fills B's original slot. C passes without any extra vote. |
| Invalid output | B submits a plausible fail on C inside a payload missing another scheduled criterion. A validly passes C. | Record B's output and judge error. An invalid payload contributes no findings to aggregation, and the pair is incomplete. |
| Independence breach | A and B agree but host records show a shared family, or show B received A's findings before B's review was complete. | Preserve findings and record the independence violation. Report incomplete evidence. Do not qualify the candidate based on agreement. A self-asserted family or independence claim in judge output cannot override host provenance. |
| Invalid answer | The final answer is missing or fails validation, and a judge is unavailable. | Keep the failed answer trial and any actual judge errors. Judge availability cannot turn an invalid answer into an unscored or successful candidate trial. |
| Confirmed critical failure | An authorized independent receipt confirms A's critical finding against the frozen evidence after an initial disagreement. | Keep the initial dispute and critical signal. Append the reasoned confirmation and record qualification blocked. |
| Judge-error resolution | An authorized independent receipt identifies a specific judge error in the initial critical finding. Repeat with a missing receipt or with a receipt from an unauthorized or nonindependent actor. | Only a resolution accepted under the frozen policy can be considered for eligibility. Keep the original critical signal and findings visible in both cases. Append the accepted receipt and compute eligibility separately under the governing policy. A missing or rejected receipt leaves the signal unresolved and qualification blocked. |
| Reference defect | An independent receipt finds that C rests on a defective frozen reference. A proposed correction asks to rerun only the preferred arm. | Keep the original findings, signal and receipt. Block affected cases until the corrected reference is versioned and the frozen rerun policy is applied across affected arms. Reject selective regrading of the preferred answer. |
| Reporting totals | A report includes a disputed case, a failed answer trial, a judge error and a case equally reliable without the evaluated context. Two trials come from the same conversation. | Preserve all cases in the exploratory report. Show counts and denominators for each reported metric, with reasons for qualification and retention exclusions. The dispute is no qualification success, the failed answer remains a failure and the equally reliable control contributes no retained-context evidence. The two same-conversation trials remain one correlated cluster. |

For adjudication and retry cases, compare the record before and after the later event. Original outputs, attempt records, findings, evidence references and critical signals must remain retrievable and unchanged. Each appended receipt must identify the affected original record and frozen policy. Reordering the stored records must preserve the outcome when recorded chronology and judge provenance are unchanged.
