## The amendment

Rungs one and two — **EXISTS** (mechanism on disk) and **WIRED** (reachable on the topology actually
run) — are **unchanged**, and remain findings.

**Rung three is not one question but two.** A guard is a two-sided instrument: it must **ACT when it
should** AND **HOLD BACK when it should**. The original ladder asked only the first. So a top-rung
verdict certified half a guard while reading as certifying all of it — *a passing condition narrower
than what it certifies*, the defect this phase found repeatedly across verification, alerting,
classification, and reporting surfaces.

Rung three therefore resolves on a 2×2, and **only `aligned` is aligned**:

| | holds back when it should | does not hold back |
|---|---|---|
| **acts when it should** | `aligned` | `over-blocks` — effective AND harmful |
| **does not act** | `inert` (the former "not effective") | `broken-both-ways` |

### The B-case supplies the second axis

An A-case alone — *a deliberately introduced violation is caught* — can only ever produce the **top
row**. It cannot distinguish `aligned` from `over-blocks`, because it never presents an input the guard
was supposed to let through.

**Therefore: a rung-three verdict claimed from an A-case alone is `acts-only` — unmeasured on the
second axis, not passing it.** Recording it as `aligned` is the amendment's own defect committed inside
the amendment. The B-case (honest inputs the guard must pass) has been mandatory in practice since
Phase A adopted it mid-audit; this change makes it **the thing that decides the verdict** rather than an
informal quality bar.

## What this amendment does NOT do

- **It does not deprecate any existing rung-three verdict.** Verdicts already recorded under the
  unamended ladder are `acts-only` where no B-case was run — a re-labelling, not a retraction. Nothing
  previously found is thereby wrong; it is thereby **narrower than it read**.
- **It does not make the second axis enforced.** Recording it in the contract does not make anything
  measure it. The honest test: if the next guard verdict in this tree is claimed as `aligned` on an
  A-case alone, this amendment is prose. (An instance of the standard it rides on — *Verify the State,
  Not Its Symbol*, as amended to bind a check's account of **itself**.)
- **It does not claim the 2×2 is complete.** `over-blocks` is measured; `broken-both-ways` has no
  observed instance in this phase. A reviewer may reasonably argue the fourth cell is theoretical.
