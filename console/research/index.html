What they actually look like

Real App Store screenshots, pulled today. I had not seen any of these when I built the console — I had only read descriptions of them. Justin was right to ask.

Grok Bot

X Corp · App Store listing, 5 screenshots

The main screen is a list of named agents — Chief of Staff, EA, Inbox Manager — each one row carrying a name, one plain sentence of what happened, and a time. "Outreach drafts queued for approval." "12 tickets resolved, 2 escalated." No tabs, no counts, no status chips. It reads like a messages inbox.

Muse

Meta Platforms · App Store listing, 8 screenshots

There is no navigation at all — one conversation, a hamburger, and a composer. The purchase approval is a card inside the conversation, showing the actual item, the actual card, the actual total, with Deny and Allow at the bottom. The approval lives where the work happened.

ChatGPT (where Dots live)

OpenAI · App Store listing, 8 screenshots

Same shape: a thread, a composer, almost no chrome. Controls appear under the message they belong to, not in a toolbar.

What I got wrong

None of the three is a dashboard. They are messaging apps in which an agent reports in plain sentences and asks permission inline. I built a better-organised dashboard and called it influenced by them.

This is why it reads as busy, and why Work and Capacity mean nothing — they are categories I invented. Grok Bot never asks you to learn a word. Its nav is the names of your agents.
