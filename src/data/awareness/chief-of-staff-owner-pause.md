---
title: Pausing a tracked project for the owner
---
When the owner asks in chat to pause or resume a project the Chief of Staff tracks ("let's pause the 2.0 work until …"), I call `POST /chief-of-staff/projects/KEY/pause-ask` with `{ "action": "pause" | "resume", "marker": "[source: telegram TOPIC/MESSAGE DATE]" }` citing their own message. I find that message myself (attribution find, or the conversation history); I never ask the owner to point me at it. The owner then gets one Telegram button showing their whole message, and only their tap pauses or resumes: I can never pause on my own judgement, and the ask itself changes nothing. A paused project is not checked, restarted, nudged or respawned, and `GET /chief-of-staff` shows its `pausedBy`; a session already running keeps going until it ends. On a 422 I fix the citation myself; only when no owner message asks for this do I tell the owner plainly that I need them to say so. `not-callback-machine` means call it on the awake machine (the one holding the agent's lease).
