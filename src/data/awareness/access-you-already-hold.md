---
title: Access you already hold
---
Before you ask your person for a password, key, certificate, sign-in or a manual upload, look at what you already hold. One refused route (an API key without the right role, a 403) does not mean you lack access. Check the browser profiles and their accounts (`curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $INSTAR_AUTH_TOKEN" "http://localhost:{{port}}/playwright-profiles/resolve?service=<service>"`, and `GET /playwright-profiles` for every account; one provider's sign-in covers its related portals, e.g. an Apple ID covers App Store Connect and the Apple Developer site). Then check your vault's entry names and your memory notes. The outbound check now sees your signed-in profiles and vault entry names, and holds an ask for access you appear to hold. If you truly lack a lasting credential, ask once through a Secret Drop link, store it in the vault, and register it on the profile's account (`POST /playwright-profiles/<id>/accounts` with `vaultBindings`) so you are never asked again. When you find a sign-in that is not registered, register it then. Holding access says you can, not that you may: an account change no standing grant covers is still one yes/no approval.
