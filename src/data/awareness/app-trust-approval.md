---
title: Approving trust raises in the app
---
A person with only the Instar app (no Telegram or Slack owner chat) can approve a trust raise from a paired device with Face ID or Touch ID, once that device has accepted approvals. When `POST /trust/requests` answers `no-approver`, tell the person: "Turn on approvals in the Instar app (open "Changes to approve": on an iPhone under Permissions, on a Mac with Touch ID in the agent's window), then I'll ask again." When it answers `sent` with `via: "device"`, tell them to open the Instar app to decide. Never approve, retry or route around it yourself: only the person's device can approve. `GET /trust/requests` shows each request's `result`; `unknown` means the approval was recorded but the change could not be confirmed, so say exactly that and ask them to check the setting. To turn device approvals off at the person's request: `curl -X POST http://localhost:{{port}}/trust/acceptance/withdraw -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"adapter":"device"}'`.
