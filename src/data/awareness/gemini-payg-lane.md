---
title: Gemini pay-per-use lane (Neon and Hydrogen)
---
Instar has one metered door to Google's Gemini API, used for two things only: Gemini 4 Neon as an independent reviewer in spec reviews, and a second opinion any of your sessions can ask for (Hydrogen by default, Neon on request). It is off until your operator approves it once with their PIN, and it spends only on the one machine designated for it, under a monthly cap with a daily guard. Ask for a second opinion with `curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $INSTAR_AUTH_TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -H "X-Instar-Session: $INSTAR_SESSION_NAME" http://localhost:{{port}}/intelligence/gemini -d '{"prompt":"…","purpose":"why you are asking","model":"hydrogen"}'` (from any machine; it is forwarded to the spender). A refusal comes back with a plain reason (limit reached, paused, not switched on); pass that on as it is, and never retry around a limit. Read spend with `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $INSTAR_AUTH_TOKEN" http://localhost:{{port}}/routing-spend/caps` and answer in dollars from that, never from memory. Stopping or lowering spending is yours to do at once, from any machine, with no PIN (it reaches the spending machine): freeze the key `gemini_api_key_payg` (`POST /routing-spend/freeze`), lower a limit (`POST /routing-spend/caps/lower` with e.g. `{"dailyCapUsd":5}`; only values at or below the current one are accepted), turn a caller off (`POST /routing-spend/callers/disable` with `{"caller":"second-opinion"}`) or lower the second-opinion share (`POST /routing-spend/share/lower`). Say "stopped" only when the answer says `confirmed: true`; a 202 means the spending machine has not confirmed yet. Raising a limit, unfreezing or adding a caller is always your operator's PIN: render a plan on the spending machine and send one card. Going live is one PIN on the `gemini-payg-setup` plan, rendered on the spending machine after the account lane's key handoff; the door then waits in `armed-pending-proof` until `POST /routing-spend/release-proof` (from a non-spending machine on a multi-machine agent) passes the release checks, which `GET /routing-spend/gemini-payg/release` lists. Sessions cannot read the key (the vault reader refuses it), and a conversation cannot run on Gemini in this version: if someone asks to pin a conversation to Gemini, tell them plainly that Neon and Hydrogen are available as reviews and second opinions, not as a conversation's engine.
