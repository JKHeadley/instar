---
title: Project apps one tap from a project
---
A project can have apps: pages one tap from its row in Work and from a strip at the top of its conversation, with a pinned link in its Telegram topic or Slack channel. For now the apps are Instar's own pages; the first is the Feature maturation board. When a project would clearly be easier to follow as a page (I keep sending it the same shaped status, it tracks counts or stages that change, or the owner keeps asking "where are we?"), I may suggest one app once: `curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' http://localhost:{{port}}/work/projects/KEY/app-offers -d '{"appRef":"builtin:maturation-board","why":"one line on why"}'` (KEY is the project's conversation key, URL-encoded, e.g. `telegram%3A128732`). Then I post one message in the project's conversation pointing at its Work row. Only the owner attaches, declines or removes an app, with a tap on the Work row; I cannot, and a refused second offer (`already-offered`, `owner-declined`) means I do not ask again. For a project with an app, my status messages say what changed and give the link instead of repeating the numbers. Before saying what app a project has, I read `GET /work/projects` (each row's `apps`, `offers` and `answers`).

Custom apps (where `projectApps.custom.enabled` is on): when no Instar page fits, I can write the project its own app. First I take its spec through convergence, then create it with `curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' http://localhost:{{port}}/project-apps -d '{"conversationKey":"telegram:128732","title":"Sales pipeline","specPath":"docs/specs/sales-app.md"}'` (an unconverged spec is refused `app-spec-not-converged`), and write its page with `curl -X PUT -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' http://localhost:{{port}}/project-apps/APP_ID/source -d '{"html":"<!doctype html>...","note":"what changed, in plain words"}'`. Each write opens one Work Board card that the owner approves on their phone; nothing runs before that, my token cannot approve it, and a console PIN tap is refused. On the console the card says "Approve this on your phone" and offers only "Stop this app". `GET /project-apps` says what each app serves (`served`, `none`, `copying`, `disabled`). The app runs in a sealed frame opened at `/dashboard/project-app/?app=APP_ID`. It gets no dashboard access and no owner keys, only the narrow key the wrapper hands it by message. With that key it talks to exactly two paths, `/project-apps/APP_ID/api/data` and `/project-apps/APP_ID/api/summary`, sending the key as `X-Project-App-Key`. A worked example is `examples/project-apps/sales-pipeline.html`. For now app keys stay on the lease holder, so another machine shows the app as still copying.
