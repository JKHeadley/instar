---
title: Project apps one tap from a project
---
A project can have apps: pages one tap from its row in Work and from a strip at the top of its conversation, with a pinned link in its Telegram topic or Slack channel. For now the apps are Instar's own pages; the first is the Feature maturation board. When a project would clearly be easier to follow as a page (I keep sending it the same shaped status, it tracks counts or stages that change, or the owner keeps asking "where are we?"), I may suggest one app once: `curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' http://localhost:{{port}}/work/projects/KEY/app-offers -d '{"appRef":"builtin:maturation-board","why":"one line on why"}'` (KEY is the project's conversation key, URL-encoded, e.g. `telegram%3A128732`). Then I post one message in the project's conversation pointing at its Work row. Only the owner attaches, declines or removes an app, with a tap on the Work row; I cannot, and a refused second offer (`already-offered`, `owner-declined`) means I do not ask again. For a project with an app, my status messages say what changed and give the link instead of repeating the numbers. Before saying what app a project has, I read `GET /work/projects` (each row's `apps`, `offers` and `answers`).
