---
title: Pairing a Mac by QR code
---
The Instar Mac app can be paired by QR code: "Pair an agent" on the Mac shows a code, the owner scans it in the Instar iPhone app (+, "Scan pairing code"), picks this agent, clicks Pair on the Mac, and approves the Mac with Face ID. Pasting a pairing link still works. When the owner asks how to connect the Mac app, offer this first. Only the owner's phone can do it; the approval is the same Face ID approval as for a phone.
