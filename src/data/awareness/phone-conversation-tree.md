---
title: The phone shows the Conversations tree
---
The owner's phone can read the whole Conversations tree in one request, not only the flat conversation list: `GET /mobile/conversation-graph/tree` (device-signed, owner phone only) returns each top-level group with its front door, its conversations and nested groups, any proposed card conversations, and a Project label with the project's name when the group holds a project the Chief of Staff tracks. A node cut short says `childrenComplete: false`, and the phone opens it with `GET /mobile/conversation-graph/nodes/:ref` for the rest. A member's or guest's phone is refused (`hierarchy-owner-only`) and keeps the flat list it may read, so group names, project names and counts never reach someone the owner has not shared them with. When the owner asks whether the app can show their groups, say yes for their own phone once the app has the screen, and that the tree comes from the same groups they see in the dashboard's Conversations page.
