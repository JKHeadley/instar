---
title: Card owners across the team, and cards blocked by cards
---
A work card is carried by me or by any person on the team (the owner, or a member the owner added), and a card can be blocked by another card. When my work on a card needs something only a person can give, I do not ask in chat and I do not stall: from the session doing the work I call `POST /work-board/cards/<id>/asks {"askKey":"<short stable key>","title":...,"need":...,"carrier":"owner" | {"personKey":...},"recommendation":...,"whyYou":...,"doneWhen":[...]}`, which creates the ask card for that person AND the blocked-by link on my card in one step (a retry with the same `askKey` finds the same ask; a different body answers `409 ask-exists`). My card then reads `blocked` ("Blocked by: …") and is not worked until the ask closes; the ask shows "Blocks: …" and sits in that person's own Waiting on you, with nobody else notified. When they close it (done, or "Not important" with a reason) the board writes the `unblocked` record itself and hands my card back to me with a brief that quotes their close-out; I read it before carrying on. A card may be blocked by any card I may read: `POST /work-board/cards/<id>/records {"kind":"blocked-by","blockerCardId":...,"note":...}` on the BLOCKED card, cleared with `{"kind":"unblocked","blockedByRecordId":...}` (reason `cleared` only; the other reasons are the board's). A self-link or a loop is refused `blocker-cycle`; a card may hold at most `maxOpenAsksPerCard` open asks (`429 too-many-open-asks`), and past `softOpenBlockers` the response says "a chain this deep is usually a project". Reopening a finished blocker re-blocks nothing: I am told once and write a new link if I need one. Passing: the owner or the card's current carrier passes a card to any team member or to me (`{"kind":"edited","carrier":...}`, or the phone's `pass`); I may pass only a card I carry, only to a person, and only with `recommendation` and `whyYou`, because that makes it an ask; I never set `carrier: "agent"` myself (`403 agent-cannot-take-card`), a person hands a card to me. A pass to someone who cannot see the card is refused `new-carrier-cannot-see-card` unless the owner makes it. A member who leaves the team leaves their cards in the owner's Waiting on you with a Pass control ("… is no longer on the team; this card needs a new owner"); re-adding them hands nothing back. A card a member passed to me still needs the owner's Approve under the strict policy. Blocker titles, close notes and `refs` are resolved under each viewer's own access: a person who may not read the other card sees "a card you cannot see", never its title or id.
