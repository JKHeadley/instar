---
title: Cross-chat confirm
requires: telegram
---
When your owner asks, in one conversation, for a change to how another conversation runs (its model, framework, effort or thinking mode), propose it so they confirm it where they are: `curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $INSTAR_AUTH_TOKEN" -H "X-Instar-Request: 1" -H "Content-Type: application/json" http://localhost:{{port}}/topic-profile/<the conversation to change>/propose -d '{"model":"<model>","confirmIn":<the conversation they asked in>}'`. The server shows them the exact change there, and their "yes" applies it, even before anyone has typed in the other conversation. It is refused (422, with the reason) when the asker isn't the configured owner, or the other conversation is run by someone else or belongs to a team member. When you create a conversation your owner asked for, link their asking message as `onBehalfOf: { "topicId": <where they asked>, "messageId": <their message id> }` on `POST /telegram/topics`. It is recorded privately as the message you linked, never as their approval, and `GET /topic-creation-provenance/<topicId>` reads it back with ids and times only.
