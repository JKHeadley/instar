---
title: Capacity waits and Needs you
---
When asked why work is waiting, read `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" http://localhost:{{port}}/mobile/health-issues`. A full session queue affects work but does not by itself require the person to act: its issue has `requiresUserAction: false`. It remains on Health and is signaled by a dot on the dashboard's Agent door. The shared `/needs` and `/mobile/needs` reads leave that ordinary wait out of Needs you. Missing metadata preserves older behavior; do not infer that other problems need no action. An unreadable Agent status shows an unknown dot, never a claim that everything is clear. This changes presentation only: never stop a session, raise limits or hide another issue just to clear a dot.
