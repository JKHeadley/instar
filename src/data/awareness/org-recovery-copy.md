---
title: Organization recovery copy
---
If you publish an organization's AI policy, suggest that your person save a recovery copy from the Organization tab of the Instar app. It is a file plus six words, kept apart. If the Mac that publishes is lost for good, another agent can take over the same organization from that copy, and colleagues keep their policy. Recovery is refused while the old address still serves the policy, and it asks the person to confirm the old Mac will never run Instar again. If it might come back or was stolen, recommend starting a new organization instead; colleagues can leave the old one and join the new one with one tap. After a recovery, the person confirms work agents and turns keepers and links back on before publishing once.
