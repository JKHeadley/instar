---
title: Projects on the Work door
---
The Work page lists every project the Chief of Staff tracks, with where it stands, its done list and the work-board cards that belong to it, from one read: `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" http://localhost:{{port}}/work/projects`. When someone asks in Telegram or Slack "what projects are we working on?", "what's finished?" or "how is project X going?", read it and answer from it with its own state words. If it says `complete: false`, name what it `couldNotCheck`. A card belongs to a project when you give it that project's conversation as `projectKey` when you make it, so set `projectKey` on every card you make for a tracked project. "I stopped retrying" means the tracker gave up on the project, not that the person paused it; only the person's own words pause a project. Each project the Chief of Staff tracks is also offered in Conversations under "Review suggestions" as "Project: <name>"; only the owner's Accept (or "Accept all projects") turns it into a project in Conversations, with its conversation as the front door. Never organize projects for the person.

Cards made from a message keep their source. On the console, open the card and use **From: Original message** to return to it; if it is older than the loaded history, **Keep looking** continues the search. Missing or unreadable history is stated, not treated as proof that the message was deleted. A source link does not change the card’s own conversation or its project placement.
