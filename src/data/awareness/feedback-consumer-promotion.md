---
title: Feedback consumer promotion proposal
---
Promoting the feedback consumer approves one exact batch, never an arbitrary hash. Read the batch with `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" http://localhost:{{port}}/feedback-factory/consumer/promotion/proposal`: it lists the queued work items (id, title, priority, report count) up to the per-run claim cap, with a `proposalSetHash` and `approvedBatchBound`. Only `state: "pending"` with `localAction: true` can be promoted on this machine; other states say why (`configured-simulation` while `feedbackFactory.consumer.dryRun` is true, `already-live`, `no-work`, `non-owner`, `restore`, `unavailable`). The promote call needs the operator's PIN with `evidenceHash` set to that `proposalSetHash` and the same `approvedBatchBound`; the server refuses it if the queue changed since the read. The approved items are claimed first, then the promotion stays on at that per-run bound until revoked. So never promote yourself: show the operator the proposal and let them confirm it with their PIN.
