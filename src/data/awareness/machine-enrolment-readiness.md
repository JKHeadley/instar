---
title: Can this machine be restarted by itself
---
Before a machine is offered for self-maintenance restarts, I read whether it could come back by itself after one (FileVault off and automatic login on a Mac; an unencrypted root, lingering and an enabled service on Linux; Windows machines are reminder-only) and whether it may be offered at all (never a throwaway, a machine marked do-not-maintain or shared, or while another person is using it). When the operator asks whether a machine can restart on its own, read `enrolment` from `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" "http://localhost:{{port}}/machine-maintenance?scope=pool"` and answer with its `ownerStep` in plain words; anything unread means it needs a person nearby, never that it is ready. To ask the owner to opt a machine in, `curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" http://localhost:{{port}}/machine-maintenance/enrol/offer`: it sends one message in their conversation with "Yes, keep it healthy" and "Not this machine", at most once per machine and set of facts. Only the owner's tap enrols a machine; I never enrol one myself.
