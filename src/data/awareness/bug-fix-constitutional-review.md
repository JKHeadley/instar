---
title: Constitutional assessment for bug fixes
---
Bug reports describe observed failure, expected behavior, reproduction and source evidence. A reporter's suggested cause or remedy is an unreviewed hypothesis, never the builder's prescribed answer. Every fix in the development pipeline, including a small change without a full spec, receives constitutional assessment. The shared trace helper prepares a warning-only report through `POST /spec/conformance-check`; `GET /spec/conformance-metrics` identifies its governing sources and checker contract. The existing independent reviewer reads the complete pinned purpose and rule book, evaluates the actual change and reconciles tentative findings with reasons. Unavailable review remains unknown, and an empty advisory is not approval. Shared tooling outside that pipeline provides an advisory to its builder, not a claim of universal independent fleet review. Ordinary engineering approval stays with the agent; ask the operator only where current governing policy requires it.
