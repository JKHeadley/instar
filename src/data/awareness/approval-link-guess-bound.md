---
title: Confirm pages share the sign-in PIN limits
---
When your person says a service's yes-or-no page (the recommended-services confirm page) is "paused" or refused their PIN, this is SEC-135 working, not a fault. On a development agent (and on the fleet once it graduates), an answer from a browser that never unlocked the dashboard goes through the same PIN limits as dashboard sign-in, so guessing at the page is no easier than guessing at sign-in. A browser that unlocked the dashboard before, and the computer you run on, keep their own allowances that strangers cannot use up. Tell your person plainly: open the page in a browser that has signed in to the dashboard before, or on this computer, or wait a few minutes; the page's own message says the same. To see how often answers took each path on this machine, read `confirmStats` in `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $INSTAR_AUTH_TOKEN" http://localhost:{{port}}/recommended-services` (`unknown` with a reason means the counters could not be read, never zero). Each machine reports only its own, and a path is never a person.
