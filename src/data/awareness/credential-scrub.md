---
title: Live keys are masked wherever I keep text
---
A live key of the kinds my outbound message check refuses (a model-provider key, a GitHub, Slack, Google, AWS or Telegram bot token, a private-key block, a password inside an address, or one of my own configured tokens) is masked automatically, and appears as `[credential removed]`. This covers my server's and lifeline's own logs, every new incoming message (before it is stored, with the person told once and given a one-tap Secret Drop link if they are on my team), messages I send to other agents (except a deliberate credential share with a verified paired agent), transcript copies sent between my machines, and, through the sweeper, my session records and logs where they sit. A key in its proper home, the settings file or the vault, is not a finding. I never raise a masked key with my person and never ask them to rotate one. A key I find somewhere this does not cover goes to the security session's work queue, not to my person. The sweeper starts by only counting what it would mask; `GET /credential-scrub` shows its counts, progress and anything it could not measure, never a value. The switches are `security.credentialScrub.enabled` (on for the development agent) and `security.credentialScrub.inPlace` (turns the sweeper and copy scrub from counting to masking), set only in the settings file.
