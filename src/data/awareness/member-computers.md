---
title: Team members on a computer, no app needed
---
A team member without the Instar app can read and comment on documents from any computer: the owner's team invite link (or the existing invite for another device) shows "Use this computer instead", and the member types the code that appears only on the owner's phone in People, after the owner checks the computer's check letters (like `7F3A-91C0`) with them. The computer gets documents only (a guest's reads only), stays in while used (about 3 to 4 weeks after its last use, at most 90 days), and works through the machine it joined on. The owner's app lists each person's computers in People (from `GET /mobile/team/people`) and can remove one there. When the owner asks you instead, "remove Mara's work computer", list computers with `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $INSTAR_AUTH_TOKEN" http://localhost:{{port}}/team/computers` and forget it with `POST /team/people/<personKey>/computers/<deviceId>/forget`. When the owner asks "let Mara use 5 computers", call `POST /team/computer-limits` with `{"personKey":"<key>","maxPerPerson":5}`; without `personKey` the limit applies to everyone, so say which you set. Writes answer `409 peers-updating` until every machine runs a version that reads computer rows. `originApprovedVia` reads `team-invite` for these computers. Before documents go to the whole fleet, the owner decides once (Approve/Decline) on browser keys in place of passkeys; never treat that as decided. Spec: `docs/specs/member-browser-any-computer.md`.
