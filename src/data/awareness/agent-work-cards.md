---
title: Agent work cards from every request
---
Every request a person I serve makes becomes a card I carry on the Work Board, by itself, the moment I record it as a commitment: `POST /commitments` from an operator conversation writes the card (id in the response as `cardId`, visible in `GET /work-board` and under its project in `GET /work/projects`; a task with no project lands in General). So I record each request with `sourceMessageId` (the relay prefix carries it; a scoped session's conversation comes from its binding, a Bearer caller passes `sourceConversationKey` too), an `externalKey` so a retried record is one commitment, and when I can `milestones[]`, `outward` and, for outward work, `recommendation` and `whyYou`. A direct request from the person is never `/feedback`: `POST /feedback` now needs a `source` (`{"kind":"agent"}` for my own reports, `other-agent`, `user`) and refuses an operator conversation with `operator-request-not-feedback`, naming the card route. The team owner's own message (sender-verified from the durable row, never from a session binding) is the yes: that card starts at once under either board policy and is never asked about again; its `need` is the owner's words, locked, shown as "Asked: …" with its age, "recorded in the agent's words" when my `userRequest` differed, and "changed since asked" when I edited the title or items. A request from a member or an unverified sender stays subject to the existing start policy and cannot bypass its evidence, note or completion gates. A genuine person-facing approval appears as its own card in Needs you. Pure agent-internal work never contributes a Needs-you aggregate, Work badge or default owner conversation, even when it is held or not started. Open `GET /work-board?view=agent` (or `/mobile/work-board?view=agent`) to inspect only internal cards; default reads project owner-visible work before counts and page cuts. Use the explicit `agentInternal` and `conversationAvailable` fields; do not infer origin from a title or `agentWork`. Internal history is created when execution actually needs it, and retained history stays outside owner discovery. An agent-work card (inward, carried by me, no owner-confirms item) needs no `recommendation` or `whyYou`; a card that asks the person (outward, a person carrier, an owner-confirms item, a `waiting-on` a person) still needs both; `outward` now defaults to `false` for my cards, so I set `outward: true` whenever the work sends anything outside the team or spends money, and when unsure. I keep progress on the card: `POST /work-board/cards/<id>/notes {"text":...}` from a Bearer session or from the Telegram topic session that recorded the request (the note lands in the card's conversation at once; `503 rate-limited` means nothing was written, write it again), substantive progress as `evidence` per item, then `claimed-done`; delivering the commitment (`POST /commitments/<id>/deliver`) writes a note and, only with `itemId` and `evidence`, the evidence. A gate-written card is held for the conversation that recorded it (no second session is started while that conversation is live); it is handed to a card session only after the hold lifts. A quiet internal card stays with the agent, including after its automatic re-pickup; progress alone never becomes an owner decision. Put the person's judgement last and only where it is real (`certify: owner-confirms`), never as a stand-in for a check I can run.
