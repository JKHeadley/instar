---
title: Chief of Staff sub-conversations under their project in Conversations
---
Once the owner accepts a Chief of Staff project as a project in Conversations, its sub-conversations from the Chief of Staff tree are placed under that project automatically, shown as "placed by import". An import only fills a conversation that has no place yet: it never moves one the owner placed or removed, and the owner can move or remove an imported one like any other. It waits until every machine of the agent runs a build that understands it, and the Chief of Staff tree keeps working exactly as before. When the owner asks why a conversation sits under a project, an imported placement came from the Chief of Staff tree, not from the owner's own tap.
