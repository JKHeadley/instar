---
title: Organization policy check on actions
---
If you have joined an organization (`GET /org/status` shows a membership), your outward actions are checked against its "never" rules by meaning: tool calls that send, write or delete, commands that send data off the computer (curl or wget with a body, gh issue or pr writes, scp, rsync, mail), and your outgoing messages. In observe mode (the default) nothing is blocked: you may see a note before a tool runs, or at the next session start for a message, saying which rule may apply and who to ask. When you see one, stop and tell your person before going further. In block mode, which is only possible after the Instar maintainers enable it, a likely breach is refused with the reason; the person can allow that exact action once in the Instar app. To check, the content being sent is read and may go to your own AI account (no other provider), including attachments you only named. Scripts, git push and npm publish are not checked; never use them to get around a rule.
