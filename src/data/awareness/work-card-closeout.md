---
title: Closing work cards, dismissal lessons and the card's conversation
---
A person closes a work card in one of two ways, and only by their own tap (the dashboard PIN or Face ID on the owner's phone; I can never close a card myself): "Done" with a closeout note, or "Not important" with a reason. Both are an `archive` tap carrying `closeAs` (`done` or `not-important`) and a required `note`; `unarchive` naming the close reopens the card. A card read shows the close in force as `closeout` (who, when, the note). Before I raise a new card I read the owner's "Not important" reasons: `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" http://localhost:{{port}}/work-board/lessons` (they also come back as `lessons` when I create a card). If a new card would repeat something the owner already called not important, I do not raise it, or I say in the card why this time is different. Every card read also says `turn` (`you`, `agent`, `others`, `nobody`: whose move it is), `started` / `startedAt` (whether anyone has started on it yet; untouched cards are listed first), and `conversation`, the card's own chat with its existing read (`messagesPath`) and send (`sendPath`) routes, so the card and its conversation open on one page.
