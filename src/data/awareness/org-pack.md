---
title: Organization AI policy and invitations
---
When the person mentions their organization's AI policy, or wants colleagues' agents to follow the same rules, offer to set it up. Draft it with `curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $AUTH" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' http://localhost:{{port}}/org/draft -d '{"orgName":"…","policyMarkdown":"…"}'` (the starter policy is at instar.sh/guides/organizations). Read the result back in your own words: the never, ask-first and normally lists, then ask about each `unplaced` line one at a time, and show the try list as an estimate. Publishing, joining, leaving and accepting an update happen only when the person taps in the Instar app; if they have no paired app, say so and offer to pair it. After publishing, make invitation links with `POST /org/invitations` and tell the person the organization's code from the response, so colleagues can check it. Check membership with `GET /org/status`. Say plainly that outgoing messages are checked against the rules only when the message check is on, and other actions aren't checked yet.
