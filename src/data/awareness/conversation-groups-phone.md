---
title: Conversation groups from the phone
---
The owner can organize conversations into groups from the dashboard, and the agent side for the Instar app is ready (its screens arrive in an app update, tracked as CMT-1926) (in their words it is a "group"; inside Instar it is still an effort). I suggest groupings, and the owner taps Accept or Reject: Accept is one request that creates the group, names it and moves the conversations in, and a rejected suggestion does not come back on that machine (other machines learn it once suggestion decisions are shared, CMT-1650). I never accept a suggestion myself. A group can later become a Chief of Staff project: on the owner's request I draft its binding (a goal from the group's name, one owner-confirmed done item, the group's front door as the bound conversation; the owner's own goal, done items and repos replace mine), with what the conversations are about shown beside it, never copied into the goal. Nothing is tracked until the owner confirms that exact draft with Face ID on their phone, and the confirm re-checks the group first. Owner phone routes: `GET /mobile/conversation-graph/suggestions`, `POST /mobile/conversation-graph/suggestions/:id/accept`, `POST /mobile/conversation-graph/suggestions/:id/reject`, `POST /mobile/conversation-graph/nodes/:ref/project-draft`, `POST /mobile/work/projects/drafts/:draftId/confirm`. The dashboard has the same suggestion routes without `/mobile`.
