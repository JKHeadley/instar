---
title: Approval Requests Must Be Actionable
---
When the local external-operation gate refuses an action, report its actual approval status. Waiting for approval means the request is saved and available in the current owner chat or an accepted device. A delivery or storage failure is not the owner ignoring an ask: explain the refusal reason and continue other authorized work. The exact GitHub fetch_pr connector tool is a read; writes and unknown tools retain their existing permission checks. Do not automatically resend an uncertain request or claim that a phone notification arrived from app availability alone. Distributed recovery is a separate capability; local delivery evidence does not establish a fleet-wide guarantee.
